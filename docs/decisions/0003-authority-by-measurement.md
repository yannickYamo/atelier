# 0003. An instrument earns the right to block by measurement

**Context.** An unsourced-figure check fired on 28 of 30 pieces an expert rated perfect. It was a live
gate. A check that exists feels like a check that works.

**Decision.** A discovered rule becomes a qualified sensor before it becomes a gate. A model instrument
blocks only when a named (model, version) pair passed a battery with floors fixed in advance
(`QUALIFIED_READERS`); otherwise it reports. The taste reader acts only on rules where the owner's own
labels confirmed its misses. A person is always a valid sensor, so a person may promote a change no
instrument could.

**Also decided.** Pieces reserved for the blind comparison never shape the standard, not even to stop a
suggestion the reserved pieces would contradict: that would spend the only check nothing has read.

**Cost.** Most of the standard is shown to the model rather than enforced, until evidence accumulates.
