# Term Summary: Project Kestrel Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

*Status: For Review*

## 1. Parties

Harrowgate Tooling Limited, a private limited company registered in England and Wales under number 09914427 (**"Harrowgate"**), and Senn & Aubrecht Robotics GmbH, a limited liability company registered in Munich under number HRB 271905 (**"Senn"**).

## 2. Purpose

Harrowgate and Senn are exchanging technical and commercial information to evaluate a possible joint development of a robotic deburring cell (the **"Purpose"**).

## 3. Key Terms

| Term | Position | Term Sheet Ref |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Protected information | All non-public information either party discloses for the Purpose, in any form (**"Confidential Information"**) | TS cl. 2.1 |
| Start date | 1 March 2026 (the **"Effective Date"**) | TS cl. 1.1 |
| Duration | 24 months from the Effective Date | TS cl. 6.1 |
| Termination notice | 30 days in writing | TS cl. 6.2 |
| Survival | 36 months after expiry or termination | TS cl. 6.3 |
| Permitted recipients | Employees and professional advisers who need the information for the Purpose | TS cl. 3.2 |
| Return or destruction | Within 14 days of a written request | TS cl. 5.1 |
| Liability cap | GBP 250,000 per party in aggregate | TS cl. 7.1 |

## 4. Obligations

1. Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information secret and use it only for the Purpose.
2. Neither party shall disclose Confidential Information to anyone other than the permitted recipients in TS cl. 3.2.
3. Each party shall protect Confidential Information with at least the care it gives its own confidential material.
4. Each party shall return or destroy Confidential Information within 14 days of a written request.
5. Senn shall not send Harrowgate's tooling drawings outside the European Economic Area without Harrowgate's written consent (TS cl. 4.4).

## 5. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not cover information that:

- (a) is or becomes public other than through a breach (TS cl. 2.2(a));
- (b) the recipient lawfully held before disclosure (TS cl. 2.2(b));
- (c) the recipient lawfully receives from a third party free of restriction (TS cl. 2.2(c)).

## 6. Term and Termination

The arrangement starts on the Effective Date and runs for 24 months. Confidentiality duties survive for 36 months after expiry or termination. Either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice.

## 7. Governing Law

The laws of England and Wales govern the arrangement, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 8. Open Points

| Point | Term Sheet Says | Recommended Position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Liability cap on deliberate breach | "capped at GBP 250,000 per party in aggregate" (TS cl. 7.1) | Confirm whether the cap covers deliberate disclosure; a capped confidentiality breach is rare in this sector. |
| Independent development | Silent | Agree whether independently developed information is excluded, since both parties build deburring equipment. |
| Injunctive relief | Silent | Record whether either party may seek an injunction without proving loss. |
