# p6, refined and ratified — the ruling and what it still does not explain

**Ruled EDIT by the author, 2026-08-28.** The machine's proposal was rejected as an overcorrection
and replaced with the author's own wording.

## The three wordings

**Original, ratified from discovery:**

> I restate the thesis as a compressed aphorism at section boundaries, even after the point has
> already been argued in full.

**Machine's proposal, rejected:**

> After I've argued a point, I write one more sentence that says the same thing again in my own
> voice — as a judgment, a stance, or a general claim about how things work… what makes it mine is
> that it asserts something evaluative or law-like beyond the evidence just given.

Rejected because *"law-like"*, *"in my own voice"* and *"general claim about how things work"* are
not supported by the labels. m16 is a synthesis of preceding material rather than the author's own
law-like judgment; m23 is closer to an implication than an evaluation. **The YES class is broader
than the proposal.** Having correctly identified that the original named a realization as the
invariant, the proposal overcorrected into a different and equally unsupported invariant.

**RATIFIED — the author's wording:**

> After developing a point, I often add a sentence that re-lands what the preceding material
> means—the judgment, takeaway, or implication I want the reader to carry forward. It does not
> simply add another fact, example, or unrelated next step. That landing can be short and
> aphoristic, but it does not have to be.

The discriminant moved from **aphorism vs ordinary sentence** to **new evidence or forward motion vs
interpretive landing of what was just established.**

**They are deliberately not encoded.** Adding "except CTA packaging" and "except meta-description"
would fit two single instances, which is the same overfitting the machine's proposal was rejected
for. They are carried as declared unknowns into fresh validation, where the question is whether the
author reproduces those judgments on cases he has not seen.

## These 24 passages are now DEVELOPMENT DATA

They produced the refinement. Scoring the refined wording on them would report how well it was
fitted, and no amount of care afterwards can separate that from how well it observes.

Fresh validation requires new passages, **information symmetry** — the author judged partly from
memory of the full essay while the observer saw 24 words, so the next set gives both the same
sufficient context — and the refined wording frozen before any of it is generated.

## The order that keeps the claims separate

1. **Expert extension stability** — do fresh judgments fit the refined concept? A property of the target.
2. **Observer qualification** — can any instrument recover those judgments? A property of the instrument.
3. **Carrier effect** — only then, and only if 1 holds. If 2 fails but 1 holds, the study proceeds
   with blind expert scoring: a target only a person can see is still a target.
