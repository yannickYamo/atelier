## Summary

Wayfarer Fintech Ltd: theft from an unlocked desk of a camera body and lens at the insured's office on Harbour Lane. Declined under exclusion 7.1.

## Facts

Wayfarer Fintech Ltd reported the loss on 10 September 2026 under policy CC-1407 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at the insured's office on Harbour Lane [notice of loss]. The items are a camera body and a lens [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 12 September 2026 under reference CR/2026/4143 [police report]. The items were left in a shared area at the time [police report]. They were not in a locked container [police report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 applies: the items were left unattended in a shared space and were not in a locked container or locked room [police report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: the loss was reported to the police within seven days [police report], [policy].

## Decision

Not covered: exclusion 7.1 applies. The claim is declined, and the insured is told why in the terms of the policy.

## Reserve

No reserve is set: the claim is declined.
