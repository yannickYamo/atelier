## Summary

Yewtree Biotech AS (policy CC-1444): water damage from a burst pipe to two monitors and a desktop workstation at a client site in Kilburn. Covered under clause 4.7; reserve 6,986 dollars.

## Facts

Yewtree Biotech AS reported the loss on 15 March 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a client site in Kilburn [notice of loss]. The items are two monitors and a desktop workstation [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The incident was recorded on 17 March 2026 under reference IR/2026/4156 [incident report]. The circumstances recorded are water damage from a burst pipe, with no third party involved [incident report]. The monitors are invoiced at 4,244 dollars [invoice 1] and the workstation at 2,742 dollars [invoice 2].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.7 of the policy, which covers damage to contents caused by water escaping from any fixed pipe, tank or appliance [policy]. The incident report records water damage from a burst pipe [incident report]. Clause 4.7 does not depend on the premises where the loss occurred, so the client-site location does not affect cover under it [policy].

- Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: it concerns theft of unattended portable equipment, and no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy].
- Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: it concerns gradual deterioration, and the incident report records a burst pipe, with no gradual deterioration recorded [incident report], [policy].
- Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: it applies only where theft is alleged, and no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.7. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 6,986 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (4,244 dollars and 2,742 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. No excess is shown in the file, so none applies under clause 9.1 [policy].
