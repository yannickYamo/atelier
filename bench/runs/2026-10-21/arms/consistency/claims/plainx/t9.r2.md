## Summary

Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd (policy CC-1703): theft of a laptop and an external drive from a locked storage unit at a university library. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 4,732 dollars.

## Facts

Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd reported the loss on 26 September 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a university library [notice of loss]. The items are a laptop and an external drive [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 28 September 2026 under reference CR/2026/4247 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report]. The file does not state the date of the theft, and it does not include the policy schedule [notice of loss], [police report], [policy].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents caused by theft, at any other premises while in the insured's custody [policy]. The police report describes theft of the two items from a locked storage unit [police report].

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container at the time [police report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 does not apply on the file as it stands: the police report is dated 28 September 2026, and the notice of loss is dated 26 September 2026, so the theft was reported to the police within seven days [police report], [notice of loss], [policy]. The theft date is not stated in the file, and this point should be confirmed when the file is next updated.

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is alleged to be theft, and no gradual deterioration is alleged [police report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 4,732 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (2,603 dollars and 2,129 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. No excess is deducted: the file shows no excess in the schedule, and clause 9.1 provides that none applies where none is shown [policy].
