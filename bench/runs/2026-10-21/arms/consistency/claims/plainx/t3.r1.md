## Summary

Zephyr Point Media Ltd (policy CC-1481): theft from a locked van of a 3D printer and filament stock at a shared workshop on Tanner Row. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 6,264 dollars.

## Facts

Zephyr Point Media Ltd reported the loss on 20 April 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a shared workshop on Tanner Row [notice of loss]. The items are a 3D printer and filament stock [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 22 April 2026, reference CR/2026/4169 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at any premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy]. The file records the items as held in a locked van at the workshop when the theft occurred [notice of loss], [police report].

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the workshop is a shared space, but the items were in a locked container [police report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is a theft and no gradual deterioration is recorded [notice of loss], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: the loss was reported to the police on 22 April 2026, within seven days of the notice of loss on 20 April 2026 [police report], [notice of loss], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 6,264 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (5,181 dollars and 1,083 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The policy schedule is not in the file and no excess is shown, so under clause 9.1 none applies [policy].
