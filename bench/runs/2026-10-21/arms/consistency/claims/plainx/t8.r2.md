## Summary

Elmhurst Clinics LLP (policy CC-1666): fire damage in a neighbouring unit to a label printer and a barcode scanner, at a storage unit on Mill Road. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 3,254 dollars.

## Facts

Elmhurst Clinics LLP reported the loss on 21 June 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a storage unit on Mill Road [notice of loss]. The cause was fire damage in a neighbouring unit [notice of loss], [incident report]. The items are a label printer and a barcode scanner [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The incident was recorded on 23 June 2026 under reference IR/2026/4234 [incident report]. No third party was involved [incident report]. The invoices are for 1,666 dollars [invoice 1] and 1,588 dollars [invoice 2].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at the named premises, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water [policy]. The damage was caused by fire [notice of loss], [incident report].

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: it concerns theft of unattended portable equipment, and no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the damage resulted from a fire, not gradual deterioration [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: it operates only where theft is alleged, and none is [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 3,254 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (1,666 dollars and 1,588 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. No excess is shown in the file, so none applies under clause 9.1 [policy].
