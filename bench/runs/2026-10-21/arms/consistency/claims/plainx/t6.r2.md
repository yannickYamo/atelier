## Summary

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc: power surge after a storm affecting a laptop and a phone at a conference venue in Bristol. No exclusion applies, but clause 4.9 requires the equipment to have been connected through a surge protector, and the file does not say whether it was. The decision is held until that is confirmed. A provisional reserve of 6,598 dollars is set.

## Facts

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc reported the loss on 11 March 2026 under policy CC-1592 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a conference venue in Bristol [notice of loss]. The cause was a power surge after a storm [notice of loss], [incident report]. The incident was recorded on 13 March 2026 under reference IR/2026/4208, and no third party was involved [incident report]. The items are a laptop and a phone [incident report]. The laptop was invoiced at 3,892 dollars by Beacon Office Supply [invoice 1]. The phone was invoiced at 2,706 dollars by Crane & Sons Electronics [invoice 2]. The file does not state whether the equipment was connected through a surge protector [notice of loss], [incident report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls to be considered under clause 4.9 of the policy, which covers damage to electronic equipment caused by a surge in the electrical supply, where the equipment was connected through a surge protector [policy]. The laptop and phone are electronic equipment, and the cause recorded is a surge [incident report]. The surge protector condition is not evidenced in the file, so clause 4.9 cannot be confirmed as met.

- Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: no theft is alleged [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy].
- Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is recorded as a single surge event, and no gradual deterioration is recorded [incident report], [policy].
- Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: it is limited to losses where theft is alleged, and none is [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

The decision is held. No exclusion applies, but cover under clause 4.9 depends on confirmation that the laptop and phone were connected through a surge protector. The insured should be asked for that confirmation. If it is given, the claim is covered under clause 4.9. If the equipment was not connected through a surge protector, clause 4.9 is not met and the claim would need to be reassessed.

## Reserve

The provisional reserve is 6,598 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (3,892 dollars and 2,706 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The file shows no excess in the schedule, so none is applied under clause 9.1 [policy].
