## Summary

Dovecote Learning Ltd: theft from a locked office overnight of a soldering station and an oscilloscope at a rented studio on Pike Street. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 5,876 dollars.

## Facts

Dovecote Learning Ltd reported the loss on 16 April 2026 under policy CC-1629 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a rented studio on Pike Street [notice of loss]. The items are a soldering station and an oscilloscope [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 18 April 2026, reference CR/2026/4221 [police report]. The theft was from a locked office overnight [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report]. The soldering station is invoiced at 4,829 dollars by Beacon Office Supply [invoice 1]. The oscilloscope is invoiced at 1,047 dollars by Crane & Sons Electronics [invoice 2]. The file does not state the date of the theft itself, and it contains no policy schedule [notice of loss], [police report], [policy].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy]. The loss was a theft of business contents from a rented studio [notice of loss], [police report].

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container, and the theft was from a locked office [police report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: the loss was reported to the police on 18 April 2026, two days after the notice of loss on 16 April 2026, and the file shows no earlier date of loss [police report], [notice of loss], [policy].

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is a theft, not gradual deterioration [police report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 5,876 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (4,829 dollars and 1,047 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. No excess is shown in the file, and under clause 9.1 none applies where the schedule shows none [policy].
