## Summary

Elmhurst Clinics LLP (policy CC-1666): fire damage in a neighbouring unit of a label printer and a barcode scanner at a storage unit on Mill Road. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 3,254 dollars.

## Facts

Elmhurst Clinics LLP reported the loss on 21 June 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a storage unit on Mill Road [notice of loss]. The cause was fire in a neighbouring unit [notice of loss], [incident report]. The items are a label printer and a barcode scanner [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The incident was recorded on 23 June 2026 under reference IR/2026/4234, and no third party was involved [incident report]. The policy number is CC-1666 [notice of loss]. No theft is alleged [notice of loss], [incident report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water [policy]. The damage was caused by fire [incident report].

The file does not say whether the Mill Road storage unit is a premises named in the schedule or one in the insured's custody. The schedule is not in the file. This should be confirmed against the schedule and the insured's tenancy or storage terms before payment.

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: it concerns theft of unattended portable equipment, and no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: it concerns gradual deterioration, and the damage is described as resulting from a fire [incident report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: it applies only where theft is alleged, and none is [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted, subject to confirming that the storage unit is a scheduled premises or was in the insured's custody.

## Reserve

The reserve is 3,254 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (1,666 dollars and 1,588 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. No excess is deducted because the file shows none; under clause 9.1, where no excess is shown in the schedule, none applies [policy]. If the schedule, once obtained, shows the 250 dollar excess, the reserve becomes 3,004 dollars [policy].
