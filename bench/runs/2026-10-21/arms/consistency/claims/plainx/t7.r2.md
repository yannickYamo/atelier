## Summary

Dovecote Learning Ltd (policy CC-1629): theft from a locked office overnight of a soldering station and an oscilloscope at a rented studio on Pike Street. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 5,876 dollars.

## Facts

Dovecote Learning Ltd reported the loss on 16 April 2026 under policy CC-1629 [notice of loss]. The loss was theft from a locked office overnight [notice of loss]. It occurred at a rented studio on Pike Street [notice of loss]. The items are a soldering station [invoice 1] and an oscilloscope [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 18 April 2026 under reference CR/2026/4221 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report]. The soldering station is invoiced at 4,829 dollars [invoice 1] and the oscilloscope at 1,047 dollars [invoice 2]. The file does not state the date on which the theft occurred. The policy extract contains no schedule [policy].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy].

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container [police report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 does not apply on the file as it stands. The loss was reported to the insurer on 16 April 2026 [notice of loss] and to the police on 18 April 2026, two days later [police report], so the police report falls within seven days [policy]. The date of the theft itself is not in the file and should be recorded when confirmed.

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is a theft, not gradual deterioration [notice of loss], [policy].

The extract contains no schedule, so I could not check which premises it names. Cover rests on the items being at a rented studio in the insured's custody [notice of loss], [policy]. That should be checked against the schedule.

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 5,876 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (4,829 dollars and 1,047 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. No excess is shown in the file, so under clause 9.1 none applies [policy].
