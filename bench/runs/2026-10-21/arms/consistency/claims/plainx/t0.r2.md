## Summary

Vellum Press Inc (policy CC-1370): theft of a laptop and docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 4,030 dollars.

## Facts

Vellum Press Inc reported the loss on 5 June 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a co-working space on Fenwick Street [notice of loss]. The items are a laptop and a docking station [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 7 June 2026, reference CR/2026/4130 [police report]. The items were in a locked locker, a locked container, at the time [notice of loss], [police report]. The file does not state the date on which the theft took place [notice of loss], [police report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy].

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container at the time, which is the exception the clause provides [police report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 does not apply on the file as it stands: the police report is dated 7 June 2026, two days after the notice of loss dated 5 June 2026 [police report], [notice of loss], [policy]. The date of the theft is not recorded in the file, so it should be added to the file for completeness.

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: no gradual deterioration is alleged [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 4,030 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (2,370 dollars and 1,660 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. No excess is shown in the file, so none applies under clause 9.1 [policy].
