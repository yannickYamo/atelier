## Summary

Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd (policy CC-1703): theft of a laptop and an external drive from a locked storage unit at a university library. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 4,732 dollars.

## Facts

Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd reported the loss on 26 September 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a university library [notice of loss]. The items are a laptop and an external drive [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 28 September 2026, reference CR/2026/4247 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report]. The file does not state the date of the theft itself [notice of loss], [police report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents caused by theft, at premises other than those named in the schedule while in the insured's custody [policy].

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container at the time [police report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 does not apply on the file: the police report is dated 28 September 2026, two days after the notice of loss dated 26 September 2026, so the theft was reported to the police within seven days of the loss being notified [notice of loss], [police report], [policy]. Because the file does not give the date of the theft, that date should be confirmed for the record.

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is alleged to be theft, not gradual deterioration [notice of loss], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 4,732 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (2,603 dollars and 2,129 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. No excess is shown in the file, so under clause 9.1 none applies [policy].
