## Summary

Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd (policy CC-1703): theft of a laptop and an external drive from a locked storage unit at a university library. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 4,732 dollars.

## Facts

Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd reported the loss on 26 September 2026 under policy CC-1703 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a university library [notice of loss]. The items are a laptop and an external drive [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 28 September 2026 under reference CR/2026/4247 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report]. The laptop was invoiced at 2,603 dollars by Beacon Office Supply [invoice 1]. The external drive was invoiced at 2,129 dollars by Crane & Sons Electronics [invoice 2].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at any premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy]. The notice of loss and police report describe a theft from a locked storage unit [notice of loss], [police report].

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container at the time [police report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is a theft, not gradual deterioration [notice of loss], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: the loss was reported to the police on 28 September 2026, within seven days of the notice of loss on 26 September 2026 [police report], [notice of loss], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 4,732 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (2,603 dollars and 2,129 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The policy extract in the file shows no excess, so under clause 9.1 none applies [policy].
