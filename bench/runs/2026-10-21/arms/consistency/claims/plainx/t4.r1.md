## Summary

Alder Mews Architects (policy CC-1518): accidental drop during transport of a tablet and a drawing display at a hotel meeting room in Leeds. Covered under clause 4.5, subject to one confirmation noted below; reserve 3,642 dollars.

## Facts

Alder Mews Architects reported the loss on 25 June 2026 [notice of loss]. The policy number is CC-1518 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a hotel meeting room in Leeds [notice of loss]. The cause was an accidental drop during transport [notice of loss], [incident report]. The incident was recorded on 27 June 2026 under reference IR/2026/4182 [incident report]. No third party was involved [incident report]. The items are a tablet, supplied by Beacon Office Supply for 2,018 dollars [invoice 1], and a drawing display, supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics for 1,624 dollars [invoice 2]. The file records no theft allegation [notice of loss], [incident report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.5 of the policy, which covers accidental loss of or damage to portable equipment while being carried by an employee between premises [policy]. A tablet and a drawing display are portable equipment, and the cause is recorded as accidental [incident report]. The file does not state who was carrying the items, so that the carrier was an employee is to be confirmed with the insured [incident report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: it concerns theft of unattended property, and no theft is alleged [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the damage is attributed to a single drop, not gradual deterioration [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: it operates only where theft is alleged, and none is [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.5. The claim is accepted, subject to confirming that the items were being carried by an employee.

## Reserve

The reserve is 3,642 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (2,018 dollars and 1,624 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The policy extract in the file contains no schedule and shows no excess, so under clause 9.1 none applies [policy]. If the full schedule shows an excess, the reserve should be reduced accordingly.
