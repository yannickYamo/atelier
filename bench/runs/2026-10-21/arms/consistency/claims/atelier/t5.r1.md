## Summary

Bramble Hill Dental: theft from an unattended bag of a projector and a sound bar at the insured's van, parked overnight. Declined under exclusion 7.1.

## Facts

Bramble Hill Dental reported the loss on 6 September 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at the insured's van, parked overnight [notice of loss]. The items are a projector and a sound bar [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 8 September 2026 [police report]. The items were not in a locked container at the time [police report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers theft of or damage to business contents in the insured's custody [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 applies: the items were unattended in a shared space and not in a locked container [police report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: the loss was reported to the police within seven days [police report], [policy].

## Decision

Not covered: exclusion 7.1 applies. The claim is declined, and the insured is told why in the terms of the policy.

## Reserve

No reserve is set: the claim is declined.
