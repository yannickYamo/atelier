## Summary

Yewtree Biotech AS: water damage from a burst pipe of two monitors and a desktop workstation at a client site in Kilburn. Covered under clause 4.7; reserve 6,986 dollars.

## Facts

Yewtree Biotech AS reported the loss on 15 March 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a client site in Kilburn [notice of loss]. The items are two monitors and a desktop workstation [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The incident was recorded on 17 March 2026 [incident report]. The incident report records no third party involved [incident report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.7 of the policy, which covers damage to contents caused by water escaping from any fixed pipe, tank or appliance [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the damage arises from a burst pipe and not from gradual deterioration [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.7. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 6,986 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (4,244 dollars and 2,742 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]; no excess is shown in the schedule, so none applies [policy].
