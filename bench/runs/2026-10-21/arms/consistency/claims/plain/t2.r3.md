# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Yewtree Biotech AS
**Policy:** CC-1444
**Incident report ref:** IR/2026/4156

## Summary

Yewtree Biotech AS reported water damage to two monitors and a desktop workstation, caused by a burst pipe at a client site in Kilburn. The claim is assessed as covered under clause 4.7 (escape of water). None of the exclusions in clause 7 applies on the file. The reserve is 6,986 dollars, with no excess applied because no schedule showing one is in the file.

## Facts

- Yewtree Biotech AS reported the loss on 15 March 2026 under policy CC-1444. [notice of loss]
- The loss is described as water damage from a burst pipe affecting two monitors and a desktop workstation. [notice of loss]
- The location is a client site in Kilburn. [notice of loss] [incident report]
- An incident report dated 17 March 2026 was made by Yewtree Biotech AS. It lists the items as two monitors and a desktop workstation. [incident report]
- The incident report states the circumstances as water damage from a burst pipe at a client site in Kilburn, with no third party involved. [incident report]
- Two monitors were supplied by Beacon Office Supply, for 4,244 dollars. [invoice 1]
- A desktop workstation was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics, for 2,742 dollars. [invoice 2]
- The file contains no police report and no theft is alleged. [notice of loss] [incident report]
- The file contains no policy schedule, and no date of loss is stated. [notice of loss] [incident report]

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.7 covers damage to contents caused by water escaping from any fixed pipe, tank or appliance. [policy, cl. 4.7] The notice of loss and incident report both describe damage to equipment caused by a burst pipe. [notice of loss] [incident report] The loss falls within clause 4.7. Clause 4.7 contains no restriction by premises. Clause 4.2 also lists escape of water as an insured cause, but it applies to premises named in the schedule or other premises where the property is in the insured's custody. [policy, cl. 4.2] The schedule and the question of custody are not documented in the file, so this assessment rests on clause 4.7.

**Other insuring clauses.** Clause 4.5 (transit) and clause 4.9 (electrical surge) are not engaged. The file describes neither carriage of equipment by an employee nor an electrical surge. [policy, cl. 4.5, 4.9] [incident report]

**Exclusions.**
- **Clause 7.1 (unattended property):** this excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space. [policy, cl. 7.1] No theft is reported. [notice of loss] [incident report] The exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.3 (wear and tear):** this excludes gradual deterioration. [policy, cl. 7.3] The file describes damage from a burst pipe, not gradual deterioration. [notice of loss] [incident report] The exclusion does not apply on the file.
- **Clause 7.5 (unproven loss):** this applies where theft is alleged and not reported to the police within seven days. [policy, cl. 7.5] No theft is alleged. [notice of loss] [incident report] The exclusion does not apply.

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.7 (escape of water). [policy, cl. 4.7]

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less the 250 dollar excess where the schedule applies one. [policy, cl. 9.1]

| Item | Amount (dollars) |
|---|---|
| Two monitors [invoice 1] | 4,244 |
| Desktop workstation [invoice 2] | 2,742 |
| **Sum of invoices** | **6,986** |
| Excess | 0 |
| **Reserve** | **6,986** |

No policy schedule is in the file, so no excess is shown. Clause 9.1 provides that none applies in that case. [policy, cl. 9.1] If the schedule is later obtained and shows an excess, the reserve should be revised under clause 9.1. With the 250 dollar excess it would be 6,736 dollars.
