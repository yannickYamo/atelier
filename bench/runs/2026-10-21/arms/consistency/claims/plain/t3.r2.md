# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Zephyr Point Media Ltd | **Policy:** CC-1481

## Summary

Zephyr Point Media Ltd reported the theft of a 3D printer and filament stock from a locked van at a shared workshop on Tanner Row. The claim falls within clause 4.2 (Contents, theft), and the locked-container exception in clause 7.1 preserves cover. Two points the file does not settle are flagged below: the date of the theft and the policy schedule. The decision is that the claim is covered under clause 4.2, subject to those two confirmations. The reserve is 6,264 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured is Zephyr Point Media Ltd, under policy CC-1481 [notice of loss].
- The loss was reported to us on 20 April 2026 [notice of loss].
- The loss is theft of a 3D printer and filament stock from a locked van at a shared workshop on Tanner Row [notice of loss].
- The theft was reported to the police on 22 April 2026 under reference CR/2026/4169, by the insured [police report].
- The items stolen were a 3D printer and filament stock [police report].
- The items were in a locked container at the time of the theft [police report].
- The 3D printer is evidenced by an invoice from Beacon Office Supply for 5,181 dollars [invoice 1].
- The filament stock is evidenced by an invoice from Crane & Sons Electronics for 1,083 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file does not state the date of the theft, whose custody the van or workshop was in, or the contents of the policy schedule. The file contains only a policy extract.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody [policy, 4.2]. The loss is theft of business contents [notice of loss; police report]. The schedule is not in the file, so I cannot confirm that Tanner Row is a named premises or that the items were in the insured's custody there. Cover under 4.2 depends on one of these being confirmed.

**Other cover clauses.**
- Clause 4.5 (Transit) requires accidental loss while the equipment is carried by an employee between premises [policy, 4.5]. The file does not describe the loss that way, so I do not rely on it.
- Clause 4.7 (Escape of water) and clause 4.9 (Electrical surge) do not apply, because the cause of loss is theft [policy, 4.7, 4.9; notice of loss].

**Exclusions.**
- **Clause 7.1 (Unattended property).** It excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room [policy, 7.1]. The workshop is shared [notice of loss], but the items were in a locked container [police report]. The exception applies, so the exclusion does not bar the claim.
- **Clause 7.3 (Wear and tear).** It excludes gradual deterioration [policy, 7.3]. The loss is a theft, not deterioration [notice of loss; police report], so the exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.5 (Unproven loss).** It excludes theft not reported to the police within seven days [policy, 7.5]. The police report is dated 22 April 2026 [police report], and the loss was reported to us on 20 April 2026 [notice of loss]. The theft therefore occurred on or before 20 April 2026. The report is within seven days if the theft occurred on or after 15 April 2026. The file does not give the theft date, so this must be confirmed before the exclusion can be treated as fully cleared.

## Decision

**Covered** under clause 4.2 (Contents, theft) [policy, 4.2]. Clause 7.1 does not bar the claim because of its locked-container exception [policy, 7.1; police report]. Clauses 7.3 and 7.5 are addressed above, with 7.5 subject to confirmation of the theft date. This decision is conditional on:
1. confirming the theft date was on or after 15 April 2026 (clause 7.5), and
2. confirming from the schedule that Tanner Row is a named premises, or that the items were in the insured's custody there (clause 4.2).
