# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Zephyr Point Media Ltd
**Policy:** CC-1481
**Police reference:** CR/2026/4169

## Summary

Zephyr Point Media Ltd reported the theft of a 3D printer and filament stock from a locked van at a shared workshop on Tanner Row [notice of loss]. The loss was reported to the police on 22 April 2026 [police report]. The claim is assessed as covered under clause 4.2, subject to the two confirmations noted in the Coverage analysis. The reserve is 6,264 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured notified the loss on 20 April 2026 under policy CC-1481 [notice of loss].
- The loss is described as theft from a locked van of a 3D printer and filament stock, at a shared workshop on Tanner Row [notice of loss].
- The insured reported the theft to the police on 22 April 2026, reference CR/2026/4169 [police report].
- The items reported stolen were a 3D printer and filament stock [police report].
- The items were in a locked container at the time of the theft [police report].
- The 3D printer was supplied by Beacon Office Supply at 5,181 dollars [invoice 1].
- The filament stock was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 1,083 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file does not state the date of the theft. It does not include the policy schedule, so the named premises and any excess are not shown [policy].

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody [policy, cl. 4.2]. The loss is a theft of business contents [notice of loss; police report]. The file does not show whether Tanner Row is a scheduled premises or whether the items were in the insured's custody there. Confirm this against the schedule.

**Other insuring clauses.** Clause 4.5 (transit) is not relied on, because the file does not say the items were being carried by an employee [policy, cl. 4.5]. Clauses 4.7 (escape of water) and 4.9 (electrical surge) do not apply, because no water or surge event is reported [notice of loss; policy, cl. 4.7, 4.9].

**Exclusions:**

- **Clause 7.1 (unattended property).** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room [policy, cl. 7.1]. The workshop is shared [notice of loss]. The items were in a locked container, and the van was locked [police report; notice of loss]. The exception applies, so the exclusion does not bar the claim.
- **Clause 7.3 (wear and tear).** This excludes gradual deterioration [policy, cl. 7.3]. The loss is a theft, and no deterioration is reported [notice of loss]. The exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.5 (unproven loss).** This excludes theft not reported to the police within seven days [policy, cl. 7.5]. The police report is dated 22 April 2026, two days after the insured's notice of loss on 20 April 2026 [police report; notice of loss]. The file does not give the date of the theft, so the seven-day period cannot be calculated. The loss occurred on or before 20 April 2026 [notice of loss]. Confirm the date of loss before the claim is paid.

**Confirmations required before payment:**

1. The date of loss, to close out clause 7.5.
2. The schedule and custody position at Tanner Row, to close out clause 4.2.

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (Contents), because the loss is theft of business contents [policy, cl. 4.2]. Clause 7.1 does not bar the claim, because the items were in a locked container [policy, cl. 7.1; police report]. The decision is subject to the two confirmations above.
