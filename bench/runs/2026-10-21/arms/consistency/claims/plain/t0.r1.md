# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Vellum Press Inc
**Policy:** CC-1370

## Summary

Vellum Press Inc reported the theft of a laptop and a docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. The claim falls under the Commercial Contents Policy. On the file as it stands, the loss is covered under clause 4.2, and no exclusion removes it. Two points need confirming on the file: the date of the theft, for clause 7.5, and the premises schedule, for clause 4.2 and the excess. The reserve is 4,030 dollars.

## Facts

- Vellum Press Inc reported the loss on 5 June 2026 under policy CC-1370. [notice of loss]
- The reported loss is theft of a laptop and docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. [notice of loss]
- The theft was reported to police under reference CR/2026/4130, dated 7 June 2026, with Vellum Press Inc as the reporting party. [police report]
- The police report lists the items as a laptop and a docking station. It records that they were in a locked container at the time. [police report]
- The laptop is invoiced by Beacon Office Supply at 2,370 dollars. [invoice 1]
- The docking station is invoiced by Crane & Sons Electronics at 1,660 dollars. [invoice 2]
- The file does not state the date of the theft. [notice of loss] [police report]
- The policy schedule is not in the file. [policy]

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody. The loss is theft of business equipment from a locker at a co-working space [notice of loss] [police report]. It falls within the theft peril of clause 4.2 [policy, cl. 4.2]. The schedule is not in the file, so it is not confirmed whether the Fenwick Street premises are named there. Cover for the items in a locker the insured used at the co-working space rests on the "other premises while in the insured's custody" limb. This should be confirmed against the schedule.

**Other cover clauses.** Clauses 4.5 (transit), 4.7 (escape of water) and 4.9 (electrical surge) do not apply. The loss is theft from a locker, not loss while being carried, water damage or a surge [notice of loss] [policy, cl. 4.5, 4.7, 4.9].

**Exclusions.**
- **Clause 7.1 (unattended property).** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared space unless it was in a locked container or locked room. The co-working space is a shared space, but the items were in a locked locker [notice of loss] [police report]. The exception applies, so the exclusion does not bar the claim [policy, cl. 7.1].
- **Clause 7.3 (wear and tear).** This excludes gradual deterioration. The loss is theft, not deterioration, so the clause does not apply [notice of loss] [policy, cl. 7.3].
- **Clause 7.5 (unproven loss).** This excludes theft not reported to police within seven days. The police report is dated 7 June 2026, two days after the notice of loss dated 5 June 2026 [police report] [notice of loss]. The date of the theft is not in the file, so the seven-day period cannot be measured directly. The theft was reported to the insurer on 5 June, so it occurred on or before that date. The police report falls within seven days if the theft occurred on or after 31 May 2026. The date of theft should be confirmed with the insured before the clause 7.5 position is treated as final [policy, cl. 7.5].

## Decision

**Covered** under clause 4.2 (theft of business contents). Clause 7.1 does not apply because the items were in a locked container. Clauses 7.3 and 7.5 do not operate on the file as it stands, subject to confirming the date of theft (clause 7.5) and the premises position in the schedule (clause 4.2).

## Reserve

No schedule is in the file, so no excess is shown, and under clause 9.1 none applies [policy, cl. 9.1].
