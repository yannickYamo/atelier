# Claims Assessment Memo

## Summary

Bramble Hill Dental (policy CC-1555) reported on 6 September 2026 the theft of a projector and a sound bar from an unattended bag in the insured's van, which was parked overnight. The police report states the items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container. The claim is **not covered**, because exclusion 7.1 (unattended property) applies. The reserve under clause 9.1 is 5,120 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured is Bramble Hill Dental, and the policy number is CC-1555 [notice of loss].
- The loss was reported to us on 6 September 2026 [notice of loss].
- The loss is the theft of a projector and a sound bar from an unattended bag in the insured's van, parked overnight [notice of loss].
- The theft was reported to the police on 8 September 2026 under reference CR/2026/4195, by Bramble Hill Dental [police report].
- The police report records that the items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container [police report].
- The projector was supplied by Beacon Office Supply at 2,955 dollars [invoice 1].
- The sound bar was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 2,165 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file does not state the date of the theft, and it contains no policy schedule.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clauses**
- **Clause 4.2 (Contents):** This covers theft of business contents at the scheduled premises, and at other premises while in the insured's custody [policy, cl. 4.2]. The schedule is not in the file, so I cannot confirm that a parked van falls within "premises". The decision below does not depend on this point, because the exclusion applies even if clause 4.2 is met.
- **Clause 4.5 (Transit):** This covers accidental loss of portable equipment while it is carried by an employee between premises [policy, cl. 4.5]. The file describes theft from an unattended bag in a parked van, not equipment being carried by an employee [notice of loss; police report]. This clause does not respond.
- **Clauses 4.7 and 4.9:** These cover escape of water and electrical surge [policy, cl. 4.7, 4.9]. Neither is alleged, so neither applies.

**Exclusions**
- **Clause 7.1 (Unattended property):** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room at the time [policy, cl. 7.1]. The items were taken from an unattended bag, were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container [notice of loss; police report]. The projector and sound bar are portable equipment, and the exception does not apply. **The exclusion applies.**
- **Clause 7.3 (Wear and tear):** This excludes gradual deterioration [policy, cl. 7.3]. The loss is a theft, not deterioration, so the exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.5 (Unproven loss):** This excludes a theft loss not reported to the police within seven days [policy, cl. 7.5]. The police report is dated 8 September 2026, two days after the notice of loss on 6 September 2026 [police report; notice of loss]. The date of the theft is not in the file, so I cannot test the seven-day period from that date. I have not relied on this exclusion, and the date should be confirmed for completeness.

## Decision

**Not covered.** Exclusion 7.1 removes cover for theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared space and not in a locked container or room [policy, cl. 7.1]. The police report records those circumstances [police report].

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices less any excess the schedule applies [policy, cl. 9.1].

- Invoice 1, projector: 2,955 dollars [invoice 1]
- Invoice 2, sound bar: 2,165 dollars [invoice 2]
- Sum of invoices: 5,120 dollars
- Excess: no schedule is in the file and no excess is shown, so none applies [policy, cl. 9.1]

**Reserve: 5,120 dollars.** This is the figure the clause 9.1 formula produces. Given the decision above, no payment arises unless the decision is revisited.
