# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Bramble Hill Dental | **Policy:** CC-1555 | **Police ref:** CR/2026/4195

## Summary

Bramble Hill Dental claims for the theft of a projector and a sound bar from an unattended bag at the insured's van, which was parked overnight. The loss was reported to us on 6 September 2026 and to the police on 8 September 2026. The police report records that the items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container. The claim is **not covered**, because exclusion 7.1 (unattended property) applies.

## Facts

- The insured is Bramble Hill Dental, under policy CC-1555. [notice of loss]
- The loss was reported to us on 6 September 2026. [notice of loss]
- The loss is the theft of a projector and a sound bar from an unattended bag. [notice of loss]
- The location is the insured's van, parked overnight. [notice of loss]
- The insured reported the theft to the police on 8 September 2026 under reference CR/2026/4195. [police report]
- The police report describes the circumstances as theft from an unattended bag at the insured's van, parked overnight. [police report]

- The sound bar was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 2,165 dollars. [invoice 2]
- The file does not state the date of the theft. It also does not include the policy schedule, so the named premises and any excess are not shown. [notice of loss] [police report] [policy]

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clauses**
- **Clause 4.2 (Contents)** covers theft of business contents at the scheduled premises, or at other premises while in the insured's custody. [policy cl. 4.2] The schedule is not in the file, and the file does not say whether a parked van is premises within this clause. The clause is assumed to be met for the purpose of this analysis, because the exclusion below is decisive.
- **Clause 4.5 (Transit)** covers accidental loss of portable equipment while being carried by an employee between premises. [policy cl. 4.5] The file describes items left in a bag at a parked van overnight, not equipment being carried by an employee. [notice of loss] [police report] This clause does not respond.
- **Clauses 4.7 (Escape of water) and 4.9 (Electrical surge)** do not apply. The loss is a theft, and neither water escape nor a power surge is reported. [notice of loss] [policy cl. 4.7, 4.9]

**Exclusions**
- **Clause 7.1 (Unattended property)** excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room at the time. [policy cl. 7.1] The projector and sound bar are portable equipment. They were taken from an unattended bag, left in a shared area, and were not in a locked container. [notice of loss] [police report] Neither document says the items were in a locked room. **This exclusion applies.**
- **Clause 7.3 (Wear and tear)** excludes gradual deterioration. [policy cl. 7.3] The loss is a theft, and no deterioration is reported. [notice of loss] This exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.5 (Unproven loss)** excludes a theft not reported to the police within seven days. [policy cl. 7.5] The police report is dated 8 September 2026, two days after the notice of loss of 6 September 2026. [police report] [notice of loss] The file does not give the date of the theft, so compliance with the seven-day period cannot be confirmed from the file alone. This does not change the outcome, because clause 7.1 applies independently.

## Decision

**Not covered.** Cover that might otherwise arise under clause 4.2 is removed by exclusion 7.1 (unattended property). [policy cl. 4.2, 7.1]

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less any excess shown in the schedule. [policy cl. 9.1]

| Item | Amount (dollars) |
|---|---|
| Projector [invoice 1] | 2,955 |
| Sound bar [invoice 2] | 2,165 |
| **Sum of invoices** | **5,120** |
| Excess | none shown in the file, so none applies [policy cl. 9.1] |
| **Reserve** | **5,120** |

The reserve is the figure calculated under clause 9.1. In light of the decision above, no payment is proposed. The schedule is not in the file, so the absence of an excess should be confirmed against it.
