# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Wayfarer Fintech Ltd
**Policy:** CC-1407
**Police reference:** CR/2026/4143

## Summary

Wayfarer Fintech Ltd reported the theft of a camera body and a lens from an unlocked desk at its office on Harbour Lane. The loss was reported on 10 September 2026 and to the police on 12 September 2026. The theft falls within the contents theft cover in clause 4.2, but exclusion 7.1 (unattended property) applies because the items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container. The claim is **not covered**. The reserve calculated under clause 9.1 is 5,508 dollars.

## Facts

1. The insured is Wayfarer Fintech Ltd, under policy CC-1407. [notice of loss]
2. The loss was reported on 10 September 2026. [notice of loss]
3. The reported loss is theft of a camera body and lens from an unlocked desk at the insured's office on Harbour Lane. [notice of loss]
4. The theft was reported to the police by the insured on 12 September 2026 under reference CR/2026/4143. [police report]
5. The police report records the items as a camera body and a lens. [police report]
6. The police report states that the items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container. [police report]
7. The camera body is invoiced at 3,307 dollars by Beacon Office Supply. [invoice 1]
8. The lens is invoiced at 2,201 dollars by Crane & Sons Electronics. [invoice 2]
9. The file contains no policy schedule, no date of theft, and no information on whether the office or the area was a locked room.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft at the premises named in the schedule, and at other premises while in the insured's custody [policy, 4.2]. The loss is a theft of business items from the insured's office [notice of loss; police report]. The schedule is not in the file, so I cannot confirm that Harbour Lane is the named premises. I have treated the loss as within 4.2, subject to that confirmation. The result does not change, because the claim fails on exclusion 7.1 either way.

**Other cover grants.** Clauses 4.5 (transit), 4.7 (escape of water) and 4.9 (electrical surge) do not apply. The loss is a theft from a desk at the office, with no carriage between premises, water damage or electrical surge reported. [policy, 4.5, 4.7, 4.9; notice of loss; police report]

**Exclusions**

- **7.1 Unattended property: applies.** The clause excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room at the time [policy, 7.1]. A camera body and lens are portable equipment. The police report states that the items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container [police report]. The notice of loss describes the desk as unlocked [notice of loss]. Nothing in the file shows that the items were in a locked room. The exception in 7.1 is therefore not shown to apply.
- **7.3 Wear and tear: does not apply.** The loss is a reported theft, not gradual deterioration. [policy, 7.3; notice of loss]
- **7.5 Unproven loss: not determinative.** The clause excludes theft not reported to the police within seven days [policy, 7.5]. The police report is dated 12 September 2026 [police report], and the loss was notified on 10 September 2026 [notice of loss]. The file does not give the date of the theft, so I cannot confirm compliance with the seven-day period. This does not affect the decision, which rests on 7.1.

## Decision

**Not covered.** Although theft of business contents is within clause 4.2, the loss is excluded under clause 7.1. The items were portable equipment left in a shared area and were not in a locked container, and the file does not show a locked room. [policy, 4.2, 7.1; police report]

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1 the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less the 250 dollar excess where the schedule applies one. Where the schedule shows no excess, none applies [policy, 9.1].

- Camera body: 3,307 dollars [invoice 1]
- Lens: 2,201 dollars [invoice 2]
- Sum of invoices: 5,508 dollars
- Excess: none shown, as no schedule is in the file, so none is applied [policy, 9.1]

**Reserve: 5,508 dollars.** This is the exposure figure under clause 9.1 and is recorded notwithstanding the decision that the claim is not covered.
