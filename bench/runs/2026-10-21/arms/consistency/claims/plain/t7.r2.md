# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Dovecote Learning Ltd | **Policy:** CC-1629 | **Police ref:** CR/2026/4221

## Summary

Dovecote Learning Ltd reported the theft of a soldering station and an oscilloscope from a locked office at a rented studio on Pike Street. The theft was reported to the police on 18 April 2026. The loss is covered under clause 4.2 (Contents), and none of the exclusions in clauses 7.1, 7.3 or 7.5 applies on the file. The reserve is 5,876 dollars. The policy schedule is not in the file, so the premises and excess points noted below should be confirmed against it.

## Facts

1. Dovecote Learning Ltd notified a loss under policy CC-1629 on 16 April 2026 [notice of loss].
2. The loss is theft, overnight, from a locked office of a soldering station and an oscilloscope [notice of loss].
3. The location is a rented studio on Pike Street [notice of loss] [police report].
4. The theft was reported to the police by Dovecote Learning Ltd on 18 April 2026, reference CR/2026/4221 [police report].
5. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report].
6. The soldering station is invoiced at 4,829 dollars by Beacon Office Supply [invoice 1].
7. The oscilloscope is invoiced at 1,047 dollars by Crane & Sons Electronics [invoice 2].
8. The file does not state the date of the theft, and it does not include the policy schedule [notice of loss] [police report] [policy].

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody [policy, cl. 4.2]. The loss is theft of business equipment from a rented studio [notice of loss] [police report]. Because the schedule is not in the file, it should be confirmed that the Pike Street studio is a scheduled premises or was in the insured's custody. Clauses 4.5 (transit), 4.7 (escape of water) and 4.9 (electrical surge) are not relied on, as the loss is not one of those perils [notice of loss] [policy].

**Exclusions.**

- **Clause 7.1 (Unattended property).** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room. The theft was from a locked office, and the items were in a locked container [notice of loss] [police report]. Even if the space were treated as shared, the exception in the clause applies. The exclusion does not operate [policy, cl. 7.1].
- **Clause 7.3 (Wear and tear).** This excludes gradual deterioration. The loss is theft, not deterioration [notice of loss] [policy, cl. 7.3]. The exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.5 (Unproven loss).** This excludes theft not reported to the police within seven days [policy, cl. 7.5]. The notice of loss is dated 16 April 2026 and the police report 18 April 2026, two days later [notice of loss] [police report]. The exclusion would apply only if the theft occurred more than seven days before 18 April 2026. The file does not give the theft date, so it should be confirmed. On the file as it stands, the exclusion is not engaged.

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (Contents) [policy, cl. 4.2]. No exclusion in clause 7.1, 7.3 or 7.5 applies on the documents in the file. This is subject to confirming from the schedule that the premises falls within clause 4.2, and to confirming the theft date for clause 7.5.

## Reserve
