# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Yewtree Biotech AS
**Policy:** CC-1444
**Incident reference:** IR/2026/4156

## 1. Summary

Yewtree Biotech AS reported water damage to two monitors and a desktop workstation, caused by a burst pipe at a client site in Kilburn. The loss was reported on 15 March 2026 and no third party is involved. The loss falls within the escape of water cover at clause 4.7, and no exclusion applies. I recommend the claim be accepted, with a reserve of 6,986 dollars.

## 3. Coverage analysis

**Insuring clauses**

- Clause 4.7 covers "damage to contents caused by water escaping from any fixed pipe, tank or appliance." The loss is damage to business equipment caused by a burst pipe, which is an escape of water from a pipe. [notice of loss] [incident report] [policy cl. 4.7]
- Clause 4.2 also lists escape of water as a covered cause of loss to business contents. It applies at the premises named in the schedule, or at other premises while in the insured's custody. The schedule is not in the file, and the file does not say whether the client site was in the insured's custody. Clause 4.7 contains no premises wording, so the conclusion rests on 4.7. [policy cl. 4.2, 4.7]
- Clause 4.5 (transit) and clause 4.9 (electrical surge) are not engaged. The file records neither carriage between premises nor an electrical surge. [incident report] [policy cl. 4.5, 4.9]

**Exclusions**

- **7.1 Unattended property.** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended. No theft is alleged, so it does not apply. [notice of loss] [incident report] [policy cl. 7.1]
- **7.3 Wear and tear.** This excludes gradual deterioration. The file describes the loss as water damage from a burst pipe and records no gradual deterioration, so it does not apply. [notice of loss] [incident report] [policy cl. 7.3]
- **7.5 Unproven loss.** This applies only where theft is alleged and not reported to the police within seven days. No theft is alleged, so it does not apply. [notice of loss] [incident report] [policy cl. 7.5]

**Points to confirm on file.** The following do not change the recommendation but should be recorded:
- the date of the loss;
- that the pipe was a fixed pipe;
- the policy schedule, including any excess.

## 4. Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.7 (escape of water). [policy cl. 4.7]

## 5. Reserve

Clause 9.1 sets the reserve as the sum of the invoices in the file, less the 250 dollar excess where the policy schedule applies one. Where no excess is shown in the schedule, none applies. [policy cl. 9.1]

| Two monitors [invoice 1] | 4,244 |
| Desktop workstation [invoice 2] | 2,742 |
| **Sum of invoices** | **6,986** |
| Excess | 0 |

No schedule is in the file, so no excess is shown and none is applied. If the schedule is later obtained and applies an excess, the reserve reduces by 250 dollars to 6,736 dollars. [policy cl. 9.1]
