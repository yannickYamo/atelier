# Claims Assessment Memo

## Summary

Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd (policy CC-1703) reported on 26 September 2026 the theft of a laptop and an external drive from a locked storage unit at a university library. The loss falls within clause 4.2 (Contents), and none of the exclusions reviewed (7.1, 7.3, 7.5) removes cover on the documents in the file. The claim is assessed as covered, with two points to confirm on file (date of theft and the schedule). The reserve is 4,732 dollars.

- The loss was reported on 26 September 2026. [notice of loss]

- The external drive is supported by an invoice from Crane & Sons Electronics for 2,129 dollars. [invoice 2]

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft, at the scheduled premises and at any other premises while in the insured's custody. The loss is theft of business equipment (a laptop and an external drive) from a locked storage unit at a university library. [notice of loss] [police report] [policy 4.2] The library is not shown as a scheduled premises, so cover depends on the "other premises" limb, which requires the items to have been in the insured's custody. The file records the items as held in a locked storage unit and gives no indication that they were outside the insured's custody. The file does not state who held the key or had control of the unit. This should be confirmed.

**Other cover clauses.** Clause 4.5 (Transit) is not relied on, as the file does not say the items were being carried between premises. Clauses 4.7 (Escape of water) and 4.9 (Electrical surge) are not engaged, as no water or surge event is reported. [notice of loss] [policy 4.5, 4.7, 4.9]

**Exclusions.**

- **7.1 Unattended property.** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room. A university library may be a shared space, but the police report records that the items were in a locked container, and the notice of loss describes a locked storage unit. The exception applies, so 7.1 does not exclude the loss. [police report] [notice of loss] [policy 7.1]
- **7.3 Wear and tear.** This excludes gradual deterioration. The loss is a theft, not deterioration, so 7.3 does not apply. [notice of loss] [policy 7.3]
- **7.5 Unproven loss.** This excludes theft not reported to the police within seven days. The police report is dated 28 September 2026, two days after the notice of loss of 26 September 2026. The file does not give the date of the theft, so the seven-day period cannot be measured directly. The date of theft should be confirmed to close this point. On the dates in the file, nothing indicates the report was outside the period. [police report] [notice of loss] [policy 7.5]

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (Contents), as theft of business contents at premises in the insured's custody. No exclusion in clauses 7.1, 7.3 or 7.5 applies on the file as it stands. This is subject to confirmation of (a) the date of theft, for clause 7.5, and (b) the insured's custody of the storage unit, for clause 4.2.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices less any excess shown in the schedule. [policy 9.1]

| Item | Amount (dollars) |
|---|---|
| Laptop [invoice 1] | 2,603 |
| External drive [invoice 2] | 2,129 |
| **Sum of invoices** | **4,732** |
| Excess | none shown in the file |

The excess of 250 dollars applies only where the schedule applies one, and the schedule is not in the file. No excess is shown, so none is applied under clause 9.1. **The reserve is 4,732 dollars.** If the schedule is later found to apply an excess, the reserve would reduce to 4,482 dollars.
