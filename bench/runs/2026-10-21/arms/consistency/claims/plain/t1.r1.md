# Claims Assessment Memo

**Claim:** Wayfarer Fintech Ltd, Policy CC-1407

## Summary

Wayfarer Fintech Ltd reported the theft of a camera body and a lens from an unlocked desk at its office on Harbour Lane. The loss was notified on 10 September 2026 and reported to police on 12 September 2026. The items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container. The loss falls within the theft cover in clause 4.2, but exclusion 7.1 (unattended property) applies. The decision is **not covered**. The reserve figure under clause 9.1 is 5,508 dollars.

## Facts

1. The insured is Wayfarer Fintech Ltd, and the policy is CC-1407 [notice of loss].
2. The loss was reported to us on 10 September 2026 [notice of loss].
3. The loss is theft of a camera body and lens from an unlocked desk at the insured's office on Harbour Lane [notice of loss].
4. The insured reported the theft to police on 12 September 2026 under reference CR/2026/4143 [police report].
5. The police report records that the items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container [police report].
6. The camera body is evidenced by an invoice from Beacon Office Supply for 3,307 dollars [invoice 1].
7. The lens is evidenced by an invoice from Crane & Sons Electronics for 2,201 dollars [invoice 2].
8. The file does not contain the policy schedule. It also does not record the date of the theft or whether the room was locked.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft at the premises named in the schedule, and at other premises while in the insured's custody [policy, cl. 4.2]. The loss is a theft of business contents at the insured's office [notice of loss]. The schedule is not in the file, so I cannot confirm from the file that Harbour Lane is the scheduled premises. Even if it is, the exclusion below applies.

**Other cover clauses.** None of these is engaged on the facts in the file:
- Clause 4.5 (transit) requires the equipment to be carried by an employee between premises. The loss was a theft from a desk at the office [policy, cl. 4.5; notice of loss].
- Clause 4.7 (escape of water) is not engaged. No water damage is reported [policy, cl. 4.7; notice of loss].
- Clause 4.9 (electrical surge) is not engaged. No surge is reported [policy, cl. 4.9; notice of loss].

**Exclusions.**
- **Clause 7.1, unattended property: applies.** The clause excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room at the time [policy, cl. 7.1]. A camera body and lens are portable equipment. They were taken from an unlocked desk, having been left in a shared area and not in a locked container [notice of loss; police report]. No document in the file states that the room was locked, so the exception for a locked container or locked room is not shown to be met.
- **Clause 7.3, wear and tear: does not apply.** The loss is a theft. No gradual deterioration is reported [policy, cl. 7.3; notice of loss].
- **Clause 7.5, unproven loss: cannot be determined from the file, and the decision does not rest on it.** The clause excludes theft not reported to police within seven days [policy, cl. 7.5]. The police report is dated 12 September 2026, but the file does not give the date of the theft [police report; notice of loss]. Compliance with the seven-day period therefore cannot be confirmed or ruled out.

## Decision

**Not covered.** Although theft of business contents falls within clause 4.2, the loss is excluded by clause 7.1. The items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container, and the file does not show a locked room [policy, cl. 4.2 and 7.1; police report].

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less any excess shown in the schedule [policy, cl. 9.1].

- Invoice 1, camera body: 3,307 dollars [invoice 1]
- Invoice 2, lens: 2,201 dollars [invoice 2]
- Sum of invoices: 5,508 dollars
- Excess: none applied. The schedule is not in the file, so no excess is shown, and clause 9.1 provides that none applies where none is shown [policy, cl. 9.1].

**Reserve: 5,508 dollars.**

Because the decision is not covered, no payment is proposed on the current file. The schedule should be added to the file to confirm the scheduled premises and the excess position.
