# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Vellum Press Inc
**Policy:** CC-1370

## Summary

Vellum Press Inc claims under its commercial contents policy for the theft of a laptop and a docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. The loss was reported to us on 5 June 2026 and to the police on 7 June 2026. The loss falls within clause 4.2, and none of the exclusions in clauses 7.1, 7.3 or 7.5 removes cover on the file as it stands. The decision is that the claim is covered. The reserve is 4,030 dollars. One item in the file should be confirmed: the date of the theft (see Coverage analysis, clause 7.5).

## Facts

- Vellum Press Inc reported the loss on 5 June 2026 under policy CC-1370. The loss is described as theft of a laptop and docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. [notice of loss]
- The theft was reported to the police on 7 June 2026 under reference CR/2026/4130. The report was made by Vellum Press Inc and lists the items as a laptop and a docking station. [police report]
- The police report records that the items were in a locked container at the time of the theft, at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. [police report]
- The laptop was supplied by Beacon Office Supply at 2,370 dollars. [invoice 1]
- The docking station was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 1,660 dollars. [invoice 2]
- The file contains no policy schedule. The file does not state the date on which the theft took place. [notice of loss] [police report]

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents "at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft". The loss is theft of business equipment, namely a laptop and docking station, from a locker at a co-working space. [notice of loss] [police report] The items were held in a locked container at that location. [police report] The loss falls within clause 4.2 [policy, cl. 4.2].

**Other cover clauses.** Clauses 4.5 (transit), 4.7 (escape of water) and 4.9 (electrical surge) are not engaged. The file describes theft from a locker, not loss while being carried, water damage or a power surge. [notice of loss] [police report]

**Exclusions.**

- **Clause 7.1, unattended property.** This clause excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared space, "unless it was in a locked container or locked room at the time". The co-working space is a shared space, but the items were in a locked locker at the time of the theft. [police report] The stated exception applies, so the exclusion does not operate [policy, cl. 7.1].
- **Clause 7.3, wear and tear.** This clause excludes gradual deterioration. The loss is a theft, and nothing in the file describes deterioration. [notice of loss] [police report] The exclusion does not apply [policy, cl. 7.3].
- **Clause 7.5, unproven loss.** This clause excludes a loss not reported to the police within seven days where theft is alleged. The police report was made on 7 June 2026, two days after the notice of loss dated 5 June 2026. [police report] [notice of loss] The clause does not say when the seven days start to run, and the file does not state the date of the theft. On the file as it stands, the exclusion is not engaged [policy, cl. 7.5]. The date of the theft should be confirmed and noted on the file to close this point.

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (Contents) of the commercial contents policy. Exclusions 7.1, 7.3 and 7.5 do not apply on the present file, subject to the date-of-theft confirmation noted above.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less the 250 dollar excess where the policy schedule applies one. Where no excess is shown in the schedule, none applies [policy, cl. 9.1]. The file contains no schedule showing an excess, so none is deducted.

| Item | Amount (dollars) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop, Beacon Office Supply | 2,370 | [invoice 1] |
| Docking station, Crane & Sons Electronics | 1,660 | [invoice 2] |
| Excess | 0 | [policy, cl. 9.1] |
| **Reserve** | **4,030** | |

If the policy schedule is later added to the file and shows a 250 dollar excess, the reserve would reduce to 3,780 dollars.
