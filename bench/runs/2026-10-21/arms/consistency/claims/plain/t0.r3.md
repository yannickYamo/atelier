# Claims Assessment Memo: Vellum Press Inc, Policy CC-1370

## Summary

Vellum Press Inc reported the theft of a laptop and a docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. The loss was reported to the insurer on 5 June 2026 and to the police on 7 June 2026. The loss falls within clause 4.2 (Contents), and no exclusion is shown to apply on the file. The claim is accepted as covered, with two points to confirm (see Coverage analysis). The reserve is 4,030 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured is Vellum Press Inc, under policy CC-1370 [notice of loss].
- The loss was reported on 5 June 2026 [notice of loss].
- The loss is theft of a laptop and docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street [notice of loss].
- The theft was reported to the police on 7 June 2026 under reference CR/2026/4130, reported by Vellum Press Inc [police report].
- The items listed in the police report are a laptop and a docking station [police report].
- The items were in a locked container at the time of the theft [police report].
- The laptop was supplied by Beacon Office Supply at 2,370 dollars [invoice 1].
- The docking station was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 1,660 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file does not state the date of the theft, and it does not contain the policy schedule.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft, at the scheduled premises and at any other premises while in the insured's custody [policy cl. 4.2]. The loss is theft of business equipment from a locker at a co-working space [notice of loss; police report]. Because the schedule is not in the file, I cannot confirm the premises it names. The items were held in a locked locker, and I have treated that as custody under the second limb of clause 4.2. This should be confirmed against the schedule.

**Other insuring clauses.** Clauses 4.5 (Transit), 4.7 (Escape of water) and 4.9 (Electrical surge) do not apply. The file records theft from a locker, not carriage by an employee, water damage or a power surge [notice of loss; police report].

**Exclusions**

- **Clause 7.1 (Unattended property).** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room [policy cl. 7.1]. A co-working space is a shared space, but the items were in a locked locker [police report]. The exception applies, so the exclusion does not bar the claim.
- **Clause 7.5 (Unproven loss).** This excludes a theft loss not reported to the police within seven days [policy cl. 7.5]. The police report is dated 7 June 2026, two days after the notice of loss on 5 June 2026 [police report; notice of loss]. The file does not state the date of the theft. On the dates available, nothing indicates a delay of more than seven days, but the date of loss should be confirmed to close this point.
- **Clause 7.3 (Wear and tear).** This excludes gradual deterioration [policy cl. 7.3]. The loss is theft, not deterioration [police report], so the clause does not apply.

**Points to confirm**

1. The policy schedule, for the premises named and for any excess.
2. The date of the theft, to confirm compliance with clause 7.5.

## Decision

Covered, under clause 4.2 (Contents) [policy cl. 4.2]. Exclusion 7.1 is displaced by its locked-container exception [policy cl. 7.1; police report]. Exclusions 7.3 and 7.5 are not engaged on the file as it stands, subject to the points to confirm above.
