# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Cinder Lane Hardware Inc
**Policy:** CC-1592
**Incident reference:** IR/2026/4208

## Summary

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc reported on 11 March 2026 that a laptop and a phone were damaged by a power surge after a storm at a conference venue in Bristol. The claim is for the two items, invoiced at 3,892 dollars and 2,706 dollars. The file does not show whether the equipment was connected through a surge protector, which clause 4.9 requires. Coverage cannot be confirmed on the present file, so the decision is pending further information. The reserve on the invoices is 6,598 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured is Cinder Lane Hardware Inc, under policy CC-1592. [notice of loss]
- The loss was reported on 11 March 2026. [notice of loss]
- The reported loss is a power surge after a storm, affecting a laptop and a phone, at a conference venue in Bristol. [notice of loss]
- The incident report is dated 13 March 2026 and carries reference IR/2026/4208. [incident report]
- The items are a laptop and a phone. [incident report]
- The circumstances are a power surge after a storm at a conference venue in Bristol. No third party was involved. [incident report]
- The laptop was supplied by Beacon Office Supply for 3,892 dollars. [invoice 1]
- The phone was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics for 2,706 dollars. [invoice 2]
- The file does not state whether the equipment was connected through a surge protector. [notice of loss] [incident report]
- The file does not state the date of the storm or whether the equipment was in the insured's custody at the venue. [notice of loss] [incident report]
- The policy schedule is not in the file, so the named premises and any excess cannot be read from it. [policy]

## Coverage analysis

**Clause 4.9 (Electrical surge).** This is the clause that fits the loss. It covers damage to electronic equipment caused by a surge in the electrical supply, where the equipment was connected through a surge protector. The laptop and phone are electronic equipment, and the loss is reported as a power surge [notice of loss] [incident report]. The surge protector condition is not addressed anywhere in the file, so it is not established that the condition is met. [policy cl. 4.9]

**Clause 4.2 (Contents).** This clause covers damage caused by storm at the scheduled premises, or at other premises while the property is in the insured's custody. The reports describe the surge as occurring "after a storm" [incident report]. They do not say that the storm caused the damage, and they do not state custody at the venue. The schedule is also absent. Clause 4.2 therefore cannot be relied on as an alternative basis without further information. It also should not be used to bypass the specific condition in clause 4.9. [policy cl. 4.2]

**Other cover clauses.**
- Clause 4.5 (Transit) is not engaged. No loss while being carried between premises is reported. [incident report] [policy cl. 4.5]
- Clause 4.7 (Escape of water) is not engaged. No water escaping from a pipe, tank or appliance is reported. [incident report] [policy cl. 4.7]

**Exclusions.**
- Clause 7.1 (Unattended property) applies only to theft of portable equipment. No theft is reported, so it does not apply. [incident report] [policy cl. 7.1]
- Clause 7.3 (Wear and tear) applies to gradual deterioration. The loss is reported as a sudden surge event, and no deterioration is reported, so it does not apply. [incident report] [policy cl. 7.3]
- Clause 7.5 (Unproven loss) applies where theft is alleged. No theft is alleged, so the police reporting requirement is not engaged. [incident report] [policy cl. 7.5]

**Information needed to conclude:**
1. Whether each item was connected through a surge protector when the surge occurred.
2. The date of the storm and the loss.
3. The policy schedule, to confirm the premises and whether an excess applies.
4. If clause 4.2 is to be considered, whether the equipment was in the insured's custody at the venue.

## Decision

Coverage is not confirmed. On the present file the claim cannot be accepted under clause 4.9, because the surge protector condition is not shown to be met. No exclusion in clauses 7.1, 7.3 or 7.5 applies. The decision is pending receipt of the information listed above. If the surge protector condition is confirmed for an item, that item is covered under clause 4.9.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices less any excess shown in the schedule. [policy cl. 9.1]

| Item | Amount (dollars) |
|---|---|
| Laptop [invoice 1] | 3,892 |
| Phone [invoice 2] | 2,706 |
| **Total of invoices** | **6,598** |

The schedule is not in the file and no excess appears in it, so no excess is deducted. [policy cl. 9.1] **Reserve: 6,598 dollars.** This should be revisited when the schedule is received. If it shows an excess, the reserve is reduced accordingly. It should also be revisited if only one item meets the clause 4.9 condition.
