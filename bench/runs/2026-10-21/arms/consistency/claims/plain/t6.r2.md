# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Cinder Lane Hardware Inc
**Policy:** CC-1592

## 1. Summary

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc reported on 11 March 2026 that a laptop and a phone were damaged by a power surge after a storm at a conference venue in Bristol. Two invoices support the claim, totalling 6,598 dollars. The policy's surge cover (4.9) depends on the equipment having been connected through a surge protector, and the file does not say whether it was. Cover also depends on the custody wording in 4.2, which the file does not address. **I cannot confirm cover on the present file, and the decision is held pending the two points set out in section 4.** The reserve is set at 6,598 dollars before any excess.

## 2. Facts

- The insured is Cinder Lane Hardware Inc, under policy CC-1592. [notice of loss]
- The loss was reported on 11 March 2026. [notice of loss]
- The reported loss is a power surge after a storm affecting a laptop and a phone, at a conference venue in Bristol. [notice of loss]
- An incident report, reference IR/2026/4208, is dated 13 March 2026 and was made by the insured. It lists the items as a laptop and a phone. [incident report]
- The incident report gives the circumstances as a power surge after a storm at a conference venue in Bristol, with no third party involved. [incident report]
- Invoice 1 is from Beacon Office Supply for a laptop, 3,892 dollars. [invoice 1]
- Invoice 2 is from Crane & Sons Electronics for a phone, 2,706 dollars. [invoice 2]
- The file does not state the date of the loss, whether the equipment was connected through a surge protector, whether it was in the insured's custody at the venue, or whether an employee was carrying it. The file contains no policy schedule. [notice of loss] [incident report] [policy]
- No theft is alleged, and no police report is in the file. [notice of loss] [incident report]

## 3. Coverage analysis

**Clause 4.9 (electrical surge).** This clause covers damage to electronic equipment caused by a surge in the electrical supply, where the equipment was connected through a surge protector. A surge is reported and a laptop and a phone are electronic equipment. The file is silent on the surge protector condition, so cover under 4.9 is not established. [policy 4.9] [notice of loss] [incident report]

**Clause 4.2 (contents).** This clause covers damage to business contents at the scheduled premises, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water. The venue in Bristol is not shown as a scheduled premises, because the schedule is not in the file. Cover under 4.2 would need the items to have been in the insured's custody at the venue, and the surge to be damage caused by the storm. The file reports a surge "after a storm" but does not address custody. Cover under 4.2 is not established. [policy 4.2] [notice of loss] [incident report]

**Other insuring clauses.**
- Clause 4.5 (transit) requires the equipment to have been carried by an employee between premises. The file does not say this. [policy 4.5] [incident report]
- Clause 4.7 (escape of water) requires water escaping from a fixed pipe, tank or appliance. None is reported. [policy 4.7] [notice of loss]

**Exclusions.**
- 7.1 (unattended property) applies only to theft of portable equipment. No theft is reported, so it does not apply. [policy 7.1] [notice of loss] [incident report]
- 7.3 (wear and tear) applies to gradual deterioration. The reported cause is a surge after a storm, and nothing in the file points to gradual deterioration, so it does not apply. [policy 7.3] [incident report]
- 7.5 (unproven loss) applies where theft is alleged and not reported to the police within seven days. No theft is alleged, so it does not apply. [policy 7.5] [notice of loss] [incident report]

## 4. Decision

**Not confirmed as covered. Decision held pending information.** No exclusion applies. However, neither 4.9 nor 4.2 can be confirmed on the present file. The following are needed:

1. Whether the laptop and phone were connected through a surge protector at the time (clause 4.9).
2. Whether the items were in the insured's custody at the venue, together with the schedule showing the named premises (clause 4.2).

The date of the loss should also be confirmed. If point 1 is confirmed, cover would sit under clause 4.9. If point 2 is confirmed and the surge is shown to be damage caused by the storm, cover would sit under clause 4.2.

## 5. Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices less any excess of 250 dollars that the policy schedule applies. [policy 9.1]

- Invoice 1 (laptop): 3,892 dollars [invoice 1]
- Invoice 2 (phone): 2,706 dollars [invoice 2]

The schedule is not in the file, so I cannot confirm whether the 250 dollar excess applies. The reserve is therefore recorded at **6,598 dollars**. If the schedule shows the excess, the reserve reduces to **6,348 dollars**. [policy 9.1]
