# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Yewtree Biotech AS | **Policy:** CC-1444 | **Incident ref:** IR/2026/4156

## 1. Summary

Yewtree Biotech AS reported water damage from a burst pipe at a client site in Kilburn. The damage affected two monitors and a desktop workstation. No third party was involved. The loss falls within the escape of water cover in clause 4.7, and none of the exclusions in clause 7 applies. I recommend the claim be accepted as covered, with a reserve of 6,986 dollars on the file as it stands (see section 5 on the excess).

## 2. Facts

- Yewtree Biotech AS reported the loss on 15 March 2026 under policy CC-1444 [notice of loss].
- The reported loss is water damage from a burst pipe to two monitors and a desktop workstation [notice of loss].
- The location is a client site in Kilburn [notice of loss].
- An incident report dated 17 March 2026, reference IR/2026/4156, was made by Yewtree Biotech AS and lists the same items: two monitors and a desktop workstation [incident report].
- The incident report gives the circumstances as water damage from a burst pipe at a client site in Kilburn, with no third party involved [incident report].
- The two monitors were supplied by Beacon Office Supply, at 4,244 dollars [invoice 1].
- The desktop workstation was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics, at 2,742 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file does not contain the policy schedule. It also does not state the date of the loss or whether the equipment was in the insured's custody at the client site.

## 3. Coverage analysis

**Operative clauses**

- Clause 4.7 covers "damage to contents caused by water escaping from any fixed pipe, tank or appliance" [policy, cl. 4.7]. The reported cause is a burst pipe, and the damaged items are business equipment [notice of loss; incident report]. Clause 4.7 does not itself restrict cover to named premises.
- Clause 4.2 also lists escape of water as a cause of loss. It covers business contents at the scheduled premises and at other premises while in the insured's custody [policy, cl. 4.2]. The schedule and custody position are not in the file, so I rely on clause 4.7 as the primary basis.

**Other cover clauses considered**

- Clause 4.5 (transit) does not apply. The file does not describe the equipment being carried between premises [policy, cl. 4.5; incident report].
- Clause 4.9 (electrical surge) does not apply. The cause reported is water, not a surge [policy, cl. 4.9; notice of loss].

**Exclusions**

- Clause 7.1 (unattended property) concerns theft of portable equipment [policy, cl. 7.1]. No theft is reported, so it does not apply [notice of loss; incident report].
- Clause 7.3 (wear and tear) excludes gradual deterioration [policy, cl. 7.3]. The cause reported is a burst pipe, and nothing in the file describes gradual deterioration, so it does not apply [notice of loss; incident report].
- Clause 7.5 (unproven loss) applies where theft is alleged and not reported to police within seven days [policy, cl. 7.5]. Theft is not alleged, so it does not apply [notice of loss; incident report].

## 4. Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.7 (escape of water) [policy, cl. 4.7], read with the escape of water peril in clause 4.2 [policy, cl. 4.2]. Please confirm the date of loss and the custody position at the Kilburn site for completeness. Neither point alters the clause 4.7 conclusion on the current file.

## 5. Reserve

Under clause 9.1 the reserve is the sum of the invoices, less the 250 dollar excess where the schedule applies one [policy, cl. 9.1].

- Invoice 1, two monitors: 4,244 dollars [invoice 1]
- Invoice 2, desktop workstation: 2,742 dollars [invoice 2]
- Total of invoices: **6,986 dollars**

The policy schedule is not in the file, so no excess is shown. Under clause 9.1 none applies where the schedule shows none [policy, cl. 9.1]. **The reserve on the file as it stands is therefore 6,986 dollars.** If the schedule is later found to apply the 250 dollar excess, the reserve becomes 6,736 dollars.
