# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd
**Policy:** CC-1703
**Police reference:** CR/2026/4247

## Summary

Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd claims under Commercial Contents Policy CC-1703 for the theft of a laptop and an external drive from a locked storage unit at a university library. The loss was reported to us on 26 September 2026 and to the police on 28 September 2026. The theft falls within clause 4.2 and is not excluded by clause 7.1 or 7.3. Clause 7.5 cannot be fully closed out because the file does not state the date of loss. The recommended decision is covered under clause 4.2, subject to confirming that date, with a reserve of 4,732 dollars.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of or damage to business contents caused by theft, at the premises named in the schedule and at any other premises while in the insured's custody [policy cl. 4.2]. The cause of loss is theft [notice of loss] [police report]. The items were in the insured's locked container at a library [police report]. I have treated them as in the insured's custody for the purposes of clause 4.2. The schedule is not in the file, so the named premises cannot be checked.

**Other cover clauses.**
- Clause 4.5 (transit) covers accidental loss while portable equipment is carried by an employee between premises [policy cl. 4.5]. The file describes theft from a storage unit, not loss in transit, so this clause is not relied on.
- Clause 4.7 (escape of water) and clause 4.9 (electrical surge) do not apply, because no water or surge event is reported [policy cl. 4.7] [policy cl. 4.9] [notice of loss].

**Exclusions.**
- **Clause 7.1 (unattended property).** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room at the time [policy cl. 7.1]. A university library may be a shared or public space. However, both the notice of loss and the police report record that the items were in a locked storage unit or container [notice of loss] [police report]. The exception applies, so the exclusion does not operate.
- **Clause 7.3 (wear and tear).** This excludes gradual deterioration [policy cl. 7.3]. The loss is a theft and no deterioration is reported [notice of loss] [police report]. The exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.5 (unproven loss).** This excludes a loss not reported to the police within seven days where theft is alleged [policy cl. 7.5]. The police report is dated 28 September 2026 [police report]. The notice of loss is dated 26 September 2026, so the loss occurred on or before that date [notice of loss]. The police report is within seven days if the loss occurred on or after 21 September 2026. The file does not state the date of loss, so this must be confirmed with the insured before the exclusion is treated as closed.

## Decision

**Covered** under clause 4.2 (contents, theft) [policy cl. 4.2]. Clauses 7.1 and 7.3 do not apply, for the reasons above. This decision is subject to confirming the date of loss, to close out clause 7.5, and to confirming that the library falls within clause 4.2 against the policy schedule.
