# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Zephyr Point Media Ltd
**Policy:** CC-1481

## Summary

Zephyr Point Media Ltd reported the theft of a 3D printer and filament stock from a locked van at a shared workshop on Tanner Row. The loss was reported to the insurer on 20 April 2026 and to the police on 22 April 2026. The claim falls within the contents cover at clause 4.2, and the locked-container exception to exclusion 7.1 applies. The date of loss is not stated in the file, so the seven-day police-reporting condition in exclusion 7.5 needs one confirmation (see Coverage analysis). The decision is covered under clause 4.2, with a reserve of 6,264 dollars.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of or damage to business contents at the scheduled premises, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft. [policy, clause 4.2] The loss is theft of business contents (a printer and filament stock) from a van at a shared workshop on Tanner Row. [notice of loss] [police report] The schedule is not in the file, so I have not confirmed whether Tanner Row is a named premises. If it is not, cover depends on the items having been in the insured's custody there. The insured's own reports of the theft are consistent with this, but the schedule should be checked.

**Exclusion 7.1, unattended property.** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room at the time. [policy, clause 7.1] The workshop is shared. [notice of loss] The exclusion does not apply, however, because the notice of loss records a locked van and the police report records that the items were in a locked container. [notice of loss] [police report] The exception in clause 7.1 is met.

**Exclusion 7.3, wear and tear.** This excludes gradual deterioration. [policy, clause 7.3] The loss is a theft, not deterioration, so the exclusion does not apply. [notice of loss]

**Exclusion 7.5, unproven loss.** This excludes a loss not reported to the police within seven days where theft is alleged. [policy, clause 7.5] The police report is dated 22 April 2026, two days after the notice of loss of 20 April 2026. [police report] [notice of loss] The loss must therefore have occurred on or before 20 April 2026. The file does not give the date of loss, so the seven-day period cannot be fully checked. The exclusion would apply only if the theft occurred before 15 April 2026. The date of loss should be confirmed with the insured before payment.

**Other clauses.** Clause 4.5 (transit) is not relied on, because the file does not say the equipment was being carried by an employee between premises. [policy, clause 4.5] [notice of loss] Clauses 4.7 (escape of water) and 4.9 (electrical surge) do not apply, because the loss is theft and neither water nor an electrical surge is reported. [policy, clauses 4.7 and 4.9] [notice of loss]

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (contents, theft). [policy, clause 4.2] Exclusion 7.1 does not bar the claim because of its locked-container exception. [policy, clause 7.1] [police report] The decision is subject to confirming that the date of loss falls within seven days before the police report, for the purposes of clause 7.5. [policy, clause 7.5]
