# Claims Assessment Memo

## Summary

Alder Mews Architects (policy CC-1518) reported on 25 June 2026 the accidental drop of a tablet and a drawing display during transport at a hotel meeting room in Leeds. No third party was involved. The loss falls within the transit cover at clause 4.5, and no exclusion applies. The claim is covered, with a reserve of 3,642 dollars.

- The location is a hotel meeting room in Leeds. [notice of loss] [incident report]

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.5 covers "accidental loss of or damage to portable equipment while being carried by an employee between premises." [policy, cl. 4.5] A tablet and a drawing display are portable equipment, and the loss is described as an accidental drop during transport. [notice of loss] [incident report] The loss therefore falls within clause 4.5. The file does not name the carrier or the premises travelled between, so the employee and between-premises elements rest on the description "during transport." These two points should be confirmed with the insured.

**Other cover clauses.**
- Clause 4.2 responds only to theft, fire, storm or escape of water. [policy, cl. 4.2] None is reported. [notice of loss] [incident report]
- Clause 4.7 requires water escaping from a fixed pipe, tank or appliance. [policy, cl. 4.7] No water damage is reported. [incident report]
- Clause 4.9 requires an electrical surge. [policy, cl. 4.9] No surge is reported. [incident report]

None of these clauses is engaged, and the claim rests on clause 4.5 alone.

**Exclusions.**
- Clause 7.1 excludes theft of unattended portable equipment in a shared or public space. [policy, cl. 7.1] The loss is an accidental drop, and no theft is reported. [notice of loss] [incident report] The exclusion does not apply.
- Clause 7.3 excludes gradual deterioration. [policy, cl. 7.3] The loss is a single accidental drop, not a gradual process. [incident report] The exclusion does not apply.
- Clause 7.5 excludes a loss not reported to the police within seven days, where theft is alleged. [policy, cl. 7.5] No theft is alleged. [notice of loss] [incident report] The exclusion does not apply, and no police report is required.

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.5 (Transit). [policy, cl. 4.5] The decision is subject to confirmation that an employee was carrying the items between premises. No exclusion in clauses 7.1, 7.3 or 7.5 applies.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less the 250 dollar excess where the policy schedule applies one. Where no excess is shown in the schedule, none applies. [policy, cl. 9.1]

| Item | Source | Amount (dollars) |

| Drawing display | [invoice 2] | 1,624 |
| **Sum of invoices** | | **3,642** |
| Excess | no schedule in file | 0 |
| **Reserve** | | **3,642** |

The file contains no policy schedule, so no excess is shown and none is applied. If the schedule is later obtained and shows an excess, the reserve should be reduced under clause 9.1. At the 250 dollar figure named in the clause, the reserve would be 3,392 dollars.
