# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Alder Mews Architects | **Policy:** CC-1518 | **Incident report ref:** IR/2026/4182

## Summary

Alder Mews Architects reported on 25 June 2026 that a tablet and a drawing display were accidentally dropped during transport at a hotel meeting room in Leeds [notice of loss]. No third party was involved [incident report]. The claim is assessed under the transit cover in clause 4.5 and is covered. The reserve is 3,642 dollars.

## Facts

- Alder Mews Architects reported the loss under policy CC-1518 on 25 June 2026 [notice of loss].
- The loss is an accidental drop during transport of a tablet and a drawing display [notice of loss].
- The location is a hotel meeting room in Leeds [notice of loss] [incident report].
- The incident report is reference IR/2026/4182, dated 27 June 2026, and was made by Alder Mews Architects [incident report].
- The incident report lists the items as a tablet and a drawing display. It states the circumstances as an accidental drop during transport and that no third party was involved [incident report].
- The tablet was supplied by Beacon Office Supply at 2,018 dollars [invoice 1].
- The drawing display was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 1,624 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file does not state the date of the drop, who was carrying the items, or the premises travelled between. It contains no policy schedule.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.5 covers "accidental loss of or damage to portable equipment while being carried by an employee between premises" [policy, 4.5]. A tablet and a drawing display are portable equipment, and the loss is an accidental drop during transport [notice of loss] [incident report]. The loss falls within clause 4.5, subject to the point under Decision about the carrier and the premises.

**Other cover clauses.**
- Clause 4.2 is limited to theft, fire, storm or escape of water [policy, 4.2]. None is reported [incident report].
- Clause 4.7 covers escape of water [policy, 4.7]. No water damage is reported [incident report].
- Clause 4.9 covers electrical surge [policy, 4.9]. No surge is reported [incident report].

None of these clauses is relied on.

**Exclusions.**
- **7.1 Unattended property.** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space [policy, 7.1]. The loss is an accidental drop and no theft is reported [notice of loss] [incident report]. The exclusion does not apply.
- **7.3 Wear and tear.** This excludes gradual deterioration [policy, 7.3]. The reported cause is a single accidental drop, not gradual deterioration [incident report]. The exclusion does not apply.
- **7.5 Unproven loss.** This applies only where theft is alleged and not reported to police within seven days [policy, 7.5]. No theft is alleged and no third party is involved [incident report]. The exclusion does not apply.

## Decision

**Covered** under clause 4.5 (Transit) [policy, 4.5].

The file describes the loss as an accidental drop during transport [notice of loss] [incident report], but does not expressly state that an employee was carrying the items between premises. The decision rests on the reported circumstances. That detail, and the date of the drop, should be added to the file.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices less any excess shown in the policy schedule [policy, 9.1].

| Item | Amount (dollars) |
|---|---|
| Tablet [invoice 1] | 2,018 |
| Drawing display [invoice 2] | 1,624 |
| **Total of invoices** | **3,642** |

No policy schedule is in the file, so no excess is shown. Under clause 9.1, none applies [policy, 9.1].

**Reserve: 3,642 dollars** [invoice 1] [invoice 2].
