# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Dovecote Learning Ltd | **Policy:** CC-1629 | **Police ref:** CR/2026/4221

## Summary

Dovecote Learning Ltd reported on 16 April 2026 the overnight theft of a soldering station and an oscilloscope from a locked office at a rented studio on Pike Street. The theft was reported to police on 18 April 2026. The loss falls within the theft cover in clause 4.2, and none of the exclusions that could apply defeats it. The file does not state the date of the theft, so clause 7.5 needs one confirmation before payment. The claim is assessed as covered, with a reserve of 5,876 dollars.

## Facts

1. The insured is Dovecote Learning Ltd, under policy CC-1629 [notice of loss].
2. The loss was reported on 16 April 2026 [notice of loss].
3. The loss was theft of a soldering station and an oscilloscope from a locked office overnight [notice of loss].
4. The location was a rented studio on Pike Street [notice of loss] [police report].

8. The oscilloscope was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 1,047 dollars [invoice 2].

**Gaps in the file:** The file contains no policy schedule, so it does not show the named premises or any excess. The date of the theft is not stated; the file gives only the date the loss was reported and the date of the police report.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody. The loss was theft of business equipment from a studio the insured rents [notice of loss] [police report]. The schedule is not in the file, so I cannot confirm whether the studio is a named premises. The insured rents the studio and held the items in a locked office there [notice of loss], so the "other premises while in the insured's custody" limb of 4.2 also applies.

**Other cover clauses.** Clauses 4.5 (transit), 4.7 (escape of water) and 4.9 (electrical surge) are not engaged. The loss is theft, not loss in transit, water damage or surge damage [notice of loss].

**Exclusions**

- **Clause 7.1 (unattended property):** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room. The theft was from a locked office, and the items were in a locked container [notice of loss] [police report]. Even if the studio were a shared space, the clause's exception for a locked container or locked room applies. The exclusion does not operate.
- **Clause 7.3 (wear and tear):** This excludes gradual deterioration. The loss is a theft, and nothing in the file describes deterioration [notice of loss]. The exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.5 (unproven loss):** This excludes a theft not reported to the police within seven days. The police report is dated 18 April 2026, two days after the insured notified us on 16 April 2026 [police report] [notice of loss]. The theft cannot have happened after 16 April, but the file does not give its date. The clause is satisfied if the theft occurred within the seven days before 18 April. **Action:** confirm the date of the theft with the insured before payment.

## Decision

**Covered** under clause 4.2 (Contents, theft). Clauses 7.1 and 7.3 do not apply. Clause 7.5 is expected to be satisfied, subject to confirming the theft date as noted above.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices less any excess shown in the schedule.

- Soldering station: 4,829 dollars [invoice 1]
- Oscilloscope: 1,047 dollars [invoice 2]
- Sum of invoices: 5,876 dollars
- Excess: none shown, because the schedule is not in the file, so none is applied under clause 9.1

**Reserve: 5,876 dollars.** If the schedule, once obtained, shows the 250 dollar excess, the reserve reduces to 5,626 dollars.
