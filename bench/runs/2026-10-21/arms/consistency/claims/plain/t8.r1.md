# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Elmhurst Clinics LLP
**Policy:** CC-1666

## Summary

Elmhurst Clinics LLP reported fire damage to a label printer and a barcode scanner. The damage occurred in a neighbouring unit at a storage unit on Mill Road. The loss was reported on 21 June 2026. The loss falls within the fire cover in clause 4.2 and no exclusion applies, so I recommend the claim be accepted. Acceptance is subject to confirming that the Mill Road unit is a scheduled premises or was in the insured's custody, because the schedule is not in the file. The reserve is 3,254 dollars.

## Facts

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of or damage to business contents at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water. [policy, cl. 4.2] The printer and scanner are business contents, and the cause reported is fire. [notice of loss] [incident report] The cause of loss therefore falls within clause 4.2.

**Location.** Clause 4.2 requires the contents to be at a scheduled premises or at other premises in the insured's custody. [policy, cl. 4.2] The file places the items at a storage unit on Mill Road. [notice of loss] Without the schedule, I cannot confirm that this unit is named in it or was in the insured's custody. This point must be confirmed before payment.

**Other cover clauses.** None of these applies on the facts reported:
- Clause 4.5 (Transit) applies to portable equipment carried by an employee between premises. The file reports no carrying or transit. [policy, cl. 4.5] [notice of loss]
- Clause 4.7 (Escape of water) applies to water escaping from a fixed pipe, tank or appliance. The cause reported is fire. [policy, cl. 4.7] [incident report]
- Clause 4.9 (Electrical surge) applies to a surge in the electrical supply. The cause reported is fire. [policy, cl. 4.9] [incident report]

**Exclusions.**
- Clause 7.1 (Unattended property) excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space. No theft is reported; the loss is fire damage. The exclusion does not apply. [policy, cl. 7.1] [notice of loss]
- Clause 7.3 (Wear and tear) excludes gradual deterioration. The loss reported is fire damage, and the file records no deterioration. The exclusion does not apply. [policy, cl. 7.3] [incident report]
- Clause 7.5 (Unproven loss) excludes a loss not reported to the police within seven days where theft is alleged. No theft is alleged, so the police-reporting requirement is not engaged. The exclusion does not apply. [policy, cl. 7.5] [notice of loss] [incident report]

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (Contents, fire). [policy, cl. 4.2] This is subject to confirming that the Mill Road storage unit is a scheduled premises or was in the insured's custody at the time of the fire.

## Reserve
