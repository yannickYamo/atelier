# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Elmhurst Clinics LLP
**Policy:** CC-1666
**Incident report ref:** IR/2026/4234

## Summary

Elmhurst Clinics LLP reported fire damage to a label printer and a barcode scanner, which were in a storage unit on Mill Road. The fire was in a neighbouring unit. No third party is involved. The loss falls within the fire cover in clause 4.2 of the policy, and none of the exclusions in section 7 applies. I recommend the claim be accepted, with one confirmation outstanding on premises (see Coverage analysis). The reserve is 3,254 dollars.

## Facts

1. Elmhurst Clinics LLP reported the loss on 21 June 2026 under policy CC-1666 [notice of loss].
2. The loss is fire damage, in a neighbouring unit, of a label printer and a barcode scanner [notice of loss].
3. The location is a storage unit on Mill Road [notice of loss].
4. An incident report dated 23 June 2026, reference IR/2026/4234, was made by Elmhurst Clinics LLP. It lists the items as a label printer and a barcode scanner [incident report].
5. The incident report describes the circumstances as fire damage in a neighbouring unit at a storage unit on Mill Road, with no third party involved [incident report].
6. The label printer was supplied by Beacon Office Supply at 1,666 dollars [invoice 1].
7. The barcode scanner was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 1,588 dollars [invoice 2].
8. The file does not state the date of the fire. It contains no policy schedule and no police report.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of or damage to business contents at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, when caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water [policy, cl. 4.2]. The label printer and barcode scanner are business contents, and the cause of loss is fire [notice of loss; incident report]. The loss therefore falls within the fire peril in clause 4.2.

**Premises.** The file places the items in a storage unit on Mill Road [notice of loss; incident report]. The schedule is not in the file, so I cannot confirm from the documents whether that unit is named in it. Clause 4.2 also extends to other premises in the insured's custody. The schedule or the insured's custody of the unit should be confirmed before payment.

**Other cover clauses.** Clauses 4.5 (transit), 4.7 (escape of water) and 4.9 (electrical surge) are not engaged. The file records no carrying of equipment between premises, no escaping water and no electrical surge [notice of loss; incident report].

**Exclusions.**
- **Clause 7.1 (unattended property).** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space. The loss is fire damage, not theft [notice of loss], so the exclusion does not apply [policy, cl. 7.1].
- **Clause 7.3 (wear and tear).** This excludes gradual deterioration. The loss is attributed to a fire, which is a single event, and not to gradual deterioration [incident report]. The exclusion does not apply [policy, cl. 7.3].
- **Clause 7.5 (unproven loss).** This applies where theft is alleged and the loss was not reported to the police within seven days. No theft is alleged [notice of loss; incident report], so the exclusion does not apply [policy, cl. 7.5].

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (Contents, fire) [policy, cl. 4.2], subject to confirmation that the Mill Road storage unit is a scheduled premises or was in the insured's custody. No exclusion in clauses 7.1, 7.3 or 7.5 applies.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less the 250 dollar excess where the schedule applies one. Where the schedule shows no excess, none applies [policy, cl. 9.1].

| Item | Amount (dollars) |
|---|---|
| Label printer [invoice 1] | 1,666 |
| Barcode scanner [invoice 2] | 1,588 |
| **Sum of invoices** | **3,254** |
| Excess | 0 |
| **Reserve** | **3,254** |

No schedule is in the file, so no excess is shown and none is applied. If the schedule, once obtained, shows an excess, the reserve should be reduced under clause 9.1. If it shows the 250 dollar excess, the reserve would be 3,004 dollars.
