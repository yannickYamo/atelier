## Summary

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc (policy CC-1592): power surge after a storm damaged a laptop and a phone at a conference venue in Bristol. The claim falls to be considered under clause 4.9, but the file does not show that the equipment was connected through a surge protector, so the decision is held pending that fact. If cover is confirmed, the reserve is 6,598 dollars.

## Facts

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc reported the loss on 11 March 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss was a power surge after a storm [notice of loss], [incident report]. It occurred at a conference venue in Bristol [notice of loss], [incident report]. The items are a laptop and a phone [incident report], [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The incident was recorded on 13 March 2026 under reference IR/2026/4208 [incident report]. No third party was involved [incident report]. The file does not state whether the equipment was connected through a surge protector [incident report], [notice of loss]. The file contains no policy schedule [policy].

## Coverage analysis

The loss is a surge in the electrical supply damaging electronic equipment. Clause 4.9 covers this only where the equipment was connected through a surge protector [policy]. That condition is not addressed in the notice of loss or the incident report, so it is not established.

Clause 4.2 also lists storm as a cause of loss. It applies at the scheduled premises or at other premises while the property is in the insured's custody [policy]. The file does not name the scheduled premises, and it does not state that the equipment was in the insured's custody at the venue [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy]. Clause 4.2 therefore cannot be relied on either.

Exclusions:
- Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy].
- Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is described as a single event, a surge after a storm, not gradual deterioration [incident report], [policy].
- Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: it operates only where theft is alleged, and none is [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Cover cannot be confirmed on the file as it stands. The claim could be accepted under clause 4.9 if the insured confirms that both items were connected through a surge protector. Without that confirmation, clause 4.9 is not met. The insured should be asked for this, and the decision made once the answer is on file. No exclusion stands in the way.

## Reserve

If cover is confirmed, the reserve is 6,598 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (3,892 dollars and 2,706 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The file shows no excess, and clause 9.1 applies none where the schedule shows none [policy]. The schedule itself is not in the file, so this should be checked when it is added.
