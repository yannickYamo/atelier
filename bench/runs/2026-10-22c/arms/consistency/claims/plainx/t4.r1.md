## Summary

Alder Mews Architects (policy CC-1518): accidental drop during transport of a tablet and a drawing display at a hotel meeting room in Leeds. Covered under clause 4.5, subject to two points the file does not record (see Coverage analysis). Reserve is 3,642 dollars before any excess; the policy schedule is not in the file.

## Facts

Alder Mews Architects reported the loss on 25 June 2026 under policy CC-1518 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a hotel meeting room in Leeds [notice of loss]. The items are a tablet and a drawing display [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The incident was recorded on 27 June 2026 under reference IR/2026/4182 [incident report]. The circumstances recorded are an accidental drop during transport, with no third party involved [incident report]. The tablet is invoiced at 2,018 dollars [invoice 1] and the drawing display at 1,624 dollars [invoice 2]. The file does not state the date of the drop, who was carrying the items, or the premises they were being carried between [notice of loss], [incident report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.5 of the policy, which covers accidental loss of or damage to portable equipment while being carried by an employee between premises [policy]. A tablet and a drawing display are portable equipment, and the drop is recorded as accidental and occurring during transport [incident report]. The file does not record that the items were being carried by an employee or between premises, both of which clause 4.5 requires [policy]. These two points should be confirmed with the insured before payment.

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: it concerns theft of unattended portable equipment, and no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: it concerns gradual deterioration, and the loss is recorded as a single accidental drop [incident report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: it applies only where theft is alleged, and no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.5. The claim is accepted, subject to confirmation that the items were carried by an employee between premises.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1 the reserve is the sum of the invoices, less the 250 dollar excess where the policy schedule applies one [policy]. The invoices total 3,642 dollars (2,018 dollars and 1,624 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The policy schedule is not in the file, so it cannot be confirmed whether an excess applies [policy]. The reserve is therefore set at 3,642 dollars. If the schedule shows the 250 dollar excess, the reserve becomes 3,392 dollars.
