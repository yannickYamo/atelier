## Summary

Vellum Press Inc (policy CC-1370): theft of a laptop and docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 4,030 dollars. The date of the theft is not stated in the file, so it should be confirmed to close out exclusion 7.5.

## Facts

Vellum Press Inc reported the loss on 5 June 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss is theft from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street [notice of loss]. The items are a laptop and a docking station [police report], [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 7 June 2026 under reference CR/2026/4130 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report]. The laptop is invoiced at 2,370 dollars [invoice 1] and the docking station at 1,660 dollars [invoice 2]. The file does not state the date on which the theft occurred.

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of business contents caused by theft at any premises while in the insured's custody [policy]. The items were at a co-working space and in a locked locker [notice of loss], [police report].

- **Exclusion 7.1 (unattended property)** does not apply. It excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared space unless it was in a locked container or locked room, and the items were in a locked container [police report], [policy].
- **Exclusion 7.3 (wear and tear)** does not apply. The loss is a theft, and no gradual deterioration is claimed [notice of loss], [policy].
- **Exclusion 7.5 (unproven loss)** requires a police report within seven days where theft is alleged [policy]. The police report is dated 7 June 2026, two days after the notice of loss dated 5 June 2026 [police report], [notice of loss]. The file gives no date of theft, so the seven-day period cannot be measured from the documents. Nothing in the file indicates a delay. The date of theft should be confirmed with the insured and noted on the file.
- Clauses 4.5, 4.7 and 4.9 do not apply, as the loss is neither transit damage, escape of water nor an electrical surge [notice of loss], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted, subject to the date of theft being confirmed as within seven days before the police report of 7 June 2026 (exclusion 7.5).

## Reserve

The reserve is 4,030 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (2,370 dollars and 1,660 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The policy extract in the file shows no schedule and no excess, and under clause 9.1 none applies where the schedule shows none [policy]. No deduction is made.
