## Summary

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc (policy CC-1592): power surge after a storm damaged a laptop and a phone at a conference venue in Bristol. Cover under clause 4.9 depends on a fact the file does not record, namely whether the equipment was connected through a surge protector. The decision is held pending that confirmation. Provisional reserve is 6,598 dollars.

## Facts

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc reported the loss on 11 March 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss is described as a power surge after a storm affecting a laptop and a phone [notice of loss]. It occurred at a conference venue in Bristol [notice of loss]. The incident report, reference IR/2026/4208, is dated 13 March 2026 and records a power surge after a storm at that venue, with no third party involved [incident report]. The items are a laptop (3,892 dollars) and a phone (2,706 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. No document in the file states whether the equipment was connected through a surge protector. No document in the file states that the items were in the insured's custody at the venue. The file contains no policy schedule [policy].

## Coverage analysis

Clause 4.9 covers damage to electronic equipment caused by a surge in the electrical supply, where the equipment was connected through a surge protector [policy]. A laptop and a phone are electronic equipment, and the loss is described as a surge [notice of loss], [incident report]. The surge protector condition is not evidenced in the file, so clause 4.9 cannot yet be confirmed.

Clause 4.2 names storm as a peril at other premises while the contents are in the insured's custody [policy]. The file records the surge as following a storm [incident report] but does not state custody or that the storm directly caused the damage. I do not rely on clause 4.2.

Exclusions:
- Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: it concerns theft of unattended portable equipment, and no theft is alleged [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy].
- Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is described as a single event after a storm, not gradual deterioration [incident report], [policy].
- Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: it applies only where theft is alleged, and none is [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Not yet determinable. The claim falls to be covered under clause 4.9 if the insured confirms, with supporting detail, that the laptop and phone were connected through a surge protector at the time. If that is not the case, clause 4.9 does not respond and clause 4.2 would need to be considered on custody and causation. The insured should be asked for this information before the claim is accepted or declined.

## Reserve

The provisional reserve is 6,598 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (3,892 dollars and 2,706 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. Clause 9.1 applies an excess only where the schedule shows one, and none applies where none is shown [policy]. No schedule is in the file and the policy extract shows no excess, so none is deducted. The schedule should be checked to confirm this.
