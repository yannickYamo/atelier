## Summary

Bramble Hill Dental: theft from an unattended bag of a projector and a sound bar at the insured's van, parked overnight. Declined under exclusion 7.1.

## Facts

Bramble Hill Dental reported the loss on 6 September 2026 under policy CC-1555 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at the insured's van, parked overnight [notice of loss]. The items are a projector and a sound bar [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 8 September 2026, reference CR/2026/4195 [police report]. The items were taken from an unattended bag, left in a shared area, and were not in a locked container [police report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy]. 

Exclusion 7.1 applies: the items were portable equipment left unattended in a shared area and were not in a locked container or locked room [police report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 is addressed as follows. The file gives the police report date (8 September 2026) and the date the loss was reported to us (6 September 2026), but it does not state the date of the theft itself [police report], [notice of loss]. The seven-day test in the exclusion therefore cannot be confirmed from the file [policy]. This does not affect the outcome, because exclusion 7.1 applies on its own.

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is a theft, not gradual deterioration [police report], [policy].

## Decision

Not covered: exclusion 7.1 applies. The claim is declined, and the insured is told why in the terms of the policy.

## Reserve

No reserve is set: the claim is declined [policy].
