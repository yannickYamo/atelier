## Summary

Elmhurst Clinics LLP (policy CC-1666): fire damage in a neighbouring unit to a label printer and a barcode scanner at a storage unit on Mill Road. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 3,254 dollars.

## Facts

Elmhurst Clinics LLP reported the loss on 21 June 2026 under policy CC-1666 [notice of loss]. The loss was fire damage in a neighbouring unit [notice of loss], [incident report]. It occurred at a storage unit on Mill Road [notice of loss], [incident report]. The items are a label printer and a barcode scanner [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The incident report, reference IR/2026/4234, is dated 23 June 2026 and records that no third party was involved [incident report]. The label printer was invoiced at 1,666 dollars [invoice 1] and the barcode scanner at 1,588 dollars [invoice 2]. The file does not include the policy schedule [policy].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water [policy]. The cause of loss is fire [notice of loss], [incident report]. The schedule is not in the file, so the premises or custody condition of clause 4.2 should be confirmed against it before payment [policy].

- Exclusion 7.1 does not apply. It concerns theft of unattended portable equipment, and no theft is alleged [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy].
- Exclusion 7.3 does not apply. It concerns gradual deterioration, and the loss is reported as fire damage [notice of loss], [policy].
- Exclusion 7.5 does not apply. It operates only where theft is alleged, and no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy].

Clauses 4.5, 4.7 and 4.9 are not engaged: the loss was not in transit, was not caused by escaping water, and was not caused by an electrical surge [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted, subject to the premises or custody confirmation noted above.

## Reserve

The reserve is 3,254 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (1,666 dollars and 1,588 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. No excess is shown in the file, so none applies under clause 9.1 [policy].
