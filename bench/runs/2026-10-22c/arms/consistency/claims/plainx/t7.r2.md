## Summary

Dovecote Learning Ltd (policy CC-1629): theft overnight from a locked office of a soldering station and an oscilloscope at a rented studio on Pike Street. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 5,876 dollars.

## Facts

Dovecote Learning Ltd reported the loss on 16 April 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss was theft from a locked office overnight [notice of loss]. It occurred at a rented studio on Pike Street [notice of loss]. The items are a soldering station [invoice 1] and an oscilloscope [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 18 April 2026 under reference CR/2026/4221 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report]. The soldering station is invoiced at 4,829 dollars [invoice 1] and the oscilloscope at 1,047 dollars [invoice 2]. The file does not state the date of the theft, and it contains no policy schedule [notice of loss], [police report], [policy].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at the scheduled premises, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy]. The loss is theft of business equipment from a studio and office [notice of loss], [police report]. The schedule is not in the file, so cover at this location rests on the premises being in the insured's custody.

- **Exclusion 7.1 (unattended property)** does not apply. It removes cover for theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space unless it was in a locked container or locked room. The items were in a locked container, within a locked office [police report], [policy].
- **Exclusion 7.5 (unproven loss)** does not apply on the file as it stands. It removes cover where theft is alleged and the loss was not reported to the police within seven days [policy]. The police report is dated 18 April 2026, two days after the notice of loss dated 16 April 2026 [police report], [notice of loss]. Because the date of the theft is not in the file, the seven-day period cannot be measured directly. The test is met if the theft took place on or after 11 April 2026. I recommend confirming the date of the theft for the file.
- **Exclusion 7.3 (wear and tear)** does not apply. The loss is theft, not gradual deterioration [notice of loss], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted, subject to the date of the theft being confirmed for exclusion 7.5.

## Reserve

The reserve is 5,876 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (4,829 dollars and 1,047 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. No excess is shown in the file, and under clause 9.1 none applies where no excess is shown in the schedule [policy].
