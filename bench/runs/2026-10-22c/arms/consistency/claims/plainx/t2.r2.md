## Summary

Yewtree Biotech AS (policy CC-1444): water damage from a burst pipe to two monitors and a desktop workstation at a client site in Kilburn. Covered under clause 4.7. The reserve is 6,986 dollars before any excess, subject to the schedule point noted in the Reserve section.

## Facts

Yewtree Biotech AS reported the loss on 15 March 2026 under policy CC-1444 [notice of loss]. The loss was water damage from a burst pipe [notice of loss], [incident report]. It occurred at a client site in Kilburn [notice of loss], [incident report]. The items are two monitors and a desktop workstation [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The incident was recorded on 17 March 2026 under reference IR/2026/4156 [incident report]. No third party was involved [incident report]. The monitors were invoiced at 4,244 dollars [invoice 1] and the workstation at 2,742 dollars [invoice 2].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.7 of the policy, which covers damage to contents caused by water escaping from any fixed pipe, tank or appliance [policy]. The file describes a burst pipe at a client site [incident report]. It does not say in terms that the pipe was fixed, so I have treated it as a fixed pipe on that description.

Clause 4.2 also lists escape of water, but only for premises named in the schedule or other premises in the insured's custody [policy]. The file does not show whether the client site was in the insured's custody, so I do not rely on clause 4.2. Clause 4.7 has no premises condition [policy].

- **Exclusion 7.1 (unattended property)** does not apply. It concerns theft of portable equipment, and no theft is alleged [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy].
- **Exclusion 7.3 (wear and tear)** does not apply. It concerns gradual deterioration, and the file records the cause as a burst pipe, not gradual deterioration [incident report], [policy].
- **Exclusion 7.5 (unproven loss)** does not apply. It applies only where theft is alleged, and no theft is alleged [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.7. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices less any excess that the policy schedule applies [policy]. The sum of the two invoices is 6,986 dollars (4,244 dollars and 2,742 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2].

The policy schedule is not in the file, and the extract does not show an excess. On the file as it stands, the reserve is 6,986 dollars. If the schedule shows the 250 dollar excess referred to in clause 9.1, the reserve becomes 6,736 dollars [policy]. The schedule should be checked before the reserve is finalised.
