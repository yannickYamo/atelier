## Summary

Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd (policy CC-1703): theft from a locked storage unit of a laptop and an external drive at a university library. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 4,732 dollars. The file does not state the date of the theft, so the seven-day police-report condition in exclusion 7.5 should be confirmed (see Coverage analysis).

## Facts

Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd reported the loss on 26 September 2026 [notice of loss]. The policy number is CC-1703 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a university library [notice of loss]. The items are a laptop and an external drive [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 28 September 2026, reference CR/2026/4247 [police report]. The items were in a locked storage unit, a locked container, at the time [notice of loss], [police report]. The file does not state the date on which the theft took place [notice of loss], [police report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy]. The notice of loss and police report record the items as those of the insured, held in a locked storage unit [notice of loss], [police report].

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container at the time [police report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is a theft, not gradual deterioration [police report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 is not triggered on the file as it stands. The loss was reported to the police on 28 September 2026, two days after the notice of loss of 26 September 2026 [police report], [notice of loss], so the theft cannot have occurred later than the notice. The exclusion applies where a theft is not reported to the police within seven days [policy]. Because the file gives no date of theft, that seven-day period cannot be measured directly. The date of theft should be obtained to close this point.

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted, subject to confirmation that the theft occurred within seven days before the police report of 28 September 2026, as exclusion 7.5 requires.

## Reserve

The reserve is 4,732 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (2,603 dollars and 2,129 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The policy extract in the file contains no schedule showing an excess, so under clause 9.1 none applies [policy].
