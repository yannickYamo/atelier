## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.5 of the policy, which covers accidental loss of or damage to portable equipment while being carried by an employee between premises [policy]. The file records an accidental drop during transport [incident report], which matches the accidental and transit elements of the clause. The carrier's status as an employee and the premises involved are not recorded in the file and should be confirmed. Clause 4.2 does not respond, because it covers theft, fire, storm or escape of water and not an accidental drop [policy].

- **Exclusion 7.1 (unattended property)** does not apply. It concerns theft of portable equipment left unattended, and no theft is alleged [policy], [notice of loss], [incident report].
- **Exclusion 7.3 (wear and tear)** does not apply. It concerns gradual deterioration, and the loss is recorded as an accidental drop [policy], [incident report].
- **Exclusion 7.5 (unproven loss)** does not apply. It applies only where theft is alleged, and none is [policy], [notice of loss], [incident report].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.5. The claim is accepted on the file as it stands, subject to confirming that an employee was carrying the items between premises.
