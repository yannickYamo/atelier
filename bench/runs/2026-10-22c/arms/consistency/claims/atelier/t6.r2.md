## Summary

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc: power surge after a storm of a laptop and a phone at a conference venue in Bristol. Covered under clause 4.9; reserve 6,598 dollars.

## Facts

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc reported the loss on 11 March 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a conference venue in Bristol [notice of loss]. The items are a laptop and a phone [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The incident was recorded on 13 March 2026 [incident report]. The circumstances are a power surge after a storm, with no third party involved [incident report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.9 of the policy, which covers damage to electronic equipment caused by a surge in the electrical supply [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is a surge after a storm and not gradual deterioration [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.9. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 6,598 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (3,892 dollars and 2,706 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]; the policy shows no excess in the schedule, so none applies [policy].
