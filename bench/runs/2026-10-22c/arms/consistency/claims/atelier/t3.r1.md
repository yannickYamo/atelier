## Summary

Zephyr Point Media Ltd: theft from a locked van of a 3D printer and filament stock at a shared workshop on Tanner Row. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 6,264 dollars.

## Facts

Zephyr Point Media Ltd reported the loss on 20 April 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a shared workshop on Tanner Row [notice of loss]. The items are a 3D printer and filament stock [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 22 April 2026 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents while in the insured's custody, caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container [police report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: the loss was reported to the police within seven days [police report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 6,264 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (5,181 dollars and 1,083 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]; the schedule applies no excess [policy].
