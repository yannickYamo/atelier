## Summary

Mossbank Analytics Inc: theft from an unlocked desk of camera body and lens at the insured's office on Harbour Lane. Declined under exclusion 7.1.

## Facts

The insured has made 4 earlier claims under this policy, totalling 9,350 dollars. Mossbank Analytics Inc reported the loss on 8 April 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at the insured's office on Harbour Lane [notice of loss]. The items are camera body and lens [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 10 April 2026 [police report]. The items were not in a locked container at the time [police report].

## Coverage analysis

A witness statement confirms the items were in use that day [witness statement]. The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers theft of or damage to business contents in the insured's custody [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 applies: the items were unattended in a shared space and not in a locked container [police report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: the loss was reported to the police within seven days [police report], [policy].

## Decision

Not covered: exclusion 7.1 applies. The claim is declined, and the insured is told why in the terms of the policy.

## Reserve

No reserve is set: the claim is declined.
