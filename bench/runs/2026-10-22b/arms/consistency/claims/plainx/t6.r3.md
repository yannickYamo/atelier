## Summary

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc: power surge after a storm damaged a laptop and a phone at a conference venue in Bristol. The loss falls to be considered under clause 4.9, but the file does not show that the equipment was connected through a surge protector, so coverage cannot yet be confirmed. Provisional reserve 6,598 dollars.

## Facts

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc reported the loss on 11 March 2026 under policy CC-1592 [notice of loss]. The loss is described as a power surge after a storm, affecting a laptop and a phone [notice of loss]. It occurred at a conference venue in Bristol [notice of loss]. The incident was recorded on 13 March 2026 under reference IR/2026/4208, and no third party was involved [incident report]. The items are a laptop, invoiced at 3,892 dollars by Beacon Office Supply [invoice 1], and a phone, invoiced at 2,706 dollars by Crane & Sons Electronics [invoice 2]. The file does not state whether the equipment was connected through a surge protector [notice of loss], [incident report]. The file contains no policy schedule, only an extract of the policy wording [policy].

## Coverage analysis

Clause 4.9 covers damage to electronic equipment caused by a surge in the electrical supply, where the equipment was connected through a surge protector [policy]. A surge is recorded [incident report]. The surge protector condition is not addressed in the file [notice of loss], [incident report], so it is not established.

Clause 4.2 covers damage caused by storm to business contents at the scheduled premises, or at other premises while in the insured's custody [policy]. The loss is recorded as a surge after a storm at a conference venue [incident report]. The file does not show whether the venue is a scheduled premises or whether the items were in the insured's custody there [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy]. Clause 4.2 therefore cannot be relied on either.

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: it concerns theft, and no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: it applies only where theft is alleged [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.3 does not apply on the file: the loss is recorded as a surge after a storm, not as gradual deterioration [incident report], [policy]. Clauses 4.5 and 4.7 do not fit the recorded circumstances: no carrying between premises and no escaping water are recorded [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Coverage is not confirmed on the current file. No exclusion applies, but the condition in clause 4.9 (connection through a surge protector) is not evidenced [incident report], [policy]. The claim should not be accepted or declined until the insured confirms how the equipment was connected. If a surge protector was in use, the claim is covered under clause 4.9. If the insured instead relies on clause 4.2, the file needs the premises schedule and a record of custody at the venue.

## Reserve

The provisional reserve is 6,598 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (3,892 dollars and 2,706 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. Clause 9.1 deducts a 250 dollar excess only where the schedule applies one, and none applies where the schedule shows none [policy]. The schedule is not in the file, so the absence of an excess should be confirmed against it before the reserve is finalised [policy].
