## Summary

Bramble Hill Dental (policy CC-1555): theft from an unattended bag of a projector and a sound bar at the insured's van, parked overnight. Declined under exclusion 7.1.

## Facts

Bramble Hill Dental reported the loss on 6 September 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at the insured's van, parked overnight [notice of loss]. The items are a projector (2,955 dollars) and a sound bar (2,165 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 8 September 2026 under reference CR/2026/4195 [police report]. The items were taken from an unattended bag [police report]. They were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container [police report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls within clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers theft of business contents at other premises while in the insured's custody [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 applies. It removes cover for theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space unless it was in a locked container or locked room, and the police report records an unattended bag in a shared area with no locked container [police report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 does not apply. The file gives no date for the theft itself, but the police report is dated 8 September 2026, two days after the notice of loss on 6 September 2026, so the report was made within seven days on the dates in the file [police report], [notice of loss], [policy]. The decision does not depend on this point.

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is a theft, not gradual deterioration [notice of loss], [policy].

## Decision

Not covered: exclusion 7.1 applies. The claim is declined, and the insured is told why in the terms of the policy.

## Reserve

No reserve is set: the claim is declined.
