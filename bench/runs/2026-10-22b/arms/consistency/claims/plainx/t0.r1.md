## Summary

Vellum Press Inc (policy CC-1370): theft of a laptop and docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 4,030 dollars.

## Facts

Vellum Press Inc reported the loss on 5 June 2026 under policy CC-1370 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a co-working space on Fenwick Street [notice of loss]. The items are a laptop and a docking station [notice of loss], [police report]. The theft was reported to the police on 7 June 2026, reference CR/2026/4130 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report]. The laptop was invoiced at 2,370 dollars by Beacon Office Supply [invoice 1]. The docking station was invoiced at 1,660 dollars by Crane & Sons Electronics [invoice 2]. The file contains the policy extract but not the policy schedule [policy].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy]. The co-working space is not shown in the file as a scheduled premises, so cover rests on the custody limb of the clause. The items were held in a locked locker at the space [police report]. The file does not state who held the locker key, and this should be confirmed when the schedule is added to the file.

- **Exclusion 7.1 (unattended property)** does not apply. It excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared space unless it was in a locked container or locked room, and the items were in a locked container [police report], [policy].
- **Exclusion 7.3 (wear and tear)** does not apply. The loss is a theft, not gradual deterioration [notice of loss], [policy].
- **Exclusion 7.5 (unproven loss)** does not apply on the file as it stands. The police report is dated 7 June 2026, two days after the notice of loss of 5 June 2026, so it falls within seven days [police report], [notice of loss], [policy]. The file does not state the date of the theft itself, and that date should be recorded to complete the seven-day check.

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 4,030 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (2,370 dollars and 1,660 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. Clause 9.1 deducts the 250 dollar excess only where the schedule applies one, and where no excess is shown in the schedule none applies [policy]. No schedule is in the file and no excess is shown, so none is deducted. If the schedule, once added, shows an excess, the reserve falls to 3,780 dollars.
