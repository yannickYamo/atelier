## Summary

Yewtree Biotech AS: water damage from a burst pipe of two monitors and a desktop workstation at a client site in Kilburn. Covered under clause 4.7; reserve 6,986 dollars.

## Facts

Yewtree Biotech AS reported the loss on 15 March 2026 under policy CC-1444 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a client site in Kilburn [notice of loss]. The loss is water damage from a burst pipe [notice of loss], [incident report]. The incident was recorded on 17 March 2026 under reference IR/2026/4156 [incident report]. No third party was involved [incident report]. The items are two monitors and a desktop workstation [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The monitors are invoiced at 4,244 dollars [invoice 1] and the workstation at 2,742 dollars [invoice 2].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.7 of the policy, which covers damage to contents caused by water escaping from any fixed pipe, tank or appliance [policy]. The damage is described as water damage from a burst pipe [incident report].

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: it concerns theft of unattended portable equipment, and no theft is alleged [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: it concerns gradual deterioration, and the loss is recorded as damage from a burst pipe, not deterioration [incident report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: it applies only where theft is alleged, and no theft is alleged [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.7. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 6,986 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (4,244 dollars and 2,742 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The policy schedule is not in the file, so no excess is shown; under clause 9.1, where no excess is shown in the schedule, none applies [policy]. If the schedule shows an excess of 250 dollars, the reserve would be 6,736 dollars. The schedule should be checked before the reserve is finalised.
