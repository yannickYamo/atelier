## Summary

Wayfarer Fintech Ltd (policy CC-1407): theft of a camera body and lens from an unlocked desk at the insured's office on Harbour Lane. Declined under exclusion 7.1.

## Facts

Wayfarer Fintech Ltd reported the loss on 10 September 2026 under policy CC-1407 [notice of loss]. The loss is described as theft from an unlocked desk of a camera body and lens [notice of loss]. It occurred at the insured's office on Harbour Lane [notice of loss]. The theft was reported to the police on 12 September 2026, reference CR/2026/4143 [police report]. The items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container [police report]. The items are a camera body (3,307 dollars) and a lens (2,201 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2].

## Coverage analysis

Clause 4.2 covers loss of or damage to business contents at the premises named in the schedule, or at other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy]. The loss is theft of business contents at the insured's office [notice of loss]. The schedule is not in the file, so I have not confirmed that the office is a named premises.

Exclusion 7.1 applies. It removes cover for theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless the equipment was in a locked container or locked room [policy]. The items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container [police report]. The file does not show them in a locked room.

Exclusion 7.5 is not relied on. It applies where theft is alleged and the loss was not reported to the police within seven days [policy]. The police report is dated 12 September 2026, two days after the notice of loss of 10 September 2026 [police report], [notice of loss]. The file does not state the date of the theft, so I cannot measure the seven days from the loss. The decision does not depend on this exclusion.

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: no gradual deterioration is alleged [notice of loss], [police report], [policy].

## Decision

Not covered: exclusion 7.1 applies. The claim is declined, and the insured is told why in the terms of the policy.

## Reserve

No reserve is set: the claim is declined.
