## Summary

Zephyr Point Media Ltd: theft from a locked van of a 3D printer and filament stock at a shared workshop on Tanner Row. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 6,264 dollars. Two points are open and noted below: the date of the theft, and the schedule, which is not in the file.

## Facts

Zephyr Point Media Ltd reported the loss on 20 April 2026 under policy CC-1481 [notice of loss]. The loss was theft from a locked van at a shared workshop on Tanner Row [notice of loss], [police report]. The items are a 3D printer [invoice 1] and filament stock [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 22 April 2026, reference CR/2026/4169 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report]. The file does not state the date on which the theft occurred. The policy extract is in the file, but the policy schedule is not [policy].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2, which covers loss of or damage to business contents caused by theft, at the premises named in the schedule and at any other premises while in the insured's custody [policy]. The file does not include the schedule or say whose van it was. The clause 4.2 conclusion therefore rests on the loss being reported by the insured as theft of its business contents [notice of loss], [police report], and the schedule and custody should be confirmed.

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container at the time [police report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 requires a police report within seven days where theft is alleged [policy]. The police report was made on 22 April 2026, two days after the notice of loss on 20 April 2026 [police report], [notice of loss]. Because the date of the theft is not in the file, the seven-day period cannot be measured from the loss itself. On the file as it stands, 7.5 is not shown to apply, and the date of theft should be confirmed to close this point.

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: no gradual deterioration is alleged [notice of loss], [police report], [policy].

Clauses 4.5, 4.7 and 4.9 do not apply: the loss is a theft, not transit loss, escape of water or an electrical surge [notice of loss], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted, subject to confirming the date of the theft (exclusion 7.5) and the schedule and custody position (clause 4.2).

## Reserve

The reserve is 6,264 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (5,181 dollars and 1,083 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. No excess is shown in the file, and under clause 9.1 none applies where the schedule shows none [policy]. If the schedule, once obtained, shows an excess, the reserve is reduced by 250 dollars to 6,014 dollars [policy].
