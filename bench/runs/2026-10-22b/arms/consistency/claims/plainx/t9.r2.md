## Summary

Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd (policy CC-1703): theft of a laptop and an external drive from a locked storage unit at a university library. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 4,732 dollars. Two points in the file need confirming, noted below.

## Facts

Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd reported the loss on 26 September 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a university library [notice of loss]. The items are a laptop and an external drive [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 28 September 2026, reference CR/2026/4247 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report]. The file does not state the date of the theft.

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at any premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy]. The items were in a locked storage unit [police report].

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container at the time [police report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 requires a reported theft to be reported to the police within seven days [policy]. The police report is dated 28 September 2026, two days after the notice of loss of 26 September 2026 [police report], [notice of loss]. The theft cannot have occurred after the notice of loss, but the file gives no theft date. The seven-day period therefore cannot be checked from the file alone, and the theft date should be confirmed as no earlier than seven days before 28 September 2026. On the file as it stands, I treat the exclusion as not applying.

Exclusions 7.3 (wear and tear) does not apply: the loss is alleged to be theft, not deterioration [notice of loss], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted, subject to confirmation of the theft date for exclusion 7.5.

## Reserve

The reserve is 4,732 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (2,603 dollars and 2,129 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. Clause 9.1 deducts the 250 dollar excess only where the policy schedule applies one, and none applies where the schedule shows none [policy]. The file contains no schedule and no excess is shown, so none is deducted. If the schedule, once obtained, shows an excess, the reserve falls to 4,482 dollars.
