## Summary

Vellum Press Inc: theft from a locked locker of a laptop and docking station at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 4,030 dollars.

## Facts

Vellum Press Inc reported the loss on 5 June 2026 under policy CC-1370 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a co-working space on Fenwick Street [notice of loss]. The items are a laptop and a docking station [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 7 June 2026, reference CR/2026/4130 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report]. The laptop invoice is 2,370 dollars [invoice 1] and the docking station invoice is 1,660 dollars [invoice 2].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy]. The file records the items as stored in a locked locker at the co-working space, reported as stolen by the insured [notice of loss], [police report]. The file does not state in terms that the locker was in the insured's custody. The cover under clause 4.2 rests on that point, so it should be confirmed.

- Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container at the time [police report], [policy].
- Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is a theft, and no gradual deterioration is recorded [notice of loss], [policy].
- Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: the loss was reported to the police on 7 June 2026, within seven days of the notice of loss on 5 June 2026 [police report], [notice of loss], [policy].

Clauses 4.5, 4.7 and 4.9 are not engaged: the loss is not accidental damage in transit, escape of water or an electrical surge [notice of loss], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2, subject to confirming that the locker was in the insured's custody. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 4,030 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (2,370 dollars and 1,660 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The policy schedule is not in the file, so no excess is shown. Under clause 9.1, where no excess is shown, none applies [policy]. If the schedule shows an excess, the reserve should be reduced by that amount.
