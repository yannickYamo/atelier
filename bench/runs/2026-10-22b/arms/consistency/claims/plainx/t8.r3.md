## Summary

Elmhurst Clinics LLP (policy CC-1666): fire damage in a neighbouring unit to a label printer and a barcode scanner at a storage unit on Mill Road. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 3,254 dollars.

## Facts

Elmhurst Clinics LLP reported the loss on 21 June 2026 under policy CC-1666 [notice of loss]. The loss was fire damage in a neighbouring unit [notice of loss], [incident report]. It occurred at a storage unit on Mill Road [notice of loss]. The items are a label printer and a barcode scanner [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The incident was recorded on 23 June 2026 under reference IR/2026/4234 [incident report]. No third party was involved [incident report]. The label printer was invoiced at 1,666 dollars [invoice 1] and the barcode scanner at 1,588 dollars [invoice 2].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water [policy]. The cause here is fire [notice of loss], [incident report]. The file does not include the policy schedule or a statement of who held the storage unit, so the premises or custody element should be confirmed against the schedule before payment.

Each exclusion has been considered:

- Exclusion 7.1 does not apply. It concerns theft of unattended portable equipment, and no theft is alleged [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy].
- Exclusion 7.3 does not apply. It concerns gradual deterioration, and the loss is fire damage [incident report], [policy].
- Exclusion 7.5 does not apply. It applies only where theft is alleged, and no theft is alleged [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy].

Clauses 4.5, 4.7 and 4.9 (transit, escape of water, electrical surge) are not engaged: the cause is fire, and the file shows no transit, water or surge event [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted, subject to confirming the schedule and custody point above.

## Reserve

The reserve is 3,254 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (1,666 dollars and 1,588 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. No excess is shown in the file, so none applies under clause 9.1 [policy].
