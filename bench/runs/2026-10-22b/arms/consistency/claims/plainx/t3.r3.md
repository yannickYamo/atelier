## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy]. The policy extract does not include the schedule, and the file does not state whose van it was.

Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container at the time [police report], [policy].

Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is a theft, not gradual deterioration [notice of loss], [policy].

Exclusion 7.5 requires a theft to be reported to the police within seven days [policy]. The police report is dated 22 April 2026, two days after the loss was reported to us on 20 April 2026 [police report], [notice of loss]. The file does not give the date of the theft itself, so the seven-day period cannot be measured from the file alone. The date of loss should be confirmed to close this point.

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted, subject to confirmation of the date of loss for exclusion 7.5.

## Reserve

The reserve is 6,264 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (5,181 dollars and 1,083 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. No excess is shown in the file, so none applies under clause 9.1 [policy].
