# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd | **Policy:** CC-1703 | **Police ref:** CR/2026/4247

## Summary

Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd claims under commercial contents policy CC-1703 for the theft of a laptop and an external drive from a locked storage unit at a university library. The loss was reported on 26 September 2026 and to the police on 28 September 2026. The claim falls within clause 4.2, and no exclusion removes it, provided the date of the theft is confirmed as noted below. The reserve is 4,732 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured notified the loss on 26 September 2026 under policy CC-1703. [notice of loss]
- The loss is the theft of a laptop and an external drive from a locked storage unit at a university library. [notice of loss]
- The theft was reported to the police on 28 September 2026 under reference CR/2026/4247, and the report was made by the insured. [police report]
- The police report records the items as a laptop and an external drive. [police report]
- The police report records that the items were in a locked container at the time of the theft. [police report]
- The laptop was supplied by Beacon Office Supply, at 2,603 dollars. [invoice 1]
- The external drive was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics, at 2,129 dollars. [invoice 2]
- The date of the theft is not stated in any document in the file. [notice of loss] [police report]
- The policy schedule is not in the file, so no excess is shown. [policy]

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft, at the scheduled premises and at any other premises while in the insured's custody. [policy, cl. 4.2] The loss is theft of business equipment from a locked storage unit at a university library. [notice of loss] [police report] The claim therefore falls within clause 4.2, on the basis that the items were in the insured's custody at premises other than the scheduled premises. The file has no separate statement on custody, and the schedule is not on file to confirm the named premises.

**Other cover clauses.** Clauses 4.5 (transit), 4.7 (escape of water) and 4.9 (electrical surge) do not apply. The file records theft from a storage unit, with no carriage between premises, water damage or electrical surge. [notice of loss] [police report] [policy, cl. 4.5, 4.7, 4.9]

**Exclusions.**
- **Clause 7.1 (unattended property).** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room. [policy, cl. 7.1] A university library may be a shared space, but the police report records that the items were in a locked container. [police report] The exception applies, so the exclusion does not bar the claim.
- **Clause 7.3 (wear and tear).** This excludes gradual deterioration. [policy, cl. 7.3] The loss is theft, not deterioration. [notice of loss] [police report] The exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.5 (unproven loss).** This excludes a theft not reported to the police within seven days. [policy, cl. 7.5] The police report is dated 28 September 2026, two days after the notice of loss of 26 September 2026. [police report] [notice of loss] The date of the theft is not in the file, so the seven-day period cannot be measured directly. The theft was reported to us on 26 September 2026, so it occurred on or before that date. [notice of loss] The exclusion does not apply if the theft occurred on or after 21 September 2026. The date of loss should be confirmed with the insured and recorded on the file to close this point.

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (contents: theft at other premises while in the insured's custody). [policy, cl. 4.2] This is subject to confirmation that the theft occurred within seven days before the police report, so that clause 7.5 does not apply. Clause 7.1 does not exclude the claim because of its locked-container exception. [policy, cl. 7.1] [police report]

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less the 250 dollar excess where the schedule applies one. Where no excess is shown, none applies. [policy, cl. 9.1]
