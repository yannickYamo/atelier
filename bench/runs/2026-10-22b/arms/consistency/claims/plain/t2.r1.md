# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Yewtree Biotech AS
**Policy:** CC-1444

## Summary

Yewtree Biotech AS reported on 15 March 2026 water damage from a burst pipe to two monitors and a desktop workstation at a client site in Kilburn. No third party is involved. The loss falls within clause 4.7 (escape of water), and no exclusion applies. I recommend the claim be accepted as covered, with a reserve of 6,986 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured is Yewtree Biotech AS, under policy CC-1444 [notice of loss].
- The loss was reported on 15 March 2026 [notice of loss].
- The reported loss is water damage from a burst pipe to two monitors and a desktop workstation [notice of loss].
- The location is a client site in Kilburn [notice of loss] [incident report].
- Incident report IR/2026/4156 is dated 17 March 2026 and was made by Yewtree Biotech AS. It lists the items as two monitors and a desktop workstation [incident report].
- The incident report states that no third party was involved [incident report].
- The two monitors were supplied by Beacon Office Supply at 4,244 dollars [invoice 1].
- The desktop workstation was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 2,742 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file contains no policy schedule. It also does not state the date of the loss or whether the items were in the insured's custody at the site.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.7 covers "damage to contents caused by water escaping from any fixed pipe, tank or appliance" [policy, cl. 4.7]. The reported cause is a burst pipe, and the damaged items are business equipment [notice of loss] [incident report]. Clause 4.7 does not restrict cover by location, so the client-site location does not prevent cover under it.

**Other cover clauses.**
- Clause 4.2 also lists escape of water, but it covers premises named in the schedule or other premises while in the insured's custody [policy, cl. 4.2]. The file has no schedule and no information on custody, so I do not rely on clause 4.2.
- Clause 4.5 (transit) is not engaged. The file does not describe the items as being carried between premises [policy, cl. 4.5].
- Clause 4.9 (electrical surge) is not engaged. The reported cause is water, not a surge [policy, cl. 4.9] [notice of loss].

**Exclusions.**
- **Clause 7.1 (unattended property)** applies only to theft of portable equipment. No theft is reported, so it does not apply [policy, cl. 7.1] [notice of loss] [incident report].
- **Clause 7.3 (wear and tear)** excludes gradual deterioration. The loss is reported as a burst pipe, not deterioration of the equipment, so it does not apply on the file [policy, cl. 7.3] [notice of loss].
- **Clause 7.5 (unproven loss)** applies only where theft is alleged. No theft is alleged, so the seven-day police reporting requirement is not engaged [policy, cl. 7.5] [incident report].

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.7 (escape of water) [policy, cl. 4.7].

Before payment, the file should be completed with the policy schedule, the date of the loss, and confirmation that the pipe was a fixed pipe.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices less any excess the schedule applies. Where the schedule shows no excess, none applies [policy, cl. 9.1].

- Invoice 1, two monitors: 4,244 dollars [invoice 1]
- Invoice 2, desktop workstation: 2,742 dollars [invoice 2]
- Total of invoices: 6,986 dollars
- Excess: none applied. The policy schedule is not in the file, so no excess is shown [policy, cl. 9.1].

**Reserve: 6,986 dollars.**

If the schedule, once obtained, shows the 250 dollar excess referred to in clause 9.1, the reserve becomes 6,736 dollars.
