# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Alder Mews Architects | **Policy:** CC-1518 | **Incident reference:** IR/2026/4182

## Summary

Alder Mews Architects reported on 25 June 2026 the accidental drop of a tablet and a drawing display during transport at a hotel meeting room in Leeds [notice of loss]. No third party was involved [incident report]. The loss falls within the transit cover at clause 4.5, and no exclusion applies. The claim is covered, with a reserve of 3,642 dollars.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clauses**

- **Clause 4.5 (Transit)** covers accidental loss of or damage to portable equipment while being carried by an employee between premises [policy cl. 4.5]. A tablet and a drawing display are portable equipment, and the loss is described as an accidental drop during transport [notice of loss] [incident report]. The loss falls within this clause. The file does not name the carrier or the premises (Fact 11), so the carrier's employee status and the journey between premises should be confirmed on the claim record.
- **Clause 4.2 (Contents)** responds only to theft, fire, storm or escape of water [policy cl. 4.2]. The reported cause is an accidental drop [notice of loss], so this clause does not apply.
- **Clause 4.7 (Escape of water)** requires water escaping from a fixed pipe, tank or appliance [policy cl. 4.7]. No water is reported [notice of loss] [incident report], so this clause does not apply.
- **Clause 4.9 (Electrical surge)** requires a surge in the electrical supply [policy cl. 4.9]. None is reported [notice of loss] [incident report], so this clause does not apply.

**Exclusions**

- **Clause 7.1 (Unattended property)** excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space [policy cl. 7.1]. The loss is an accidental drop and no theft is reported [notice of loss] [incident report], so this exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.3 (Wear and tear)** excludes gradual deterioration [policy cl. 7.3]. The reported cause is a single accidental drop [notice of loss] [incident report], not gradual deterioration, so this exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.5 (Unproven loss)** excludes a loss not reported to the police within seven days, where theft is alleged [policy cl. 7.5]. Theft is not alleged [notice of loss] [incident report], so this exclusion does not apply.

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.5 (Transit) [policy cl. 4.5]. No exclusion at clause 7.1, 7.3 or 7.5 applies.

## Reserve

Clause 9.1 sets the reserve as the sum of the invoices in the file, less the excess where the policy schedule applies one. Where no excess is shown in the schedule, none applies [policy cl. 9.1]. No excess is shown in the file (Fact 10), so none is deducted.
