# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Cinder Lane Hardware Inc | **Policy:** CC-1592

## Summary

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc reported on 11 March 2026 that a laptop and a phone were damaged by a power surge after a storm at a conference venue in Bristol. No third party was involved. The only clause that squarely addresses surge damage is 4.9, which requires the equipment to have been connected through a surge protector. The file does not show that. The file also does not show that the venue is a scheduled premises or one in the insured's custody. The claim is therefore not covered on the present file. The reserve is 6,598 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured reported the loss on 11 March 2026 under policy CC-1592. [notice of loss]
- The reported loss is a power surge after a storm, affecting a laptop and a phone. [notice of loss]
- The location is a conference venue in Bristol. [notice of loss] [incident report]
- The incident report is reference IR/2026/4208, dated 13 March 2026, and was reported by the insured. [incident report]
- The items are a laptop and a phone. No third party was involved. [incident report]
- The laptop invoice is from Beacon Office Supply for 3,892 dollars. [invoice 1]
- The phone invoice is from Crane & Sons Electronics for 2,706 dollars. [invoice 2]
- The file does not state the date of the loss. It also does not state whether the equipment was connected through a surge protector, whether it was being carried by an employee, or whether the venue is a scheduled premises or one in the insured's custody. The policy schedule is not in the file. [notice of loss] [incident report] [policy]

## Coverage analysis

**Clause 4.9 (Electrical surge).** This clause covers damage to electronic equipment caused by a surge in the electrical supply, where the equipment was connected through a surge protector. [policy cl. 4.9] The laptop and phone are electronic equipment, and the loss is described as a power surge. [notice of loss] [incident report] The surge-protector condition is not evidenced anywhere in the file, so cover under 4.9 is not established.

**Clause 4.2 (Contents).** This clause covers damage by storm to business contents at scheduled premises, or at other premises in the insured's custody. [policy cl. 4.2] The loss followed a storm, but it is described as a power surge. [notice of loss] The file does not show that the conference venue is scheduled or in the insured's custody, and the schedule is not in the file. Cover under 4.2 is not established.

**Clause 4.5 (Transit).** This clause covers accidental loss or damage to portable equipment while carried by an employee between premises. [policy cl. 4.5] The file does not say the items were being carried between premises. Cover under 4.5 is not established.

**Clause 4.7 (Escape of water).** No escape of water from a fixed pipe, tank or appliance is reported. [policy cl. 4.7] [notice of loss] [incident report] This clause does not apply.

**Exclusions.**
- **7.1 Unattended property.** This excludes theft of unattended portable equipment. [policy cl. 7.1] No theft is reported. [notice of loss] [incident report] It does not apply.
- **7.3 Wear and tear.** This excludes gradual deterioration. [policy cl. 7.3] The loss is reported as a surge event, not gradual deterioration. [notice of loss] It does not apply.
- **7.5 Unproven loss.** This excludes theft not reported to the police within seven days. [policy cl. 7.5] No theft is alleged. [notice of loss] [incident report] It does not apply.

No exclusion bars the claim. The difficulty is that the conditions of the granting clauses are not shown to be met.

## Decision

**Not covered on the present file.** Cover under clause 4.9 requires connection through a surge protector, and cover under clause 4.2 requires scheduled premises or premises in the insured's custody. [policy cl. 4.9] [policy cl. 4.2] Neither condition is evidenced. This position should be reviewed if the insured provides evidence of the following:
- connection through a surge protector;
- the venue's status as scheduled premises or premises in the insured's custody; or
- carriage by an employee between premises.

The policy schedule should also be added to the file.

## Reserve

The reserve is the sum of the invoices: 3,892 dollars [invoice 1] plus 2,706 dollars [invoice 2], which is 6,598 dollars. No schedule is in the file, so no excess is shown and none applies. [policy cl. 9.1]

**Reserve: 6,598 dollars.** It is held pending the outstanding coverage information above.
