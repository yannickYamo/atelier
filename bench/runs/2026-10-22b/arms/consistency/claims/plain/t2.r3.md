# Claims Assessment Memo

## Summary

Yewtree Biotech AS (policy CC-1444) reported water damage from a burst pipe to two monitors and a desktop workstation at a client site in Kilburn. No third party was involved. The loss falls within the escape of water cover in clauses 4.2 and 4.7, and no exclusion applies. The decision is that the claim is covered, with a reserve of 6,986 dollars.

## Facts

- Yewtree Biotech AS reported the loss on 15 March 2026 under policy CC-1444 [notice of loss].
- The reported loss is water damage from a burst pipe to two monitors and a desktop workstation [notice of loss].
- The location is a client site in Kilburn [notice of loss], [incident report].
- An incident report, reference IR/2026/4156, was made by Yewtree Biotech AS on 17 March 2026 [incident report].
- The incident report lists the items as two monitors and a desktop workstation, and states that no third party was involved [incident report].
- The two monitors are invoiced by Beacon Office Supply at 4,244 dollars [invoice 1].
- The desktop workstation is invoiced by Crane & Sons Electronics at 2,742 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file contains no policy schedule, no police report, and no statement of the date of loss.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clauses.**
- Clause 4.2 covers loss of or damage to business contents at the scheduled premises, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, when caused by escape of water [policy, 4.2]. The monitors and workstation are business contents. The loss occurred at a client site, so cover depends on the items being in the insured's custody there. The file does not state this expressly, but the incident report records the items as those of the insured and no third party as involved [incident report]. I have treated custody as met. It should be confirmed.
- Clause 4.7 covers damage to contents caused by water escaping from any fixed pipe, tank or appliance [policy, 4.7]. The cause reported is a burst pipe [notice of loss], [incident report]. The file does not describe the pipe as fixed, and I have treated it as a fixed pipe.
- Clauses 4.5 (transit) and 4.9 (electrical surge) are not engaged. The file describes neither carriage between premises nor a surge [notice of loss], [incident report].

**Exclusions.**
- Clause 7.1 (unattended property) applies only to theft of portable equipment [policy, 7.1]. No theft is reported [notice of loss], [incident report]. It does not apply.
- Clause 7.3 (wear and tear) excludes gradual deterioration [policy, 7.3]. The loss is reported as water damage from a burst pipe, and nothing in the file describes gradual deterioration [notice of loss], [incident report]. It does not apply.
- Clause 7.5 (unproven loss) applies only where theft is alleged [policy, 7.5]. No theft is alleged [notice of loss], [incident report]. The absence of a police report is therefore not relevant. It does not apply.

**Points to confirm.** The schedule is not in the file, so I cannot confirm the named premises or whether it shows an excess. Custody of the items at the Kilburn site and the fixed nature of the pipe should also be confirmed. None of these points changes the conclusion on the file as it stands.

## Decision

Covered, under clause 4.2 (contents at other premises in the insured's custody, caused by escape of water), read with clause 4.7 (damage caused by water escaping from a fixed pipe) [policy, 4.2, 4.7]. No exclusion in clause 7 applies.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices less the 250 dollar excess where the policy schedule applies one. Where no excess is shown in the schedule, none applies [policy, 9.1].

- Invoice 1, two monitors: 4,244 dollars [invoice 1]
- Invoice 2, desktop workstation: 2,742 dollars [invoice 2]
- Sum of invoices: 6,986 dollars
- Excess: none. The file contains no policy schedule showing an excess, so none is applied [policy, 9.1].

**Reserve: 6,986 dollars.** If the schedule, once obtained, shows an excess, the reserve reduces by 250 dollars to 6,736 dollars.
