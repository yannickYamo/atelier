# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Zephyr Point Media Ltd | **Policy:** CC-1481

## Summary

Zephyr Point Media Ltd reported the theft of a 3D printer and filament stock from a locked van at a shared workshop on Tanner Row. The loss was notified on 20 April 2026 and reported to the police on 22 April 2026. The items were in a locked container at the time, so the unattended property exclusion does not apply. The loss falls within clause 4.2 (Contents). I recommend accepting the claim, subject to two confirmations noted below, with a reserve of 6,264 dollars.

## Facts

1. The insured reported the loss on 20 April 2026 [notice of loss].
2. The loss was theft of a 3D printer and filament stock from a locked van at a shared workshop on Tanner Row [notice of loss].
3. The insured reported the theft to the police on 22 April 2026, under reference CR/2026/4169 [police report].
4. The items stolen were a 3D printer and filament stock [police report].
5. The items were in a locked container at the time of the theft [police report].
6. The 3D printer is invoiced by Beacon Office Supply at 5,181 dollars [invoice 1].
7. The filament stock is invoiced by Crane & Sons Electronics at 1,083 dollars [invoice 2].
8. The file does not state the date of the theft. It does not contain the policy schedule.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft, at the premises named in the schedule and at any other premises while in the insured's custody [policy, cl. 4.2]. The loss is theft of business contents [notice of loss; police report]. The schedule is not in the file, so it is not confirmed whether the Tanner Row workshop is a named premises, or whether the items were in the insured's custody there. This needs to be confirmed.

**Other cover grants considered.**
- Clause 4.5 (Transit) is not engaged. Nothing in the file shows the equipment was being carried by an employee between premises [policy, cl. 4.5; notice of loss].
- Clause 4.7 (Escape of water) is not engaged. The cause of loss is theft [policy, cl. 4.7; notice of loss].
- Clause 4.9 (Electrical surge) is not engaged. The cause of loss is theft [policy, cl. 4.9; notice of loss].

**Exclusions.**
- **Clause 7.1 (Unattended property).** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room at the time [policy, cl. 7.1]. The workshop is shared, but the items were in a locked container at the time of the theft [police report]. The exception applies, so the exclusion does not operate.
- **Clause 7.3 (Wear and tear).** This excludes gradual deterioration [policy, cl. 7.3]. The cause of loss is theft, not deterioration [notice of loss; police report]. The exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.5 (Unproven loss).** This excludes a theft not reported to the police within seven days [policy, cl. 7.5]. The police report is dated 22 April 2026 [police report], and the loss was notified to us on 20 April 2026 [notice of loss]. The date of theft is not in the file. The theft must have occurred on or before 20 April 2026. The police report is within seven days if the theft occurred on or after 15 April 2026. The date of theft needs to be confirmed to close out this exclusion.

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (Contents) [policy, cl. 4.2]. Exclusion 7.1 does not apply because the items were in a locked container [policy, cl. 7.1; police report]. Exclusion 7.3 does not apply [policy, cl. 7.3]. Exclusion 7.5 is not engaged if the theft occurred on or after 15 April 2026 [policy, cl. 7.5; police report].

This decision is subject to confirming:
1. The date of the theft (clause 7.5).
2. The policy schedule (premises under clause 4.2, and any excess under clause 9.1).

Clause 9.1 sets the reserve as the sum of the invoices, less the 250 dollar excess only where the schedule applies one. Where no excess is shown in the schedule, none applies [policy, cl. 9.1]. The schedule is not in the file and shows no excess, so none is deducted.

**Reserve: 6,264 dollars** [invoice 1; invoice 2]. If the schedule, once obtained, applies an excess, the reserve reduces to 6,014 dollars.
