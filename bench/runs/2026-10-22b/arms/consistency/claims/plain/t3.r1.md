# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Zephyr Point Media Ltd
**Policy:** CC-1481
**Police reference:** CR/2026/4169

## Summary

Zephyr Point Media Ltd reported the theft of a 3D printer and filament stock from a locked van at a shared workshop on Tanner Row. The loss was reported to us on 20 April 2026 and to the police on 22 April 2026. The claim falls within clause 4.2 (Contents), and exclusion 7.1 does not apply because the items were in a locked container. Two points are not documented in the file and should be confirmed before payment: the date of the theft (for exclusion 7.5) and the insured's custody of, or schedule listing for, the premises (for clause 4.2). The recommended reserve is 6,264 dollars.

## Facts

1. The insured is Zephyr Point Media Ltd, under policy CC-1481 [notice of loss].
2. The loss was reported to us on 20 April 2026 [notice of loss].
3. The loss is theft from a locked van of a 3D printer and filament stock [notice of loss].
4. The location is a shared workshop on Tanner Row [notice of loss] [police report].
5. The theft was reported to the police by the insured on 22 April 2026 under reference CR/2026/4169 [police report].
6. The police report records that the items were in a locked container at the time [police report].
7. The 3D printer was supplied by Beacon Office Supply at 5,181 dollars [invoice 1].
8. The filament stock was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 1,083 dollars [invoice 2].
9. The file does not state the date of the theft. It does not contain the policy schedule, so it shows neither the named premises nor any excess.

## Coverage analysis

**Clause 4.2 (Contents).** This clause covers loss of business contents caused by theft at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody [policy, 4.2]. The cause of loss is theft [notice of loss] [police report]. The file does not include the schedule or anything showing that the workshop is a named premises or was in the insured's custody. This needs to be confirmed.

**Clause 4.5 (Transit).** This clause covers portable equipment being carried by an employee between premises [policy, 4.5]. The file describes a theft from a locked van at a workshop, not a loss during carriage by an employee [notice of loss]. I have not relied on this clause.

**Clause 4.7 (Escape of water) and clause 4.9 (Electrical surge).** The file describes theft only, with no water or electrical event [notice of loss] [police report]. Neither clause applies.

**Exclusion 7.1 (Unattended property).** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space unless it was in a locked container or locked room [policy, 7.1]. The workshop is shared [notice of loss], but the items were in a locked container [police report]. The exception applies, so the exclusion does not bar the claim.

**Exclusion 7.3 (Wear and tear).** This excludes gradual deterioration [policy, 7.3]. The loss is a theft, and nothing in the file describes deterioration [notice of loss]. It does not apply.

**Exclusion 7.5 (Unproven loss).** This excludes theft not reported to the police within seven days [policy, 7.5]. The police report is dated 22 April 2026 [police report], and the loss was reported to us on 20 April 2026 [notice of loss]. The file does not give the date of the theft, so I cannot confirm from the documents that the seven-day period was met. The date of the theft should be obtained and checked against 22 April 2026.

## Decision

**Covered under clause 4.2 (Contents)**, subject to two confirmations before payment:

1. The date of the theft falls within seven days before the police report of 22 April 2026, so exclusion 7.5 does not apply [policy, 7.5].
2. The Tanner Row workshop is a premises named in the schedule or was in the insured's custody [policy, 4.2].

Exclusions 7.1 and 7.3 do not apply, for the reasons given above.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less the 250 dollar excess where the schedule applies one [policy, 9.1]. No excess is shown in the file, so none is deducted.

| Item | Amount (dollars) |

| 3D printer [invoice 1] | 5,181 |
| Filament stock [invoice 2] | 1,083 |
| Excess | none shown |
| **Reserve** | **6,264** |

If the schedule, once obtained, shows an excess, the reserve should be reduced accordingly.
