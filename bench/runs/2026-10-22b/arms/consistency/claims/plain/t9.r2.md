# Claims Assessment Memo

## Summary

Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd (policy CC-1703) reported the theft of a laptop and an external drive from a locked storage unit at a university library [notice of loss]. The loss was reported to police under reference CR/2026/4247 [police report]. The claim is covered under clause 4.2 (Contents). One point must be confirmed: the date of loss is not in the file, so compliance with the seven-day police reporting condition in clause 7.5 is not yet established. The reserve is 4,732 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured is Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd, under policy CC-1703 [notice of loss].
- The loss was reported to us on 26 September 2026 [notice of loss].
- The loss is theft of a laptop and an external drive from a locked storage unit at a university library [notice of loss].
- The theft was reported to police on 28 September 2026 under reference CR/2026/4247, and the report was made by Ferrous Gate Engineering Ltd [police report].
- The police report records the items as a laptop and an external drive, stolen from a locked storage unit at a university library. It states that the items were in a locked container at the time [police report].
- The laptop was supplied by Beacon Office Supply, at 2,603 dollars [invoice 1].
- The external drive was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics, at 2,129 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file does not state the date of the loss, and it contains no policy schedule. The schedule is therefore not available to show the named premises or any excess.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of or damage to business contents at the scheduled premises, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, when caused by theft [policy cl. 4.2]. The cause of loss is theft [notice of loss] [police report]. The library is not shown to be a scheduled premises, because the schedule is not in the file. Cover therefore rests on the items being in the insured's custody. They were held in a locked storage unit or container at the library [police report]. The file does not say in terms whose unit it was, so this should be confirmed on the file.

**Other cover clauses.** Clause 4.5 (Transit) does not apply, because the file records no carrying of the items between premises [policy cl. 4.5] [notice of loss]. Clauses 4.7 (Escape of water) and 4.9 (Electrical surge) do not apply, because no water damage or surge is reported [policy cl. 4.7] [policy cl. 4.9] [notice of loss].

**Exclusions.**

- **Clause 7.1 (Unattended property).** A university library is a shared or public space, and the items were unattended when stolen. The exclusion, however, does not apply where the property was in a locked container or locked room at the time [policy cl. 7.1]. The police report records that the items were in a locked container [police report], and the notice of loss describes a locked storage unit [notice of loss]. The exception is met, so the exclusion does not bar the claim.
- **Clause 7.3 (Wear and tear).** The loss is a theft, not gradual deterioration [policy cl. 7.3] [notice of loss]. The exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.5 (Unproven loss).** The exclusion applies where theft is alleged and the loss was not reported to police within seven days [policy cl. 7.5]. The police report is dated 28 September 2026 [police report], and the loss was reported to us on 26 September 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss therefore occurred on or before 26 September, but the file gives no date of loss. The seven-day period cannot be calculated from the file alone. The exclusion is not shown to apply, but the date of loss should be obtained and recorded to close this point.

## Decision

Covered, under clause 4.2 (Contents) [policy cl. 4.2]. The exclusions at clauses 7.1 and 7.3 do not apply, as set out above. The decision is subject to confirmation of the date of loss, so that clause 7.5 can be shown not to apply, and to confirmation that the storage unit was in the insured's custody.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1 the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less the 250 dollar excess where the policy schedule applies one. Where no excess is shown in the schedule, none applies [policy cl. 9.1]. No schedule is in the file, so no excess is shown and none is deducted.
