# Claims Assessment Memo

## Summary

Elmhurst Clinics LLP, policy CC-1666, reported on 21 June 2026 fire damage to a label printer and a barcode scanner. The items were at a storage unit on Mill Road, and the fire was in a neighbouring unit [notice of loss]. The claim is covered under clause 4.2 (Contents), subject to the confirmation noted in the coverage analysis. The reserve is 3,254 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured is Elmhurst Clinics LLP, under policy CC-1666 [notice of loss].
- The loss was reported on 21 June 2026 [notice of loss].
- The loss is fire damage, in a neighbouring unit, to a label printer and a barcode scanner [notice of loss].
- The location is a storage unit on Mill Road [notice of loss].
- An incident report, reference IR/2026/4234, dated 23 June 2026, was made by Elmhurst Clinics LLP. It lists the same two items and describes fire damage in a neighbouring unit at a storage unit on Mill Road. It states that no third party was involved [incident report].
- The label printer was supplied by Beacon Office Supply at 1,666 dollars [invoice 1].
- The barcode scanner was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 1,588 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file does not state the date of the fire, and it does not contain the policy schedule.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of or damage to business contents at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, when caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water [policy, cl. 4.2]. A label printer and a barcode scanner are business contents, and the cause of loss reported is fire [notice of loss; incident report]. Fire is an insured peril under clause 4.2.

**Open point.** The schedule is not in the file, so I cannot confirm from the file that the Mill Road storage unit is a scheduled premises. The file also does not say in terms that the unit was in the insured's custody. Cover under 4.2 depends on one of these two points. Confirm it against the schedule or with the insured before payment.

**Other cover clauses.** These do not apply on the facts in the file.
- Clause 4.5 (Transit) applies to equipment carried by an employee between premises. The file records no transit [policy, cl. 4.5; incident report].
- Clause 4.7 (Escape of water) applies to water escaping from a fixed pipe, tank or appliance. No water damage is reported [policy, cl. 4.7; notice of loss].
- Clause 4.9 (Electrical surge) applies to damage from a surge in the electrical supply. No surge is reported [policy, cl. 4.9; notice of loss].

**Exclusions.**
- Clause 7.1 (Unattended property) excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space [policy, cl. 7.1]. The loss reported is fire, not theft, so the exclusion does not apply [notice of loss; incident report].
- Clause 7.3 (Wear and tear) excludes gradual deterioration [policy, cl. 7.3]. The damage is attributed to a fire, not to gradual deterioration, so the exclusion does not apply [notice of loss; incident report].
- Clause 7.5 (Unproven loss) excludes a loss not reported to the police within seven days where theft is alleged [policy, cl. 7.5]. Theft is not alleged, so the exclusion does not apply and no police report is required [notice of loss; incident report].

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (Contents), as fire damage to business contents [policy, cl. 4.2]. This is subject to confirming that the Mill Road storage unit is a scheduled premises or was in the insured's custody. No exclusion in clause 7 applies.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less the 250 dollar excess where the policy schedule applies one. Where the schedule shows no excess, none applies [policy, cl. 9.1].

The schedule is not in the file, so no excess is shown and none is deducted. If the schedule applies the 250 dollar excess, the reserve reduces to 3,004 dollars.
