# Claims Assessment Memo

## Summary

Elmhurst Clinics LLP (policy CC-1666) reported on 21 June 2026 that a label printer and a barcode scanner were damaged by fire in a neighbouring unit at a storage unit on Mill Road. No third party is involved. The claim is covered under clause 4.2 (Contents, fire), subject to the open point on premises noted below. The reserve is 3,254 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured is Elmhurst Clinics LLP, under policy CC-1666 [notice of loss].
- The loss was reported on 21 June 2026 [notice of loss].
- The loss is fire damage in a neighbouring unit to a label printer and a barcode scanner [notice of loss].
- The location is a storage unit on Mill Road [notice of loss].
- Incident report IR/2026/4234 is dated 23 June 2026 and was made by Elmhurst Clinics LLP [incident report].
- The incident report lists the items as a label printer and a barcode scanner [incident report].
- The incident report describes the circumstances as fire damage in a neighbouring unit at a storage unit on Mill Road, with no third party involved [incident report].
- The label printer was supplied by Beacon Office Supply, at 1,666 dollars [invoice 1].
- The barcode scanner was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics, at 1,588 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file does not state the date of the fire, and it does not include the policy schedule [policy].

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of or damage to business contents at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, when caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water [policy, cl. 4.2]. A label printer and a barcode scanner are business contents, and the reported cause is fire [notice of loss; incident report]. The peril is therefore one that clause 4.2 covers.

**Open point on premises.** The policy schedule is not in the file, so the file does not show whether the Mill Road storage unit is a scheduled premises. It also does not state whether the unit was in the insured's custody. Clause 4.2 requires one or the other [policy, cl. 4.2]. This should be confirmed against the schedule before payment.

**Other cover clauses.** Clause 4.5 (Transit) does not apply because nothing in the file indicates the items were being carried between premises [policy, cl. 4.5; notice of loss]. Clause 4.7 (Escape of water) does not apply because the cause reported is fire, not water [policy, cl. 4.7; incident report]. Clause 4.9 (Electrical surge) does not apply because no surge is reported [policy, cl. 4.9; incident report].

**Exclusions.**
- **Clause 7.1 (Unattended property):** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space. The reported cause is fire and no theft is alleged, so it does not apply [policy, cl. 7.1; notice of loss].
- **Clause 7.3 (Wear and tear):** This excludes gradual deterioration. The reported cause is fire damage, and nothing in the file attributes the damage to gradual deterioration, so it does not apply [policy, cl. 7.3; incident report].
- **Clause 7.5 (Unproven loss):** This applies where theft is alleged and the loss was not reported to the police within seven days. No theft is alleged, so a police report is not required and the clause does not apply [policy, cl. 7.5; notice of loss; incident report].

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (Contents, fire) [policy, cl. 4.2]. No exclusion in clause 7 applies. This decision is subject to confirming from the policy schedule that the Mill Road storage unit is a scheduled premises or was in the insured's custody.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less the 250 dollar excess where the schedule applies one. Where the schedule shows no excess, none applies [policy, cl. 9.1].

| Item | Amount (dollars) |
|---|---|
| Label printer [invoice 1] | 1,666 |
| Barcode scanner [invoice 2] | 1,588 |
| **Sum of invoices** | **3,254** |
| Excess | 0 |
| **Reserve** | **3,254** |

No schedule is in the file, so no excess is shown and none is applied [policy, cl. 9.1]. If the schedule, once obtained, shows an excess, the reserve will be reduced by 250 dollars to 3,004 dollars.
