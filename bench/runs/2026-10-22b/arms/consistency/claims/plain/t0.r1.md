# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Vellum Press Inc
**Policy:** CC-1370

## Summary

Vellum Press Inc reported on 5 June 2026 the theft of a laptop and a docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street [notice of loss]. The theft was reported to the police on 7 June 2026 under reference CR/2026/4130 [police report]. The loss is covered under clause 4.2 of the policy. The reserve is 4,030 dollars, with no excess shown in the file.

## Facts

- Vellum Press Inc reported the loss under policy CC-1370 on 5 June 2026 [notice of loss].
- The loss is described as theft of a laptop and docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street [notice of loss].
- The theft was reported to the police by Vellum Press Inc on 7 June 2026, reference CR/2026/4130 [police report].
- The police report lists the items as a laptop and a docking station [police report].
- The police report states the items were in a locked container at the time of the theft, at a co-working space on Fenwick Street [police report].
- The laptop was supplied by Beacon Office Supply at 2,370 dollars [invoice 1].
- The docking station was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 1,660 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file contains a policy extract but no policy schedule [policy].
- The file does not state the date on which the theft occurred [notice of loss] [police report].

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody [policy, cl. 4.2]. The items were business equipment stolen from a locker at a co-working space [notice of loss] [police report]. The schedule is not in the file, so the premises cannot be checked against it. The loss falls within the "other premises" limb to the extent the items were in the insured's custody in the locked locker. The cause of loss, theft, is an insured peril under clause 4.2.

**Other insuring clauses.** Clauses 4.5, 4.7 and 4.9 do not apply. Nothing in the file shows the items were being carried between premises (cl. 4.5), or that water (cl. 4.7) or an electrical surge (cl. 4.9) was involved [notice of loss] [police report] [policy].

**Exclusions.**
- **Clause 7.1 (unattended property).** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared space unless it was in a locked container or locked room [policy, cl. 7.1]. The co-working space is a shared space, but the items were in a locked locker [notice of loss] and a locked container [police report]. The exception applies, so the exclusion does not operate.
- **Clause 7.3 (wear and tear).** This excludes gradual deterioration [policy, cl. 7.3]. The loss is a theft, not deterioration [notice of loss] [police report]. The exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.5 (unproven loss).** This excludes theft not reported to the police within seven days [policy, cl. 7.5]. The police report is dated 7 June 2026, two days after the notice of loss on 5 June 2026 [police report] [notice of loss]. The file does not give the date of the theft. The exclusion is therefore treated as not engaged, on the basis that the theft was discovered within seven days before 7 June 2026. This should be confirmed with the insured.

**Points to confirm on the file.**
1. The policy schedule, to confirm the premises and whether an excess is shown.
2. The date of the theft, to close out clause 7.5.

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (contents: theft at other premises while in the insured's custody) [policy, cl. 4.2]. Clause 7.1 does not exclude the loss because the items were in a locked container [policy, cl. 7.1] [police report]. The decision is subject to the two confirmations listed above.
