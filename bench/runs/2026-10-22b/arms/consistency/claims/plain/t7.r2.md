# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Dovecote Learning Ltd | **Policy:** CC-1629

## Summary

Dovecote Learning Ltd reported the theft of a soldering station and an oscilloscope from a locked office at a rented studio on Pike Street. The claim falls under the Commercial Contents Policy, clause 4.2. None of the exclusions at 7.1, 7.3 or 7.5 defeats it on the file as it stands. The decision is that the claim is covered, with a reserve of 5,876 dollars. Two points should be confirmed from documents not in the file (see Coverage analysis).

## Facts

- Dovecote Learning Ltd reported the loss on 16 April 2026 under policy CC-1629 [notice of loss].
- The loss was theft, overnight, from a locked office, of a soldering station and an oscilloscope [notice of loss].
- The location was a rented studio on Pike Street [notice of loss].
- The theft was reported to the police on 18 April 2026 under reference CR/2026/4221, by Dovecote Learning Ltd [police report].
- The police report records that the items were in a locked container at the time of the theft [police report].
- The soldering station was supplied by Beacon Office Supply at 4,829 dollars [invoice 1].
- The oscilloscope was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 1,047 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file does not state the date of the theft. The policy schedule is not in the file.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents, caused by theft among other perils, at the premises named in the schedule and at any other premises while in the insured's custody [policy, 4.2]. The loss is theft of business equipment from a rented studio [notice of loss; police report]. The schedule is not in the file, so the studio should be checked against the named premises. If it is not named, cover depends on the studio being in the insured's custody, which a rented studio would ordinarily satisfy.

**Other cover clauses.** Clauses 4.5 (transit), 4.7 (escape of water) and 4.9 (electrical surge) are not engaged. The file records theft, not carriage between premises, water damage or a power surge [notice of loss; police report].

**Exclusions.**

- **7.1 Unattended property.** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room [policy, 7.1]. The file does not say whether the studio is shared. Even if it is, the exception applies: the theft was from a locked office [notice of loss], and the items were in a locked container [police report]. The exclusion does not apply.
- **7.3 Wear and tear.** This excludes gradual deterioration [policy, 7.3]. The loss is theft, not deterioration [notice of loss]. The exclusion does not apply.
- **7.5 Unproven loss.** This excludes a loss not reported to the police within seven days, where theft is alleged [policy, 7.5]. The police report was made on 18 April 2026 [police report], and the loss was notified to us on 16 April 2026 [notice of loss]. The date of the theft is not stated in the file, so the seven-day period cannot be measured exactly. The exclusion would apply only if the theft occurred more than seven days before 18 April 2026. The theft date should be confirmed with the insured to close this point. Nothing in the file indicates the report was late.

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (Contents, theft) [policy, 4.2]. No exclusion applies on the present file (7.1, 7.3, 7.5 addressed above). This is subject to confirming the date of theft against clause 7.5 and the premises against the schedule.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1 the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less the 250 dollar excess where the schedule applies one. Where no excess is shown in the schedule, none applies [policy, 9.1]. No schedule is in the file and no excess is shown, so none is deducted.

| Item | Source | Amount (dollars) |
|---|---|---|
| Soldering station | [invoice 1] | 4,829 |
| Oscilloscope | [invoice 2] | 1,047 |
| Excess | [policy, 9.1] | 0 |
| **Reserve** | | **5,876** |

If the schedule, once obtained, shows an excess, the reserve should be reduced accordingly under clause 9.1.
