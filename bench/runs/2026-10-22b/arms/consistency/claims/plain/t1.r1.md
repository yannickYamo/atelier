# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Wayfarer Fintech Ltd
**Policy:** CC-1407
**Police reference:** CR/2026/4143

## Summary

Wayfarer Fintech Ltd reported the theft of a camera body and a lens from an unlocked desk at its office on Harbour Lane [notice of loss]. The police report describes the items as left in a shared area and not in a locked container [police report]. Exclusion 7.1 (unattended property) applies, so the claim is not covered. The reserve figure under clause 9.1 is 5,508 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured reported the loss on 10 September 2026 under policy CC-1407 [notice of loss].
- The loss is described as theft of a camera body and lens from an unlocked desk at the insured's office on Harbour Lane [notice of loss].
- The theft was reported to police on 12 September 2026 under reference CR/2026/4143, and the items listed are a camera body and a lens [police report].
- The police report records that the items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container [police report].
- The camera body is invoiced by Beacon Office Supply at 3,307 dollars [invoice 1].
- The lens is invoiced by Crane & Sons Electronics at 2,201 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file does not contain the policy schedule, the date of the theft, or any record of whether a locked room was involved.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft at the premises named in the schedule [policy, cl. 4.2]. The loss alleged is theft at the insured's office on Harbour Lane [notice of loss]. The schedule is not in the file, so I cannot confirm that Harbour Lane is a named premises. I have assumed it is for this analysis, and the decision does not depend on it.

**Exclusions considered**

- **7.1 Unattended property: applies.** The clause excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room at the time [policy, cl. 7.1]. The camera body and lens are portable equipment. The police report says they were left in a shared area, in an unlocked desk, and not in a locked container [police report; notice of loss]. The file records no locked room. I have read "left in a shared area" as meaning the items were unattended when taken. The file does not say whether anyone was present, so this reading should be confirmed if the decision is challenged.
- **7.3 Wear and tear: does not apply.** The loss is a theft and not gradual deterioration [notice of loss; policy, cl. 7.3].
- **7.5 Unproven loss: cannot be assessed, and not relied on.** The clause excludes theft not reported to police within seven days [policy, cl. 7.5]. The police report is dated 12 September 2026 [police report], but the file does not give the date of the theft. I therefore cannot say whether the seven-day period was met. The decision does not rest on this clause.

**Other cover clauses.** Clauses 4.5 (transit), 4.7 (escape of water) and 4.9 (electrical surge) do not fit the loss described. The file shows no carriage by an employee, no water damage and no electrical surge [notice of loss; police report].

## Decision

**Not covered.** Theft is an insured peril under clause 4.2, but exclusion 7.1 removes cover. The items were portable equipment left in a shared area and not in a locked container or locked room [policy, cl. 7.1; police report].

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1 the reserve is the sum of the invoices, less any excess shown in the schedule [policy, cl. 9.1].

- Invoice 1: 3,307 dollars [invoice 1]
- Invoice 2: 2,201 dollars [invoice 2]
- Sum of invoices: 5,508 dollars
- Excess: the schedule is not in the file and shows no excess, so none applies under clause 9.1.

**Reserve: 5,508 dollars** [invoice 1; invoice 2]. This is the figure the clause 9.1 method produces. Given the decision above, no payment is anticipated unless the decision is revised.
