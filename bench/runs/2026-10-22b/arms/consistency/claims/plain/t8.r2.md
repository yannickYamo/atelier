# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Elmhurst Clinics LLP | **Policy:** CC-1666 | **Incident reference:** IR/2026/4234

## Summary

Elmhurst Clinics LLP reported fire damage to a label printer and a barcode scanner at a storage unit on Mill Road. The loss was reported on 21 June 2026 under policy CC-1666. The loss is a fire loss to business contents and falls within clause 4.2, and no exclusion applies. I recommend accepting the claim, subject to confirming that the storage unit is a scheduled premises or was in the insured's custody (see Coverage analysis). The reserve is 3,254 dollars before any excess.

## Facts

1. The insured is Elmhurst Clinics LLP, under policy CC-1666. [notice of loss]
2. The loss was reported on 21 June 2026. [notice of loss]
3. The reported loss is fire damage in a neighbouring unit of a label printer and a barcode scanner. [notice of loss]
4. The location is a storage unit on Mill Road. [notice of loss]
5. An incident report, reference IR/2026/4234, is dated 23 June 2026 and was made by Elmhurst Clinics LLP. It lists the items as a label printer and a barcode scanner. [incident report]

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of or damage to business contents at the scheduled premises, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water. [policy, cl. 4.2] The printer and scanner are business contents, and the stated cause is fire. [notice of loss; incident report]

**Open point on premises.** The policy schedule is not in the file, so I cannot confirm that the Mill Road storage unit is a scheduled premises. Nor does the file state that the unit was in the insured's custody, which is the alternative route under clause 4.2. [policy, cl. 4.2] The notice of loss and incident report also refer to a "neighbouring unit" without saying in which unit the items were kept. [notice of loss; incident report] These points should be confirmed with the insured and against the schedule before payment.

**Other cover clauses.** Clauses 4.5 (transit), 4.7 (escape of water) and 4.9 (electrical surge) are not engaged, because the reported cause is fire and nothing in the file refers to carriage, water or a power surge. [policy, cl. 4.5, 4.7, 4.9; notice of loss; incident report]

**Exclusions.**
- **Clause 7.1 (unattended property):** This applies only to theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space. [policy, cl. 7.1] The loss reported is fire damage, not theft, so the exclusion does not apply. [notice of loss; incident report]
- **Clause 7.3 (wear and tear):** This excludes gradual deterioration. [policy, cl. 7.3] The loss is described as fire damage, not gradual deterioration, so the exclusion does not apply. [notice of loss; incident report]
- **Clause 7.5 (unproven loss):** This applies only where theft is alleged and the loss was not reported to the police within seven days. [policy, cl. 7.5] No theft is alleged, so the exclusion does not apply and the absence of a police report does not affect cover. [notice of loss; incident report]

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (Contents, fire). [policy, cl. 4.2] No exclusion in clauses 7.1, 7.3 or 7.5 applies. Payment is subject to confirming that the Mill Road storage unit is a scheduled premises or was in the insured's custody.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices less any excess of 250 dollars where the policy schedule applies one. If the schedule shows no excess, none applies. [policy, cl. 9.1]

- Invoice 1, label printer: 1,666 dollars [invoice 1]
- Invoice 2, barcode scanner: 1,588 dollars [invoice 2]
- **Sum of invoices: 3,254 dollars**

The schedule is not in the file, so I cannot confirm whether an excess applies. The reserve is therefore **3,254 dollars**, reducing to **3,004 dollars** if the schedule shows the 250-dollar excess. [policy, cl. 9.1] The schedule should be checked and the reserve finalised accordingly.
