# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Vellum Press Inc
**Policy:** CC-1370

## Summary

Vellum Press Inc reported the theft of a laptop and a docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. The loss was reported on 5 June 2026 and to the police on 7 June 2026. The claim is covered under clause 4.2 (Contents). No exclusion in clause 7 applies on the file. The reserve is 4,030 dollars.

## Facts

- Vellum Press Inc reported the loss on 5 June 2026 under policy CC-1370 [notice of loss].
- The loss is theft of a laptop and docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street [notice of loss].
- The theft was reported to the police on 7 June 2026 under reference CR/2026/4130. The report was made by Vellum Press Inc [police report].
- The police report lists the items as a laptop and a docking station. It records that they were in a locked container at the time of the theft [police report].
- The laptop was supplied by Beacon Office Supply at 2,370 dollars [invoice 1].
- The docking station was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 1,660 dollars [invoice 2].
- The date of the theft is not stated in the file [notice of loss] [police report].
- The policy schedule is not in the file [policy].

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of or damage to business contents caused by theft, at the premises named in the schedule and at any other premises while in the insured's custody [policy, cl. 4.2]. The cause of loss is theft, and the items are business equipment [notice of loss] [police report]. The co-working space is not shown to be a scheduled premises, because the schedule is not in the file. The claim therefore rests on the "other premises" limb, which requires the items to have been in the insured's custody. The file records that they were in a locked locker [police report]. I treat that as custody, but the file does not state it expressly.

**Clauses 4.5, 4.7 and 4.9.** These are not relied on. The loss is theft, not transit, escape of water or electrical surge [notice of loss] [policy, cl. 4.5, 4.7, 4.9].

**Exclusion 7.1 (Unattended property).** This excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared space unless it was in a locked container or locked room [policy, cl. 7.1]. A co-working space is a shared space. The items were in a locked locker at the time, so the exception applies and the exclusion does not [police report] [notice of loss].

**Exclusion 7.3 (Wear and tear).** This excludes gradual deterioration [policy, cl. 7.3]. The loss is theft, not deterioration, so it does not apply [notice of loss].

**Exclusion 7.5 (Unproven loss).** This excludes a theft claim not reported to the police within seven days [policy, cl. 7.5]. The police report is dated 7 June 2026, two days after the notice of loss dated 5 June 2026 [police report] [notice of loss]. The file does not state the date of the theft, so the seven-day period cannot be measured from the theft itself. On the dates in the file, the exclusion is not shown to apply.

**Points to confirm.**
- The date of the theft, to close out clause 7.5.
- That the items were in the insured's custody, for clause 4.2.
- The schedule, for any excess.

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (Contents) [policy, cl. 4.2]. Exclusions 7.1, 7.3 and 7.5 do not apply on the file [policy, cl. 7.1, 7.3, 7.5]. The decision is subject to confirming the points above.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1 the reserve is the sum of the invoices, less the 250 dollar excess where the schedule applies one. Where no excess is shown in the schedule, none applies [policy, cl. 9.1]. The schedule is not in the file, so no excess is shown and none is deducted.
