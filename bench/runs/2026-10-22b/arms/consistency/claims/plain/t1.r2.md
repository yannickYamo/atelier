# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Wayfarer Fintech Ltd | **Policy:** CC-1407 | **Police ref:** CR/2026/4143

## Summary

Wayfarer Fintech Ltd reported on 10 September 2026 the theft of a camera body and a lens from an unlocked desk at its office on Harbour Lane. The items were in a shared area and not in a locked container. Theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared space is excluded by clause 7.1 unless the equipment was in a locked container or locked room. The file shows neither, so the claim is not covered. The reserve calculated under clause 9.1 is 5,508 dollars, and no payment is proposed.

## Facts

- The insured is Wayfarer Fintech Ltd, under policy CC-1407. [notice of loss]
- The loss was reported on 10 September 2026. [notice of loss]
- The reported loss is theft of a camera body and a lens from an unlocked desk at the insured's office on Harbour Lane. [notice of loss]
- The insured reported the theft to the police on 12 September 2026, reference CR/2026/4143. The items listed are a camera body and a lens. [police report]
- The police report records that the items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container. [police report]
- The camera body is invoiced by Beacon Office Supply at 3,307 dollars. [invoice 1]
- The lens is invoiced by Crane & Sons Electronics at 2,201 dollars. [invoice 2]
- The file does not state the date of the theft. [notice of loss] [police report]
- The policy schedule is not in the file. Only a policy extract is. [policy]

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft at the premises named in the schedule, or at other premises in the insured's custody. The loss is a theft of business contents from the insured's own office. [policy, cl. 4.2] [notice of loss] The schedule is not in the file, so I cannot confirm from the file that Harbour Lane is the scheduled premises. Clause 4.2 would respond to this loss only if no exclusion applies.

**Other cover clauses.** None of these fits the reported loss:
- Clause 4.5 (transit) requires loss while the equipment is carried by an employee between premises. The file reports a theft from a desk at the office. [policy, cl. 4.5] [notice of loss]
- Clause 4.7 (escape of water) is not engaged, because no water damage is reported. [policy, cl. 4.7] [notice of loss]
- Clause 4.9 (electrical surge) is not engaged, because no surge is reported. [policy, cl. 4.9] [notice of loss]

**Exclusions.**
- **Clause 7.1, unattended property: applies.** The exclusion removes cover for theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless the equipment was in a locked container or locked room. [policy, cl. 7.1] A camera body and lens are portable equipment. They were left in a shared area on an unlocked desk and were not in a locked container. [police report] [notice of loss] Nothing in the file says they were in a locked room. The file does not use the word "unattended", but the items were left in the shared area and then taken. I recommend confirming that no locked room was involved before the decision is issued.
- **Clause 7.3, wear and tear: does not apply.** The loss is a theft, not gradual deterioration. [policy, cl. 7.3] [notice of loss]
- **Clause 7.5, unproven loss: not shown to apply.** The exclusion applies where theft is not reported to the police within seven days. [policy, cl. 7.5] The police report is dated 12 September 2026, two days after the notice of loss of 10 September 2026. [police report] [notice of loss] The date of the theft is not in the file, so I cannot confirm the seven-day period from the file alone. Nothing in the file indicates it was exceeded. This point does not affect the outcome, because clause 7.1 applies independently.

## Decision

**Not covered.** The theft falls within the exclusion for unattended portable equipment in a shared space, which was not in a locked container or locked room. [policy, cl. 7.1] The exclusion removes the loss from cover under clause 4.2. [policy, cl. 4.2] [police report]

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less any excess shown in the schedule. [policy, cl. 9.1]

- Invoice 1, camera body: 3,307 dollars. [invoice 1]
- Invoice 2, lens: 2,201 dollars. [invoice 2]
- Sum of invoices: 5,508 dollars.
- Excess: the schedule is not in the file, so no excess is shown and none is applied. [policy, cl. 9.1]

**Reserve: 5,508 dollars.** This figure is calculated under clause 9.1. Given the decision above, no payment is proposed. The reserve should be revisited once the schedule is obtained and the locked-room point is confirmed.
