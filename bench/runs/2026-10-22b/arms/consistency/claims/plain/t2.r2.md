# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Yewtree Biotech AS
**Policy:** CC-1444
**Incident reference:** IR/2026/4156

## Summary

Yewtree Biotech AS reported on 15 March 2026 that a burst pipe at a client site in Kilburn damaged two monitors and a desktop workstation [notice of loss]. No third party was involved [incident report]. The loss is accepted as covered under the escape of water cover in clause 4.7, read with clause 4.2. Two points need confirming from documents not in the file: the policy schedule and the insured's custody of the site. The reserve is 6,986 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured is Yewtree Biotech AS, under policy CC-1444 [notice of loss].
- The loss was reported on 15 March 2026 [notice of loss].
- The loss is described as water damage from a burst pipe affecting two monitors and a desktop workstation [notice of loss].
- The location is a client site in Kilburn [notice of loss], [incident report].
- An incident report, reference IR/2026/4156, is dated 17 March 2026 and was made by Yewtree Biotech AS [incident report].
- The incident report lists the items as two monitors and a desktop workstation. It records that no third party was involved [incident report].
- The two monitors were supplied by Beacon Office Supply, at 4,244 dollars [invoice 1].
- The desktop workstation was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics, at 2,742 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file contains no police report, and no theft is alleged [notice of loss], [incident report].
- The file does not contain the policy schedule [policy].
- The file does not state the date of the burst pipe itself, or whether the equipment was in the insured's custody at the site. It records only the report date of 15 March 2026 and the incident report date of 17 March 2026 [notice of loss], [incident report].

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clauses.**
- Clause 4.7 covers "damage to contents caused by water escaping from any fixed pipe, tank or appliance" [policy, cl. 4.7]. A burst pipe is water escaping from a fixed pipe, and the damaged items are business equipment [notice of loss], [incident report]. The loss falls within clause 4.7.
- Clause 4.2 covers business contents at the scheduled premises, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, where caused by escape of water among other perils [policy, cl. 4.2]. The loss occurred at a client site, not a named premises [notice of loss]. Cover under clause 4.2 therefore depends on the equipment having been in the insured's custody at that site. The file does not record this, so it should be confirmed with the insured.
- Clause 4.5 (transit) is not engaged. The file does not describe the items as being carried between premises [policy, cl. 4.5], [incident report].
- Clause 4.9 (electrical surge) is not engaged. The cause reported is escaped water, not a surge in the electrical supply [policy, cl. 4.9], [notice of loss].

**Exclusions.**
- **Clause 7.1 (unattended property)** applies only to theft of portable equipment [policy, cl. 7.1]. No theft is alleged [notice of loss], [incident report]. It does not apply.
- **Clause 7.3 (wear and tear)** excludes gradual deterioration [policy, cl. 7.3]. The loss is reported as a burst pipe, and nothing in the file indicates gradual deterioration as the cause [notice of loss], [incident report]. It does not apply on the file as it stands.
- **Clause 7.5 (unproven loss)** applies only where theft is alleged and not reported to the police within seven days [policy, cl. 7.5]. No theft is alleged, so no police report is required. It does not apply [notice of loss], [incident report].

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.7 (escape of water), read with clause 4.2. This is subject to confirming that the equipment was in the insured's custody at the Kilburn site and to checking the policy schedule. No exclusion in clause 7 applies.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less the 250 dollar excess where the schedule applies one. Where the schedule shows no excess, none applies [policy, cl. 9.1].
