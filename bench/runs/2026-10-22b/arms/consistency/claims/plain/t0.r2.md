# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Vellum Press Inc
**Policy:** CC-1370

## Summary

Vellum Press Inc claims under policy CC-1370 for the theft of a laptop and a docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. The loss was reported to us on 5 June 2026 and to the police on 7 June 2026. The claim falls within clause 4.2 (Contents), and no exclusion in clause 7 removes it. I recommend accepting the claim, subject to the two confirmations listed under Coverage analysis. The reserve is 4,030 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured is Vellum Press Inc, under policy CC-1370. [notice of loss]
- The loss was reported to us on 5 June 2026. [notice of loss]
- The loss is theft of a laptop and a docking station from a locked locker at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. [notice of loss]
- The theft was reported to the police on 7 June 2026 under reference CR/2026/4130, with Vellum Press Inc as the reporting party. [police report]
- The police report lists the items as a laptop and a docking station. [police report]
- The police report records that the items were in a locked container at the time of the theft, at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. [police report]

- The date of the theft is not stated in the file. [notice of loss] [police report]
- The policy schedule is not in the file. [policy]

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clause.** Clause 4.2 covers loss of business contents caused by theft, at the premises named in the schedule and at any other premises while in the insured's custody [policy, cl. 4.2]. The laptop and docking station are business items, and the cause of loss is theft [notice of loss] [police report]. The schedule is not in the file, so I cannot confirm whether the Fenwick Street co-working space is a named premises. If it is not, cover depends on the items having been in the insured's custody there. The file records that the items were in a locked locker at that location [police report], but it does not say who held the locker or its key. This is the first confirmation required.

**Other cover clauses.** Clause 4.5 (Transit) applies to equipment being carried by an employee between premises. Clause 4.7 (Escape of water) and clause 4.9 (Electrical surge) apply to water and surge damage. The file describes theft from a locker, not any of these events, so none of them is relied on [policy, cl. 4.5, 4.7, 4.9] [notice of loss].

**Exclusions.**

- **Clause 7.1 (Unattended property)** excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless it was in a locked container or locked room at the time [policy, cl. 7.1]. A co-working space is a shared space, but the police report records that the items were in a locked container [police report]. The exception applies, so the exclusion does not operate.
- **Clause 7.3 (Wear and tear)** excludes gradual deterioration [policy, cl. 7.3]. The loss is theft, and the file records no deterioration [notice of loss] [police report]. The exclusion does not apply.
- **Clause 7.5 (Unproven loss)** excludes a theft not reported to the police within seven days [policy, cl. 7.5]. The police report is dated 7 June 2026 [police report]. The notice of loss gives 5 June 2026 as the date reported to us, so the theft occurred on or before that date [notice of loss]. The file does not give the date of the theft, so I cannot confirm that the police report fell within seven days of it. The report would be in time if the theft occurred on or after 31 May 2026. This is the second confirmation required.

**Confirmations required.**
1. The policy schedule, to confirm the premises named and any excess [policy, cl. 4.2, 9.1].
2. The date of the theft, to close out clause 7.5 [policy, cl. 7.5].

## Decision

**Covered**, under clause 4.2 (Contents) [policy, cl. 4.2]. Exclusion 7.1 does not apply because the items were in a locked container [policy, cl. 7.1] [police report]. Exclusion 7.3 does not apply [policy, cl. 7.3]. Exclusion 7.5 is not engaged on the file as it stands, but the date of theft should be confirmed [policy, cl. 7.5]. The decision stands subject to the two confirmations above.
