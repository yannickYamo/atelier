# Claims Assessment Memo

## Summary

Bramble Hill Dental (policy CC-1555) reported the theft of a projector and a sound bar from an unattended bag at the insured's van, which was parked overnight. The police report states the items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container. The claim is **not covered** because exclusion 7.1 (unattended property) applies. Invoices in the file total 5,120 dollars, and the reserve is set on that figure under clause 9.1.

## Facts

- The insured is Bramble Hill Dental, under policy CC-1555 [notice of loss].
- The loss was reported to us on 6 September 2026 [notice of loss].
- The loss is the theft of a projector and a sound bar from an unattended bag in the insured's van, parked overnight [notice of loss].
- The theft was reported to the police on 8 September 2026 under reference CR/2026/4195, by Bramble Hill Dental [police report].
- The police report records the circumstances as theft from an unattended bag at the insured's van, parked overnight. It states the items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container [police report].
- The projector was supplied by Beacon Office Supply at 2,955 dollars [invoice 1].
- The sound bar was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics at 2,165 dollars [invoice 2].
- The file does not contain the policy schedule, so no named premises and no excess are shown. The file also does not state the date of the theft itself.

## Coverage analysis

**Insuring clauses**
- Clause 4.2 covers theft of business contents at the scheduled premises, and at other premises while in the insured's custody [policy cl. 4.2]. The schedule is not in the file, and the file does not establish whether a parked van is a premises within this clause. This point does not change the outcome because of exclusion 7.1 below.
- Clause 4.5 covers accidental loss of portable equipment while being carried by an employee between premises [policy cl. 4.5]. The file describes items left in an unattended bag overnight, not equipment being carried by an employee, so this clause does not respond [notice of loss] [police report].
- Clauses 4.7 (escape of water) and 4.9 (electrical surge) concern water damage and surge damage [policy cl. 4.7, 4.9]. The loss is a theft, so neither applies [notice of loss].

**Exclusions**
- **Clause 7.1, unattended property: applies.** It excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space, unless the equipment was in a locked container or locked room [policy cl. 7.1]. The projector and sound bar were portable equipment left in an unattended bag in a shared area, and were not in a locked container [notice of loss] [police report]. The file shows no locked room.
- **Clause 7.3, wear and tear: does not apply.** The loss is a theft, not gradual deterioration [policy cl. 7.3] [notice of loss].
- **Clause 7.5, unproven loss: not relied on.** It excludes theft not reported to the police within seven days [policy cl. 7.5]. The police report is dated 8 September 2026, two days after the notice of loss of 6 September 2026 [police report] [notice of loss]. The date of the theft is not stated in the file, so the seven-day period cannot be measured precisely. The date should be confirmed for the record, but the decision does not depend on it.

## Decision

**Not covered**, under exclusion 7.1 (unattended property) [policy cl. 7.1]. The items were portable equipment left unattended in a shared area and were not in a locked container or locked room [police report].

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1 the reserve is the sum of the invoices, less the 250 dollar excess where the policy schedule applies one. Where the schedule shows no excess, none applies [policy cl. 9.1].

- Invoice 1: 2,955 dollars [invoice 1]
- Invoice 2: 2,165 dollars [invoice 2]
- Sum of invoices: 5,120 dollars
- Excess: none deducted, because the file contains no schedule showing one [policy cl. 9.1]

**Reserve: 5,120 dollars.** This is the figure produced by clause 9.1 on the current file. It sits alongside the not-covered decision above and should be revisited if that decision changes or if the schedule shows an excess.
