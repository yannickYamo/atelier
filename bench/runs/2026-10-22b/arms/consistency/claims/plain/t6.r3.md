# Claims Assessment Memo

**Insured:** Cinder Lane Hardware Inc | **Policy:** CC-1592 | **Incident ref:** IR/2026/4208

## Summary

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc reported on 11 March 2026 that a laptop and a phone were damaged by a power surge after a storm at a conference venue in Bristol. The file does not establish that the equipment was connected through a surge protector, which clause 4.9 requires. Coverage cannot be confirmed on the present file, so the claim is not accepted at this stage. A reserve of 6,598 dollars is set.

## Facts

- The insured reported the loss on 11 March 2026 under policy CC-1592. [notice of loss]
- The reported loss is a power surge after a storm affecting a laptop and a phone at a conference venue in Bristol. [notice of loss]
- The incident report is dated 13 March 2026, reference IR/2026/4208, and was made by the insured. It lists the items as a laptop and a phone. [incident report]
- The incident report gives the circumstances as a power surge after a storm at a conference venue in Bristol, with no third party involved. [incident report]
- The laptop was supplied by Beacon Office Supply, amount 3,892 dollars. [invoice 1]
- The phone was supplied by Crane & Sons Electronics, amount 2,706 dollars. [invoice 2]
- The file does not say whether the equipment was connected through a surge protector. [notice of loss] [incident report]
- The file contains no policy schedule, so the premises named and any excess are not shown. [policy]
- No theft, water escape or carriage between premises is reported. [notice of loss] [incident report]

## Coverage analysis

**Clause 4.9 (Electrical surge).** This is the clause that deals directly with surge damage to electronic equipment. It covers such damage only where the equipment was connected through a surge protector. The file is silent on this, so the condition is not shown to be met. [policy cl. 4.9] [notice of loss] [incident report]

**Clause 4.2 (Contents).** This clause covers damage at other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by storm among other perils. The file records a surge "after" a storm, which does not itself state that the storm caused the damage. The file also does not state that the equipment was in the insured's custody at the venue. Because clause 4.9 deals specifically with surge damage and attaches a condition, I do not consider clause 4.2 an alternative route around that condition without further information. [policy cl. 4.2] [notice of loss] [incident report]

**Clause 4.5 (Transit).** Nothing in the file shows the equipment was being carried by an employee between premises when damaged. The clause is not engaged. [policy cl. 4.5] [incident report]

**Clause 4.7 (Escape of water).** No escape of water from a pipe, tank or appliance is reported. The clause is not engaged. [policy cl. 4.7] [incident report]

**Exclusions**
- **7.1 Unattended property** applies only to theft of portable equipment. No theft is reported, so it does not apply. [policy cl. 7.1] [incident report]
- **7.3 Wear and tear** applies to gradual deterioration. The loss is reported as a single surge event, and no deterioration is reported, so it does not apply. [policy cl. 7.3] [incident report]
- **7.5 Unproven loss** applies where theft is alleged. No theft is alleged, so it does not apply. [policy cl. 7.5] [notice of loss] [incident report]

## Decision

**Not covered on the present file.** The only clause that addresses the reported cause of loss is 4.9, and its surge-protector condition is not shown to be met. [policy cl. 4.9] No exclusion applies. If the insured provides evidence that both items were connected through a surge protector, the claim should be reassessed under clause 4.9. Evidence of custody and of storm causation would be needed for any reassessment under clause 4.2.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices less any excess the schedule applies. [policy cl. 9.1]

- Invoice 1: 3,892 dollars [invoice 1]
- Invoice 2: 2,706 dollars [invoice 2]
- Total: 6,598 dollars
- Excess: none. The schedule is not in the file and shows no excess, and clause 9.1 provides that where none is shown, none applies. [policy cl. 9.1]

**Reserve: 6,598 dollars.** This should be confirmed against the schedule when it is obtained.
