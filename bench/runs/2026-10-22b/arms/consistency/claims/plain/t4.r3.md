# Claims Assessment Memo

## Summary

Alder Mews Architects (policy CC-1518) reported on 25 June 2026 the accidental drop of a tablet and a drawing display during transport at a hotel meeting room in Leeds [notice of loss]. The loss falls within the transit cover at clause 4.5, subject to confirming two points the file does not state expressly (see Coverage analysis). No exclusion applies. The recommended reserve is 3,642 dollars.

## Facts

- The insured is Alder Mews Architects, under policy CC-1518 [notice of loss].
- The loss was reported on 25 June 2026 [notice of loss].
- The loss is an accidental drop during transport of a tablet and a drawing display [notice of loss].
- The location is a hotel meeting room in Leeds [notice of loss], [incident report].
- The incident report, reference IR/2026/4182, is dated 27 June 2026 and was made by Alder Mews Architects [incident report].

- The file contains no policy schedule, so no excess is shown.

## Coverage analysis

**Clause 4.5 (Transit).** This clause covers "accidental loss of or damage to portable equipment while being carried by an employee between premises" [policy, cl. 4.5]. A tablet and a drawing display are portable equipment, and the loss was an accidental drop during transport [notice of loss], [incident report]. The file does not state who was carrying the items or between which premises they were being moved. Both points should be confirmed with the insured before payment. On the file as it stands, the loss is of the kind clause 4.5 covers.

**Other insuring clauses.**
- Clause 4.2 (Contents) covers loss caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water [policy, cl. 4.2]. The file records an accidental drop and none of those perils, so this clause does not apply [incident report].
- Clause 4.7 (Escape of water) does not apply because no water damage is reported [policy, cl. 4.7], [incident report].
- Clause 4.9 (Electrical surge) does not apply because no surge is reported [policy, cl. 4.9], [incident report].

**Exclusions.**
- Clause 7.1 (Unattended property) excludes theft of portable equipment left unattended in a shared or public space [policy, cl. 7.1]. No theft is alleged, so it does not apply [notice of loss], [incident report].
- Clause 7.3 (Wear and tear) excludes gradual deterioration [policy, cl. 7.3]. The loss is reported as a single accidental drop, not gradual deterioration, so it does not apply [incident report].
- Clause 7.5 (Unproven loss) excludes a loss not reported to the police within seven days where theft is alleged [policy, cl. 7.5]. No theft is alleged, so it does not apply [notice of loss], [incident report].

## Decision

Covered, under clause 4.5 (Transit) [policy, cl. 4.5]. This is subject to confirmation that the items were being carried by an employee between premises. No exclusion in clauses 7.1, 7.3 or 7.5 applies.

## Reserve

Under clause 9.1, the reserve is the sum of the invoices in the file, less the 250 dollar excess where the policy schedule applies one. Where no excess is shown in the schedule, none applies [policy, cl. 9.1]. The file shows no excess, so none is deducted.
