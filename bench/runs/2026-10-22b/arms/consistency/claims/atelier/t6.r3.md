## Summary

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc: power surge after a storm of a laptop and a phone at a conference venue in Bristol. Declined under clause 4.9; no reserve is set.

## Facts

Cinder Lane Hardware Inc reported the loss on 11 March 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a conference venue in Bristol [notice of loss]. The items are a laptop and a phone [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The incident was recorded on 13 March 2026 [incident report]. The circumstances are a power surge after a storm, with no third party involved [incident report]. The file does not show that the equipment was connected through a surge protector [notice of loss], [incident report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.9 of the policy, which covers damage to electronic equipment caused by a surge in the electrical supply, where the equipment was connected through a surge protector [policy]. The condition of connection through a surge protector is not shown in the file, so the clause is not met [notice of loss], [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: no theft is alleged [policy]. Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: no gradual deterioration is reported [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: no theft is alleged [policy].

## Decision

Not covered: the condition of clause 4.9 is not met. The claim is declined, and the insured is told why in the terms of the policy.

## Reserve

No reserve is set: the claim is declined. The invoices in the file are 3,892 dollars and 2,706 dollars [invoice 1], [invoice 2].
