## Summary

Bramble Hill Dental: theft from an unattended bag of a projector and a sound bar at the insured's van, parked overnight. Declined under exclusion 7.1.

## Facts

Bramble Hill Dental reported the loss on 6 September 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at the insured's van, parked overnight [notice of loss]. The items are a projector and a sound bar [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 8 September 2026 [police report]. The items were left in a shared area and were not in a locked container at the time [police report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 applies: the items were unattended in a shared space and not in a locked container [police report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: the loss was reported to the police within seven days [police report], [policy].

## Decision

Not covered: exclusion 7.1 applies. The claim is declined, and the insured is told why in the terms of the policy.

## Reserve

No reserve is set: the claim is declined. The invoices in the file (2,955 dollars and 2,165 dollars, 5,120 dollars in total) are not reserved [invoice 1], [invoice 2].
