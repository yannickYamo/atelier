## Summary

Elmhurst Clinics LLP: fire damage in a neighbouring unit of a label printer and a barcode scanner at a storage unit on Mill Road. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 3,254 dollars.

## Facts

Elmhurst Clinics LLP reported the loss on 21 June 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a storage unit on Mill Road [notice of loss]. The items are a label printer and a barcode scanner [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The incident was recorded on 23 June 2026 [incident report]. No third party was involved [incident report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: no theft is alleged [policy]. Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the loss is fire damage in a neighbouring unit and not gradual deterioration [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: no theft is alleged [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 3,254 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (1,666 dollars and 1,588 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]; no excess is shown in the schedule, so none applies under clause 9.1 [policy].
