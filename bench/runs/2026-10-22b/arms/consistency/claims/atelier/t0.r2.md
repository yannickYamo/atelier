## Summary

Vellum Press Inc: theft from a locked locker of laptop and docking station at a co-working space on Fenwick Street. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 4,030 dollars.

## Facts

Vellum Press Inc reported the loss on 5 June 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a co-working space on Fenwick Street [notice of loss]. The items are laptop and docking station [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 7 June 2026 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container [police report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: the loss was reported to the police within seven days [police report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: no gradual deterioration is alleged [notice of loss], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 4,030 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (2,370 dollars and 1,660 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]; no excess is shown in the schedule, so none applies under clause 9.1 [policy].
