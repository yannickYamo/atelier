## Summary

Alder Mews Architects: accidental drop during transport of a tablet and a drawing display at a hotel meeting room in Leeds. Covered under clause 4.5; reserve 3,642 dollars.

## Facts

Alder Mews Architects reported the loss on 25 June 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a hotel meeting room in Leeds [notice of loss]. The items are a tablet and a drawing display [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The incident was recorded on 27 June 2026 [incident report]. The circumstances are an accidental drop during transport, with no third party involved [incident report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.5 of the policy, which covers accidental loss of or damage to portable equipment while being carried by an employee between premises [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.3 does not apply: the damage arose from an accidental drop and not from gradual deterioration [incident report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: no theft is alleged [incident report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.5. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 3,642 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (2,018 dollars and 1,624 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]; no excess is shown in the schedule, so none applies [policy].
