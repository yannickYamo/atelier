## Summary

Dovecote Learning Ltd: theft from a locked office overnight of a soldering station and an oscilloscope at a rented studio on Pike Street. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 5,876 dollars.

## Facts

Dovecote Learning Ltd reported the loss on 16 April 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a rented studio on Pike Street [notice of loss]. The items are a soldering station and an oscilloscope [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 18 April 2026 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers loss of or damage to business contents at the premises named in the schedule, and at any other premises while in the insured's custody, caused by theft, fire, storm or escape of water [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container [police report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: the loss was reported to the police within seven days [police report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 5,876 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (4,829 dollars and 1,047 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]; no excess is shown in the schedule, so none applies under clause 9.1 [policy].
