## Summary

Saltmarsh Games Oy: theft from a locked office overnight of a soldering station and an oscilloscope at a rented studio on Pike Street. Covered under clause 4.2; reserve 5,896 dollars.

## Facts

Saltmarsh Games Oy reported the loss on 14 September 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a rented studio on Pike Street [notice of loss]. The items are a soldering station and an oscilloscope [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The theft was reported to the police on 16 September 2026 [police report]. The items were in a locked container at the time [police report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.2 of the policy, which covers theft of or damage to business contents in the insured's custody [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: the items were in a locked container [police report], [policy]. Exclusion 7.5 does not apply: the loss was reported to the police within seven days [police report], [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.2. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 5,896 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (3,659 dollars and 2,237 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]; the schedule applies no excess [policy].
