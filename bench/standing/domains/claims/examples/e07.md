## Summary

Rushmere Logistics Inc: power surge after a storm of a laptop and a phone at a conference venue in Bristol. Covered under clause 4.9; reserve 4,418 dollars.

## Facts

Rushmere Logistics Inc reported the loss on 9 June 2026 [notice of loss]. The loss occurred at a conference venue in Bristol [notice of loss]. The items are a laptop and a phone [invoice 1], [invoice 2]. The incident was recorded on 11 June 2026 [incident report].

## Coverage analysis

The loss falls under clause 4.9 of the policy, which covers damage from an electrical surge through a protector [policy]. Exclusion 7.1 does not apply: no theft is alleged [policy].

## Decision

Covered under clause 4.9. The claim is accepted.

## Reserve

The reserve is 4,418 dollars, the sum of the two invoices (2,722 dollars and 1,696 dollars) [invoice 1], [invoice 2]; the schedule applies no excess [policy].
