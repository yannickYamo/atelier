How we draft a mutual confidentiality agreement

1. Open with a Parties section that names both parties exactly as the term sheet does.
2. Then a Confidential Information section that defines the term.
3. Then an Obligations section.
4. Then a Term section that gives the term in months, as the term sheet does.
5. End with a Governing Law section that names the law the term sheet names.
6. Never use the phrase "best efforts".
7. Between 220 and 420 words.
8. Take every name, date and figure from the term sheet: add none of your own.
