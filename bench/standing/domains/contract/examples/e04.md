## Parties

This Mutual Confidentiality Agreement is entered into by and between Osprey Grid Systems Inc (the "First Party") and Gantry & Gale Ltd (the "Second Party"), each referred to individually as a "Party" and together as the "Parties". This Agreement takes effect on 22 November 2026 (the "Effective Date") and is entered into in connection with a manufacturing trial (the "Purpose").

## Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party") in connection with the Purpose, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically, visually or in any other form, and whether or not marked or identified as confidential. Confidential Information includes, without limitation, technical data, designs, drawings, specifications, processes, manufacturing methods, samples, test results, software, pricing, commercial terms, customer and supplier information, and business plans. Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party; (b) was lawfully in the Receiving Party's possession without obligation of confidence before disclosure by the Disclosing Party; (c) is lawfully received from a third party free to disclose it; or (d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Confidential Information.

## Obligations

Each Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose. The Receiving Party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party, except that disclosure may be made to its employees, officers, directors, professional advisers and subcontractors who need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement. The Receiving Party shall protect the Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it applies to its own confidential information of like importance, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care. The Receiving Party shall remain responsible for any breach by those to whom it discloses Confidential Information. Where disclosure is required by law, regulation or court order, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted, notify the Disclosing Party in advance so that protective measures may be sought. Upon written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy the Confidential Information in its possession.

## Term

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for a term of 36 months.

## Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the law of the Netherlands.
