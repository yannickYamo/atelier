## Parties

This Mutual Confidentiality Agreement is entered into by and between Harbour Lane Robotics GmbH and Driftwood Semiconductors Inc, each a "Party" and together the "Parties". This Agreement takes effect on 8 June 2026 (the "Effective Date") and is entered into in connection with the Parties' discussions and dealings concerning a licence of a data set (the "Purpose").

## Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by or on behalf of one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether before or after the Effective Date, in writing, orally, visually, electronically or in any other form, that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes technical, commercial, financial and operational information, data sets and samples thereof, specifications, designs, source code, business plans, pricing, customer and supplier information, and the existence and contents of the Parties' discussions concerning the Purpose. Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party; (b) was lawfully in the Receiving Party's possession without an obligation of confidence before disclosure by the Disclosing Party; (c) is lawfully received from a third party free of any obligation of confidence; or (d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Confidential Information.

## Obligations

Each Receiving Party shall: (a) use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (b) keep the Confidential Information confidential and protect it using at least the degree of care it applies to its own confidential information of like importance, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care; (c) disclose the Confidential Information only to its employees, officers, directors, affiliates and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out here; and (d) not copy, reverse engineer or otherwise exploit the Confidential Information except as required for the Purpose. The Receiving Party remains responsible for any breach by persons to whom it discloses Confidential Information. Disclosure compelled by law, regulation or court order is permitted, provided the Receiving Party gives prompt notice to the Disclosing Party where lawful and discloses only what is required.

## Term

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for a term of 48 months.

## Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the law of the State of Delaware.
