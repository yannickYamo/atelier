## Parties

This Mutual Confidentiality Agreement is entered into as of 3 March 2026 by and between Larkspur Freight Co and Orchard Quay Retail Ltd. Each of Larkspur Freight Co and Orchard Quay Retail Ltd may act as a disclosing party and as a receiving party under this Agreement. The parties are entering into this Agreement in connection with a proposed acquisition of a product line.

## Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by or on behalf of one party to the other party, whether before or after the effective date of 3 March 2026, in writing, orally, electronically, visually or by any other means, that relates to the proposed acquisition of a product line or to the disclosing party's business, operations, products, customers, suppliers, pricing, finances, personnel, technology or plans, and that is identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes the existence and contents of this Agreement and the fact that discussions between Larkspur Freight Co and Orchard Quay Retail Ltd are taking place. Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Agreement, that the receiving party already lawfully possessed without a duty of confidence, that is independently developed without reference to the disclosing party's Confidential Information, or that is lawfully received from a third party free of any confidentiality obligation.

## Obligations

The receiving party shall use the disclosing party's Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating, negotiating and implementing the proposed acquisition of a product line. The receiving party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any person other than its directors, officers, employees, affiliates and professional advisers who need to know it for that purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement, and the receiving party remains responsible for any breach by such persons. The receiving party shall protect Confidential Information using no less than a reasonable degree of care. If the receiving party is required by law, regulation or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it shall, where lawfully permitted, give the disclosing party prompt written notice. Upon written request, the receiving party shall return or destroy the Confidential Information in its possession.

## Term

This Agreement commences on 3 March 2026 and continues for a term of 15 months.

## Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the law of Singapore.
