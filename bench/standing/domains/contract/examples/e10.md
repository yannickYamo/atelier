## Parties

This Mutual Confidentiality Agreement is made as of 10 June 2026 between Saltmarsh Bio Ltd and Pallister Marine Ltd. Each party may disclose information to the other in connection with a reseller arrangement. In this Agreement, the party disclosing information is the "Disclosing Party" and the party receiving it is the "Receiving Party", and each party may act in either capacity.

## Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one party to the other in connection with the reseller arrangement, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically or by inspection of tangible objects, and whether or not marked or identified as confidential, that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure. It includes, without limitation, technical data, product and formulation information, research results, business plans, pricing, customer and supplier lists, forecasts, and the existence and contents of discussions between the parties. Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party without obligation of confidence before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

## Obligations

Each Receiving Party shall hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party except as permitted here. The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating, negotiating and performing the reseller arrangement. The Receiving Party shall apply at least the same degree of care it applies to its own confidential information of like importance, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care. Disclosure is permitted to the Receiving Party's directors, employees, advisers and contractors who need to know the information for the stated purpose and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement; the Receiving Party remains responsible for their compliance. If disclosure is required by law, regulation or court order, the Receiving Party shall, where lawfully permitted, give prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party and disclose only the portion legally required. On written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy the Confidential Information, subject to retention required by law or routine backup systems.

## Term

This Agreement commences on 10 June 2026 and continues for 18 months.

## Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of New South Wales.
