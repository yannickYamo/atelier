## Parties

This Mutual Confidentiality Agreement is made on 24 November 2026 between Juniper Vale Capital LLP ("the First Party") and Sable Fen Analytics Inc ("the Second Party"), each of which may disclose Confidential Information to the other and each of which is referred to individually as a "Party" and together as "the Parties". The Parties are entering into this Agreement for the purpose of evaluating and progressing a possible supply agreement ("the Purpose").

## Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by or on behalf of one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether before or after the date of this Agreement, in writing, orally, electronically, visually or in any other form, and whether or not marked or identified as confidential, which relates to the Disclosing Party's business, finances, customers, suppliers, pricing, products, services, operations, technology, data, methods, plans or affairs, together with the fact and content of the discussions between the Parties concerning the Purpose. Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public other than through a breach of this Agreement, that the Receiving Party already lawfully held free of any duty of confidence, that the Receiving Party independently develops without reference to the Disclosing Party's information, or that is lawfully received from a third party with no obligation of confidence.

## Obligations

Each Receiving Party shall keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information strictly confidential and shall use it solely for the Purpose. The Receiving Party shall apply at least the same degree of care it applies to its own confidential information of like importance, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care. The Receiving Party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent, save that it may disclose to those of its officers, employees and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by obligations of confidence no less protective than these. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation or a competent authority, giving the Disclosing Party prompt written notice where lawful. On written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy the Confidential Information in its possession.

## Term

This Agreement takes effect on 24 November 2026 and continues for a term of 12 months.

## Governing Law

This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the law of England and Wales.
