## Parties

This Mutual Confidentiality Agreement is entered into by Meridian Kiln Works SA ("First Party") and Kestrel Loop Payments Inc ("Second Party"), each a "Party" and together the "Parties". This Agreement takes effect on 9 June 2026 (the "Effective Date") and is entered into for the purpose of a shared research project (the "Purpose").

## Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one Party (the "Discloser") to the other Party (the "Recipient") in connection with the Purpose, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically or by any other means, and whether or not marked or identified as confidential, including technical data, research results, methods, samples, designs, specifications, software, financial information, commercial plans, customer and supplier information, and the existence and contents of this Agreement.

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement by the Recipient; (b) was lawfully in the Recipient's possession, without restriction, before disclosure by the Discloser; (c) is lawfully received from a third party who is under no obligation of confidence in respect of it; or (d) is independently developed by the Recipient without reference to the Confidential Information.

## Obligations

Each Party, when acting as Recipient, shall:

1. use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose;
2. keep the Confidential Information secret and not disclose it to any third party without the Discloser's prior written consent;
3. disclose the Confidential Information only to those of its directors, employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose, and ensure that each such person is bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out in this Agreement;
4. apply at least the same degree of care to the Confidential Information as it applies to its own confidential information of similar importance, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care;
5. not copy or reproduce the Confidential Information except as reasonably required for the Purpose; and
6. on written request by the Discloser, return or destroy the Confidential Information and any copies, save for one archival copy retained for legal or regulatory compliance.

A Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, court order or regulatory authority, provided it gives the Discloser prompt written notice where lawful to do so.

## Term

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for 30 months.

## Governing Law

This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the law of Scotland.
