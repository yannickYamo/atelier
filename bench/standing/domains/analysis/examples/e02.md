## Verdict

Inkwell costs least for a team of 12, at $96 per month. At 8 dollars per seat with a 1-seat minimum, the team pays for exactly the 12 seats it uses, with no minimum-seat padding. Borealis is the next option at $144 per month; Dorado is far more expensive at $760 per month because its 40-seat minimum forces payment for 28 seats the team will not use.

## Vendor pricing

| Vendor | Price per seat | Minimum seats |
|---|---|---|
| Dorado | $19 | 40 |
| Inkwell | $8 | 1 |
| Borealis | $12 | 10 |

## Cost at this size

Seats paid for is the larger of the team size (12) and the vendor minimum.

- **Dorado:** minimum 40 exceeds the team of 12, so 40 seats are paid. 40 × $19 = **$760 per month**.
- **Inkwell:** minimum 1 is below the team of 12, so 12 seats are paid. 12 × $8 = **$96 per month**.
- **Borealis:** minimum 10 is below the team of 12, so 12 seats are paid. 12 × $12 = **$144 per month**.

Ranked: Inkwell $96, Borealis $144, Dorado $760. Inkwell is $48 per month cheaper than Borealis and $664 per month cheaper than Dorado at this size.

## Risks

The price list covers per-seat rates and seat minimums only. It carries no information on feature sets, support tiers, usage caps, or contract terms, so this comparison is a price comparison and nothing more; the cheapest vendor may not meet functional requirements.

All figures assume monthly billing at list price, as stated. Annual commitments or negotiated discounts are not covered and could change the ranking.

Growth changes the picture. Borealis and Inkwell both scale linearly from 12 seats, so Inkwell stays cheaper as the team grows. Dorado only reaches its effective per-seat rate at 40 seats; below that, cost per actual user is inflated ($63.33 per person at 12 people).
