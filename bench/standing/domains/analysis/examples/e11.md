## Verdict

**Gable** costs least for a team of 9, at **$72 per month**. Its $8 per-seat price is the lowest on the list, and its 5-seat minimum sits below the team size, so the team pays only for the seats it uses. Lumen Path and Estuary both carry minimums well above 9 seats, forcing the team to buy unused capacity and inflating effective cost far past their headline rates.

## Vendor Pricing

| Vendor | Price per seat | Minimum seats |
|---|---|---|
| Gable | $8 | 5 |
| Lumen Path | $12 | 20 |
| Estuary | $17 | 40 |

## Cost at this size

Seats paid for = the larger of 9 or the vendor minimum.

- **Gable:** 9 seats (9 > 5) × $8 = **$72 per month**
- **Lumen Path:** 20 seats (20 > 9, minimum applies) × $12 = **$240 per month**
- **Estuary:** 40 seats (40 > 9, minimum applies) × $17 = **$680 per month**

Gable is $168 per month cheaper than Lumen Path and $608 per month cheaper than Estuary. Put differently, Lumen Path costs 3.3× Gable and Estuary costs 9.4× Gable at this headcount. Effective cost per actual user: Gable $8.00, Lumen Path $26.67 ($240 ÷ 9), Estuary $75.56 ($680 ÷ 9).

## Risks

The minimums dominate this comparison, and that advantage is size-dependent. Gable's lead narrows in relative terms as the team grows: at 20 seats Gable costs $160 against Lumen Path's $240, and Lumen Path's unused-seat penalty disappears entirely. Any plan to scale past 20 should re-run these figures.

This analysis covers price only. The price list carries no information on features, support tiers, usage limits, or contract terms, so a cheaper seat may buy less. Prices are billed monthly, so they may be subject to change at renewal, and annual commitments — not quoted here — could reorder the ranking.
