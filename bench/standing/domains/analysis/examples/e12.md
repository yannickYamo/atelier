## Verdict

Cygnus Flow costs least for a team of 6. At $8 per seat with a 5-seat minimum, the team pays for all 6 seats and nothing more: $48 per month. Both alternatives force the team to buy seats it will not use — Heronry bills for 20 seats, Juno Board for 50 — making them 5x and 19.8x more expensive respectively at this size.

## Vendors and Pricing

| Vendor | Price per seat | Minimum seats |
|---|---|---|
| Juno Board | $19 | 50 |
| Cygnus Flow | $8 | 5 |
| Heronry | $12 | 20 |

## Cost at this size

Seats paid for = the greater of 6 (team size) or the vendor minimum.

- **Cygnus Flow:** minimum 5 < team 6, so 6 seats paid. 6 × $8 = **$48 per month**.
- **Heronry:** minimum 20 > team 6, so 20 seats paid. 20 × $12 = **$240 per month**.
- **Juno Board:** minimum 50 > team 6, so 50 seats paid. 50 × $19 = **$950 per month**.

Cygnus Flow saves $192 per month against Heronry and $902 per month against Juno Board — $2,304 and $10,824 annually.

## Risks

The ranking is driven almost entirely by seat minimums, not by per-seat rates, so it is unstable as the team grows. Heronry's effective per-seat cost falls until headcount reaches 20; at 20 seats Cygnus Flow costs $160 against Heronry's $240, so Cygnus Flow stays cheaper on list price well beyond this team size.

The price list covers per-seat monthly rates only. It does not state what each tier includes, so a cheaper seat may carry fewer features, lower limits, or weaker support. Nothing here supports a conclusion about value, only about price.

Annual billing, discounts, and implementation or migration costs are not on the price list and are not reflected in these figures. Prices are stated as billed monthly and could change at renewal.
