## Verdict

Dorado costs least for a team of 35, at $420 per month. Its $12 per-seat rate is the lowest on the list, and its 20-seat minimum is below the team size, so no seats are wasted. Inkwell is the next cheapest at $680 despite a 40-seat minimum that forces payment for 5 unused seats; Borealis is the most expensive at $840.

## Vendor pricing

| Vendor | Price per seat | Minimum seats |
|---|---|---|
| Dorado | $12 | 20 |
| Inkwell | $17 | 40 |
| Borealis | $24 | 1 |

## Cost at this size

- **Dorado:** team of 35 exceeds the 20-seat minimum, so 35 seats are paid. 35 × $12 = **$420/month**.
- **Inkwell:** the 40-seat minimum exceeds the team of 35, so 40 seats are paid. 40 × $17 = **$680/month**.
- **Borealis:** the 1-seat minimum is below the team of 35, so 35 seats are paid. 35 × $24 = **$840/month**.

Dorado undercuts Inkwell by $260/month ($3,120/year) and Borealis by $420/month ($5,040/year).

## Risks

- **Minimum-seat exposure at Inkwell.** At 35 people you pay for 40 seats, an effective rate of $19.43 per actual user. That penalty only disappears if the team grows to 40; below 35 it worsens.
- **Growth sensitivity.** Dorado stays cheapest as the team grows, since it has the lowest per-seat rate and the team is already above its minimum. Shrinking below 20 people would make Borealis cheaper than Dorado, as Borealis has a 1-seat minimum (at 17 seats or fewer, Borealis costs less than Dorado's $240 floor).
- **Price list scope.** These figures cover monthly billing only. Annual terms, discounts, onboarding fees, and feature tiers are not on the price list, so the ranking holds only for the per-seat monthly rates given.
- **Non-price factors excluded.** This comparison is price-only; capability, support, and security differences are not assessed here.
