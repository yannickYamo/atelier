## Verdict

Estuary costs least for a team of 18, at $144 per month. It is the only vendor on this list whose minimum seat count (1) falls below the team size, so the team pays for exactly the seats it uses. Gable and Lumen Path both force payment for unused seats at this size, and Lumen Path's 40-seat floor more than doubles the billable headcount.

## Vendor pricing

| Vendor | Price per seat | Minimum seats |
|---|---|---|
| Gable | $14 | 20 |
| Lumen Path | $19 | 40 |
| Estuary | $8 | 1 |

## Cost at this size

Seats paid for is the team size (18) or the vendor minimum, whichever is larger.

- **Estuary:** minimum is 1, team is 18, so 18 seats paid. 18 × $8 = **$144 per month**.
- **Gable:** minimum is 20, which exceeds the team of 18, so 20 seats paid. 20 × $14 = **$280 per month**.
- **Lumen Path:** minimum is 40, which exceeds the team of 18, so 40 seats paid. 40 × $19 = **$760 per month**.

Estuary is $136 per month cheaper than Gable and $616 per month cheaper than Lumen Path. Gable's effective rate at 18 users is $15.56 per person ($280 ÷ 18); Lumen Path's is $42.22 per person ($760 ÷ 18), against Estuary's true $8.

## Risks

This comparison covers list price only. The price list carries no feature, support, or security detail, so a cheaper seat may buy less product — that gap is unmeasured here.

Minimum-seat penalties invert with growth. Gable overtakes Estuary on effective rate only below its 20-seat floor; past 20 seats Estuary stays cheaper per seat regardless. Lumen Path's $760 is fixed until 40 users, so a team scaling toward 40 would see its effective rate fall sharply.

All figures assume monthly billing as listed. Annual commitments, discounts, onboarding fees, and overage terms are not in the source and could change the ranking.
