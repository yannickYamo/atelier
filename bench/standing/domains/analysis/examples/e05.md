## Verdict

Fennel HQ costs least for a team of 45. At $9 per seat with a 5-seat minimum, all 45 seats are billed at list, giving $405 per month — less than half of Kelp's $630 and under a third of Acme Desk's $1,050.

## Vendors

| Vendor | Price per seat | Minimum seats |
|---|---|---|
| Acme Desk | $21 | 50 |
| Fennel HQ | $9 | 5 |
| Kelp | $14 | 20 |

## Cost at this size

Team size is 45. Seats paid for is the larger of 45 and the vendor minimum.

- **Acme Desk:** minimum 50 exceeds the team of 45, so 50 seats are billed. 50 × $21 = **$1,050 per month**. The team pays for 5 seats it does not use, an effective rate of $23.33 per person ($1,050 ÷ 45).
- **Fennel HQ:** minimum 5 is below 45, so 45 seats are billed. 45 × $9 = **$405 per month**. Effective rate $9.00 per person.
- **Kelp:** minimum 20 is below 45, so 45 seats are billed. 45 × $14 = **$630 per month**. Effective rate $14.00 per person.

Spread between cheapest and dearest: $1,050 − $405 = $645 per month, or $7,740 per year.

## Risks

Only one variable has been compared: list price per seat, billed monthly. The price list carries no information on features, support tiers, security posture, integrations or data limits, so this ranking cannot say which vendor is the better buy — only which is the cheaper one.

Two sensitivities are worth flagging. First, Acme Desk's penalty is purely a minimum-seat artefact; at 50 or more people its effective rate drops to $21 and the gap narrows. Second, growth changes nothing for Fennel HQ and Kelp below 45 seats but matters above it, since all three then scale linearly and Fennel HQ's lead widens in absolute terms.

Annual or committed-term pricing, discounts and onboarding fees are not in the price list and may alter these totals.
