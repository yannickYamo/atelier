- We wrote design docs for every project over 2 weeks of work. Average doc: 11 pages. Average readers who commented: 1.4.
- We capped docs at 2 pages plus an appendix nobody has to read. Commenters per doc rose to 4.1.
- Time from doc to first commit fell from 12 days to 5.
- Two projects were cancelled at the doc stage in six months; none had been in the year before.
- Some senior engineers dislike the cap and say nuance is lost. They may be right on the one distributed-locking project.
