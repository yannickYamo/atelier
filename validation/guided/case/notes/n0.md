- We ran an on-call rota of 6 engineers, one week each. Pages per week: 41 on average in Q1.
- 27 of the 41 were alerts nobody acted on (disk at 80%, a retry storm that self-heals).
- We deleted every alert that had not led to an action in 90 days: 58 of 93 alert rules removed.
- Pages per week after: 11. One real incident was caught 6 minutes later than the old rules would have (a queue backlog).
- We did not buy an alert-routing product. Two people wanted to.
